# Cook Verification Scans — Summary of Searches and Findings

**Date:** 26 November 2026
**Prepared for:** Alex H. Cook
**Scope:** Record searches requested for the Cook lineage project — the will and probate records of the testator George Cook of Pittsylvania County, Virginia; the George Cook × Catharine marriage; and original-document verification scans for key records in the proven line.
**Standard:** Genealogical Proof Standard. Free sources only. Newspapers.com sources excluded per project rule.

## 1. Where the Line Stands

**Proven line (unchanged by these searches):**
Alex Cook → Brian Cook → Arlin Cook → Russell Cook → George William Cook (1852–1935) → George Cook Sr. (~1813–1870s, m. Eliza Cole) → **George B Cook** of Pittsylvania County (died before 15 October 1831; widow Catharine).

Proof stops at George B. The next generation up — the testator George Cook (b. ~1740, d. 1835, wife Sarah) as George B's father, likely through the hypothesis that George B is the "George Cook Jr." whose wife was Ruth Pruett — remains a **hypothesis at 80/Probable**, not proven. The immigrant ancestor has not yet been reached.

## 2. Searches Requested and Their Results

### 2.1 The testator's will — Will Book 1, pp. 293–294, Pittsylvania County

- **Citation index-verified** in Ancestry Library collection 62347 ("Wills, 1800–1870," an index only): "COOK, George, Sen. 1835 W. B. 1 293."
- **Will text NOT RETRIEVED.** Collection 62347 carries no Will Book 1 volume for Pittsylvania (its only "Will Book" entry is Vol. 9–10, 1943–44). The "Wills, Inventories, and Accounts Current, Vol 1a–2a" film (007646031) was swept; pp. 293–294 there are 1847–1849 estate accounts, not the Cook will.
- The will is on **FamilySearch Film #007642302** (Will Book 1, 1814–1845) — images restricted, viewable only at a FamilySearch Center. **In-person pull is the only route.**

### 2.2 Mixed Probate Books 3 and 7, Pittsylvania County

- **NOT RETRIEVED.** Neither volume exists in Ancestry Library collection 62347's Pittsylvania browse list. Both are on the in-person pull list (same FamilySearch Center visit as the will).

### 2.3 George Cook × Catharine marriage, Pittsylvania County, 1810–1825

- **Ancestry Compiled Marriages (3723):** zero results, both spellings (Catharine/Catherine).
- **Ancestry Select Marriages (60214):** zero results, both spellings.
- **FamilySearch Full-Text Search of the original Pittsylvania Marriage Bonds (1767–1862):** no George Cook–Catharine bond. A George-Cook-only search of the bonds for 1810–1825 returns just 4 hits, and in all of them George Cook is only a **bondsman**, never the groom:
  - Bondsman for Thomas Sadler & Mary Nancy Clark — bond dated 12 April 1815.
  - Bondsman for Leonard Clarke & Ann Fryday — 22 December 1819.
- This adds to earlier negatives: published Pittsylvania sources, FamilySearch Virginia Marriages, Halifax County, and Caswell County, NC (all searched 1790–1825).
- **Conclusion:** No marriage record found in any digitized source. Catharine's identity remains unknown. The most valuable unchecked source is *Marriages of Pittsylvania County 1806–1830* (print only, not digitized). If George B is George Jr., Catharine would be a post-1815 wife (Ruth was still living in July 1814), and George W. Cook (b. ~1813) could be Ruth's son — which is why George W.'s mother is left blank in the trees.

### 2.4 The testator in other researchers' trees

- His Ancestry profile (292840786099) has **zero hints** in every category and an empty gallery.
- No other public Ancestry tree matches him (b. ~1740, d. 1835 Pittsylvania, wife Sarah). He is effectively unresearched outside this project.

## 5. Open Conflicts and Leads

- **Deed Book 39 citation conflict (unresolved):** the project's DB 39 page citations came from earlier FamilySearch readings; in Ancestry film 007646022 those pages hold other people's deeds. Either the two filmings paginate differently or a citation is wrong. Needs a targeted re-check before those page numbers are trusted.
- **1831 deed acreage (unresolved):** read as **227 acres** in the 2026-11-26 pass; project notes say **277 acres**. The full-resolution image (`Pittsylvania_DB33_p101.png`) is in hand for a zoom check.
- **New lead — 1830 George Cook estate:** Inventory & Appraisement, Accounts Current Book 7, p. 210, dated 19 May 1830. The page itself has not been pulled. Its identity is open: it predates George B's October 1831 heirs' deed, so it either belongs to him with an earlier death than the deed implies, or to another George Cook.
- **Catharine's own-name deeds** (1819, DB 22/207; 1824, DB 25/542 and DB 27, pp. 91–92) remain the next Pittsylvania reads; if she is George B's widow, he died before June 1819.
