# Archival Traps, Misindexing & Microfilm Offsets

This directory documents records that were initially retrieved under faulty citations or misindexed microfilm rolls, and provides the exact side-by-side analysis showing how the true records were recovered.

---

## 1. Adair County Deed Book Z: Microfilm Offset vs. Actual Deed
- **Faulty Initial Scans:** `Adair_DBZ_p194-195.png` and `Adair_DBZ_1877deed_start.png`.
- **Trap:** The FamilySearch/Ancestry film reel binding placed Deed Book Z mortgages for pages 252–255 at the position expected for pages 194–195 due to out-of-sequence register inserts.
- **Resolution:** Re-auditing the roll folio-by-folio located the genuine February 1877 heirship conveyance from George W. Cook to Thomas H. Cook on folio 195, preserved in `evidence/ky-adair/Adair_DBZ_p194-195_EXACT.png`.

## 2. Pittsylvania County Deed Book 39: Sheriff Sale vs. Coleman Deed
- **Faulty Initial Scan:** `Pittsylvania_DB39_p319-320.png`.
- **Trap:** A commercial multi-volume digital film skip shifted the page capture ten pages forward, retrieving pages 329–330 (a mortgage from Coleman to Keen) instead of pages 319–320.
- **Resolution:** The true deed was captured in `evidence/va-pittsylvania/Pittsylvania_DB39_p319-320_EXACT.png`, showing the 27 January 1837 conveyance by Sheriff Thomas H. Wooding *c.t.a.* of George Cook Senr decd selling 97 acres on Sandy Creek to John Williams for $107.67.
