## Transcription
Verbatim; line breaks not kept. Margin note p. 453: "Cook fm Martin Deed". Item "DB7A" (db7a_20260526) ends at about p. 234 and DB7B begins at about p. 235.

p. 453: "This Indenture made this fourth day of June in the year of our Lord one thousand seven hundred and eighty five Between Peter Martin of Halifax County and State of Virginia of the one part and Elijah Cook of Pittsylvania County and State aforesaid of the other part Witnesseth that the said Peter Martin for and in consideration of the sum of Ten pounds Virg[inia] currency to him in hand paid by the said Elijah Cook, the receipt whereof the said Martin doth hereby acknowledge hath bargained and sold, and by these presents doth bargain and sell unto the said Elijah Cook a tract or parcel of land in Pittsylvania County on Sandy Creek where Elijah Cook now lives containing by estimation fifty acres, be the same more or less, and bounded as followeth (Viz) Beginning at a white oak standing in Jeremiah Stemson's line by the side of the Saw pitt branch and runing along the same line to Rosamon Stimson Spring branch, thence dow[n] the said branch to John Watters line, thence along the said line to George Cooks (Senr) corner pine, thence along the said line to John Watters corner formerly called James Collies, thence along the said line to the first branch to a black Gum, thence up the said branch along a line of marked trees to the beginning and"

p. 454: "and the said Peter Martin doth for himself his heirs and assigns warrant and for ever defend the above Granted land and premises unto the said Elijah Cook his heirs and assigns for ever against the lawfull claim or claims of all and every person or persons whatsoever In Witness whereof the said Peter Martin hath hereunto set his hand and affixed his seal the day and year first above written [right:] Peter [his] X [mark] Martin L.S."
"Signed, sealed and delivered in presence of" Zachariah Butt, Uriah [his mark] Owen / George Cook Sener, John [mark] Everett [sic: "Everett" read as Everett or Everitt; medium]
Court: "At a Court held for Pittsylvania County the 20th day of June 1785 The within written Indenture was proved by the oaths of three of the subscribing witnesses to be the act and deed of the within named Peter Martin and the same was ordered to be recorded. Teste Will Tunstall C[lerk]" Margin: "Examin'd".

## Notes
- Informant: the clerk (copy); terms by the parties. The recording date (20 Jun 1785) matches the Grantee Index entry (Elijah, 20 Jun 1785, from Peter Martin, B & S, 7:453).
- Names to weigh: "Jeremiah Stemson" and "Rosamon Stimson" are the clerk's spellings (not read as Stinson); "Zachariah Butt" medium (could be Bull/Butt); "Uriah Owen" medium; "John Everett" medium. "George Cook Sener" (written "Sener"/"Senr", first letter long s) is the third witness; "George Cooks (Senr) corner pine" is a boundary call, so George Cook Sr. held adjoining land by 4 Jun 1785. The boundary also names "John Watters corner formerly called James Collies" (cf. William Collie in the FAN list).
- No relationship between Elijah and George Cook is stated. The deed gives no age or parentage.
- Same page 453 also holds Joseph Mottley to Daniel Mottley (18 Mar 1785), no Cook content. Page 454 continues with Spencer Clack to William Ryan (1785), no Cook content. Page 452 (Martha Clement to David Ross, 6 Dec 1780) has no Cook, Musick, Holloway or Flippin name.
