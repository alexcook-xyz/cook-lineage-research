(surveyor, Pittsylvania County); Clerk of the Circuit Court's office, Chatham; image 356 of 367, *Internet Archive* (https://archive.org/details/old-land-survey-book-1 : accessed 28 Nov 2026; page image n355); file "Old Land Survey Book 1.pdf".

## Transcription
Heading: "5 Sept.r 1781. Resurvey'd for Isaac & Adam Clements Ex.rs of Benj.n Clements Decea.d Agreeable to an Order of Court dated April Court 1781 ... 375 Acres of Land Lying on the S.o Side of Stanton River & on both sides Sycamore creek which said Land was Granted to the Said Benj.n Clements Decea.d By Letters Patent Bearing Date 20 June 1772, mistakes being found in the courses & Discriptions of the Said Land is the cause of Resurveying. It is Bounded as follows (to Wit) Beginning at a Spanish Oak on the S. River ..."
Extract of the calls naming Holloway: "...to a R. Oak N 1 E 60 po. to Hollowey's Co. Pine & thence on his Line N 10 W 76 po. to a hickory on Sycamore Co. and thence down the Creek as it Meanders to Ralph Smith's Co. on the S.d Creek..." [reading of "Hollowey" at about 150 dpi; "Hollowey's Cor. Pine"]. Signed "By Joshua Stone S.P.C."; transfer note "Transferd to Ralph Smith 16.th Aug.t 1784" is on the Abston survey at the foot of the page, not this one.

## Notes
- Sycamore Creek / Staunton River, not Sandy Creek. Holloway has no given name. Compare [[S-pitt-olsb2-img10-ellis-sycamore-holloway-lines-1771]] (Holloway line on a Sycamore branch). No Cook, Musick, Flippin, Echols or Collie.
