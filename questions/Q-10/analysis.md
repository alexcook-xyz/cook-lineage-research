# Analysis: Q-10, George, Josiah, and Zachariah Cook in Halifax County, Virginia, 1752–1767

Stage 4. Date: 2026-11-28. Inputs: plan.md, log.md (groups 1–6), the Q-10 source files, and the repo notes the plan cites. No site was contacted for this analysis and no new search was made. This file classifies and correlates evidence only. It states no conclusion (stage 5).

## Scope notes
- **Question:** did George, Josiah, or Zachariah Cook own land, appear in a deed, or litigate in Halifax County between 1752 and 1767? The plan's Objective says 1752–1775; the Done-when tests say before 1767. This analysis tests 1752 to mid-1767 and records 1767–1780 items only where they bear on identity or citation.
- **Era:** early modern. Every dated item is 1758 or later, after the 1752 calendar change; no double dating applies.
- **Tool provenance:** every transcription or summary below came from a browser view, a fetch-and-summarize tool, text extraction, or a search snippet. None has been checked by a second reader against the original. The one reading of an original page image (Pittsylvania p. 17) is the searcher's own reading and is unverified until a second reader checks it.
- **Examined:** Halifax road orders 1752–1767 (one modern abstract); Pittsylvania Court Records Book 1(A) pp. 11–17; the 1767 Pittsylvania tithables (one web transcription).
- **Not examined:** Halifax deed books and indexes (group 1); Halifax Pleas apart from road orders (group 2); the Halifax will books (group 3); Land Office patents and grants (group 4); Bell's Lunenburg tithables (group 5).
