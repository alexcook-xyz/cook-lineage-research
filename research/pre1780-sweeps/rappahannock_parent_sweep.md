# Rappahannock, Mattaponi, and Potomac Basin Parent & Neighboring County Sweep (1656–1750)

**Scope:** Pre-1741 origins and regional footprint of **George Cook** (and wife **Sarah**) and neighbor **Elexius Musick** (plus the **George Musick** family) across Spotsylvania's parent and neighboring counties (`1656–1750`), together with a complete statewide sweep of Virginia Land Office Patents (`1732–1761`, corresponding to *Cavaliers and Pioneers* Vols. 4–6) and Northern Neck Grants (`1690–1775`).

> **Verification standard:** Every positive hit and negative check below distinguishes primary/digitized texts directly inspected in this session (Library of Virginia Land Office patent images, LVA Primo catalog/index records, Archive.org `_djvu.txt` full texts) from secondary/indexed web abstracts (`search_web`).

## Executive Summary: Key Findings vs. Negative Searches

### Verified Positive Hits (Directly Relevant to George Cook & the Musick Family)
1. **George Musick's Pre-Spotsylvania Origin Confirmed in King & Queen -> King William County (`1704–1719`):**
   - **`1704` King & Queen County Quit Rent Roll** (Wertenbaker, *The Planters of Colonial Virginia*, 1922, p. 228): **`Musick George — 100` [acres]**. No `George Cook(e)` appears in King & Queen County (only `Cook Benjamin` 200 a., `Cook Thomas Junr` 50 a., `Cook Thomas Senr` 100 a., `Cook Jno` 50 a.).
   - **`11 July 1719` Royal Patent** (`Land Office Patents No. 10, p. 430`, LVA LONN image `010_0447.tif`): **`George Musick of King William County`** patented **250 acres** (for 25 shillings composition money) on the NW side of Mays Run on the south side of the South River in **St. John's Parish, King William County**, adjoining **Holderby**, **Maj. Braxton**, **Harry Beverley**, and **James Taylor**. Notice that George Musick already adjoined **Harry Beverley** in King William County in 1719—the same Harry Beverley whose Spotsylvania land George Musick & Thomas Allen adjoined in `1728` and from whose daughter Catherine Stubblefield **George Cook** purchased his first Spotsylvania tract in `1741/42`.
2. **George Cook's `12 February 1742[/43]` Spotsylvania Patent Explicitly Names George Musick (`Patents No. 21, pp. 145–146`):**
   - In LVA `Land Office Patents No. 21, pp. 145–146` (images `019_0086.tif`–`0087.tif`, indexed under `Cooke, George`, written **`George Cook`** in both the margin and patent text), George Cook received **400 acres** in **Spotsylvania County** for **40 shillings** composition money (no headrights listed).
   - **Critical newly read bounds (p. 146):** The tract began on the west side of the **New Mine Bank Road** at a corner of **Mr. Griffin Fantleroy** and **Thomas Shelton**, ran along Fantleroy's line near the head of a glade, and then ran *"South ten Degrees West one hundred and eighty four Poles to a Corner White Oak [at/near] the Fork of **Devils Ditch Swamp** close by another White Oak hiccory and red Oak a Corner of **George Musick and Nicholas Randalls Land**"* before running back to **Thomas Shelton's** line. (George & Ann Musick had sold 170 acres to Nicholas Randall on `3 July 1742`, Crozier p. 160; George Musick & John Graves patented 520 acres in the fork of Devils Ditch Swamp adjoining **Joseph Roberts** on `10 July 1745`, `Patents No. 23, p. 936`).
   - **Edmund Waller's `25 November 1743` Patent** (`Patents No. 21, pp. 584–585`, image `019_0330.tif`) for 552 acres in St. George's Parish, Spotsylvania Co., likewise began at Griffin Fantleroy's line *"being a Corner to **George Cooks** Land"* and ran along Cook's line to **Joseph Roberts's** line.
   - Together, these patents prove George Cook's patented Spotsylvania tract (`1742/43`, sold with wife Sarah to Henry Lewis on `6 Sept 1743`) sat immediately contiguous with **George Musick's** Devils Ditch Swamp neighborhood.
3. **Orange County Importation List (`1749/1750`):**
   - W. W. Scott, *A History of Orange County, Virginia* (1907), Appendix A ("Importations", p. 226, compiled by Clerk Philip H. Fry from Orange County Court Order Books), lists **`1750. Cook, Geo.`** (indexed as `1749` in some secondary transcripts). Because our George Cook was already in Spotsylvania by May 1741 (`Crozier`, p. 158) and appeared in Spotsylvania Court in 1748, this Orange County entry is either a distinct later Valley/Orange settler (cf. `1747. Cook, James` on the same page) or a much-delayed proof of an earlier arrival; it warrants direct inspection of Orange County Order Book 5 (`1747–1754`).
4. **Caroline County Order Book Link to the Musick Neighborhood:**
   - Via indexed abstracts (`search_web`), **Ambrose Musick** (son of George Musick Sr.) appears in a Caroline County Order Book attachment obtained by **Joseph Roberts** (the same neighbor adjoining both George Cook's 1742/43 patent and George Musick's 1745 patent in Spotsylvania) summoning Mary Collawan.
5. **Amelia County `3 November 1750` Patent (`Patents No. 29, p. 316`):**
   - **`George Cook`** (`Geo: Cook` in margin, image `027_0330.tif`), **400 acres** in **Amelia County** on the head branches of Harris's Creek (40 shillings composition money, adjoining Hudson and Williamson). This falls in the *Cavaliers and Pioneers* Vol. 6 (`1749–1761`) window, in Southside Virginia (distinct from the Spotsylvania/Rappahannock basin).

### Summary of Negative Searches (No Pre-1748 George Cook(e) or Elexius Musick Found)
- **1704 Virginia Quit Rent Rolls (all surviving counties, Wertenbaker):** **Zero** entries for `George Cook(e)`. (Corrects secondary web claims asserting a `George Cook` on the 1704 King & Queen or Essex rolls).
- **Essex County (`1692–1750`) & Old Rappahannock County (`1656–1692`):** **Zero** hits for `George Cook(e)` or `Musick` in Dorman's *Deeds & Wills No. 13 (1707–1711)*, Dorman's *Wills, Bonds, Inventories (1722–1730)*, LVA's *Essex County Wills & Administrations (1692–1800)* index, LVA's *Land Office Patents* for Essex, or Crozier's Vol. 6 Old Rappahannock abstracts. - **Orange County (`1734–1745`):** **Zero** hits for `George Cook(e)` or `Musick` in Dorman's *Deed Books 1 & 2 (1735–1738)*, *Will Book 1 (1735–1743)*, *Will Book 2 (1744–1778)*, or Miller's *Road Orders (1734–1749)* (all early Orange Cook records belong to Thomas, Daniel, and John Cook on North River/Cook's Run, or German-community Michael/Shem Cook).
- **Cavaliers and Pioneers Vol. 4 (`1732–1741`) & Virginia Northern Neck Land Grants Vols. 1–2 (`1690–1775`):** **Zero** patents or grants for `George Cook(e)` or `Musick` anywhere in Virginia (`1732–1741` or in the Northern Neck Proprietary `1690–1775`).

## Primary Implications for Issue #16 (Pre-1741 Origins)
1. **George Musick & Elexius Musick:** The Musick origin prior to Spotsylvania (`1721/1728`) is cleanly anchored in **King & Queen County (`1704`) -> St. John's Parish, King William County (`1719`)**, with **no** Northern Neck, Stafford, King George, Richmond, or Essex footprint. Notice that George Musick already adjoined **Harry Beverley** in King William County in `1719`—the same proprietor whose Spotsylvania patent line George Musick (`1728`) and **George Cook** (`1741/42`) later settled on. No pre-1748 record of **Elexius Musick** was found outside Spotsylvania County (where he appears in the `1748` Order Book and `1754` will of George Musick Sr.).
2. **George Cook & Wife Sarah:** Despite checking Essex, Old Rappahannock, King & Queen, King William, Caroline, King George, Richmond, Stafford, and Orange counties (`1656–1750`), **no pre-1741 record of George Cook(e)** appeared in any parent or neighboring Rappahannock/Mattaponi/Potomac county—nor on the `1704` Quit Rent Rolls, nor in *Cavaliers and Pioneers* Vols. 4 or 6 (in the northern/central Virginia basin), nor in the Northern Neck Grants.
3. **New Neighborhood Lead (`1742/43` Patent):** Reading George Cook's original `12 February 1742[/43]` patent (`Patents No. 21, pp. 145–146`) establishes that George Cook's 400-acre patent in Spotsylvania **literally cornered on `George Musick and Nicholas Randalls Land` at Devils Ditch Swamp**, right beside **Joseph Roberts** (who also sued Ambrose Musick in Caroline County) and **Thomas Shelton**.
4. **Two Follow-Up Items Flagged:**
   - Inspect **Orange County Order Book 5 (`1747–1754`)** for the **`1750. Cook, Geo.`** (or `1749`) importation oath cited in Scott's *History of Orange County* (p. 226) to confirm or rule out any link to George Cook of Spotsylvania.
   - Direct manual check of **Dorman's *Caroline County Order Books (1732–1746)*** and **Campbell's *Colonial Caroline*** (not open-access online) if an exhaustive negative proof for Caroline civil suits/witnesses is needed.
