## 3. Geographic & Jurisdictional Guides (Places)

- [Adair County, Kentucky](places/adair-county-ky.md) — Russell's Creek settlements, probate, deeds, and vital records (1802–present).
- [Cumberland County, Kentucky](places/cumberland-county-ky.md) — 1836 Power of Attorney and tax list transitional records.

- [Halifax & Lunenburg Counties, Virginia Borderlands](places/halifax-lunenburg-borderlands.md) — Pre-1767 parent county records and North Carolina borderlands.
- [Colonial Tidewater & Chesapeake Ports of Entry](places/colonial-tidewater-ports.md) — 17th- and early 18th-century headrights, passenger lists, and port records.
