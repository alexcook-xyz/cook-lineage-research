## 2. Metes-and-Bounds Mathematical Match

The connection between Amelia County and Prince Edward County is established through survey bearing continuity:
- **1750 Patent (Patents 29:316–317):** 400 acres granted to George Cook by President Thomas Lee for 40 shillings, beginning at a corner white oak running `South 10 degrees West`, `West 6 degrees South 120 poles`, `North 3 degrees West`.
- **1768 Deed (Prince Edward DB 3:366–368):** George Cook sells 150 acres on the branches of Harris's Creek adjoining Lockett and Jones to Alexander Guill. The boundary lines replicate the exact bearing angles of the 1750 patent.
