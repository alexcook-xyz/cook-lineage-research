## Notes

- George's parents' birthplaces are both **Virginia** here — against the 1880 sheet's "Ky" for his father (see `source-1880-census-ed1.md`). The 1900 entry agrees with George Sr.'s own 1860 statement (Pittsylvania, VA). Carried to analysis.
- Eliza reports **8 children born, 4 living** (June 1900). Named children so far: Thomas H., John, George W., Liddie, Laura B., Mattie — six. Two further children are unaccounted for, and only two of {Thomas, John, Liddie, Mattie} can have been living in 1900 alongside George and Laura. Arithmetic only; no identification is made from this alone.
- Laura B.'s birth is given as Dec 1870 (age 29); the 1880 sheet gives her age as 13 (b. ~1866–67). Normal census age drift; noted for analysis.
- The sheet carries later pencilled numbers (67/69) above the dwelling/family columns; the written dwelling/family numbers are 3/3. The FamilySearch index's "Household Identifier 69" comes from the pencilled number.
