# Halifax & Lunenburg Counties, Virginia: Borderlands Guide

- **Lunenburg County Formation:** Created 1746 from Brunswick County (originally encompassed all of Southside Virginia west of the Brunswick line).
- **Halifax County Formation:** Created 1752 from Lunenburg County.
- **Pittsylvania County Formation:** Created 1767 from the western portion of Halifax County.
- **Key Watercourses:** Staunton (Roanoke) River, Dan River, Banister River, and Sandy Creek.

---

## 1. Relevance to the Cook Lineage

Because Pittsylvania County did not exist prior to 1767, any early records of George Cook, Josiah Cook, or Zachariah Cook in the Dan River and Sandy Creek region prior to 1767 are recorded in Halifax and Lunenburg counties:
- **Zachariah Cook:** Appears in early Halifax records and died in 1767 right as Pittsylvania was formed (Pittsylvania Court Order Book 1A, page 17).
- **Josiah Cook:** Patented 261 acres on Sandy Creek surveyed 25 March 1779 and granted 12 June 1780 (Grants Book A, pages 403–404).

---

## 2. High-Priority Research Tasks

- **Halifax County Deed Books 1–10 & Plea Books 1–9 (1752–1775):** Search for Cook entries along the Dan River and Sandy Creek (tracked in Issue #10).
- **Lunenburg County Tithable Lists (1748–1783):** Search Landon C. Bell's *Sunlight on the Southside* for Cook tithables in the upper districts.
- **North Carolina Borderlands:** Check Caswell, Orange, and Granville County records along the state line directly south of Sandy Creek.
