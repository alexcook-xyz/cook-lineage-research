## Transcription (verbatim, verified)

> I Eliza E Cook of Adair County Kentucky make and publish this my last will and testament First I will and bequeath to my two sons **Thomas H Cook** and **John D Cook**, to be divided equally between them, all the live stock that I may own at my death and all the money that I may have at my death, Second I will and bequeath to my son **George W. Cook** all the remainder of my estate, real and personal, including my interest in the tract of land I now live on, Witness my hand this 8th day of October 1900
>
> Eliza E Cook (her mark)
>
> Witnessed by Jas Earnest [?] Jr, H. A. Coffey

Transcription verified from the full image by the lead (Jolly), 2026-11-28, including an enlarged reading of the names. Retrieved by Muse browser pass M-6 on 2026-11-28.

## Notes

- **This settles conflict C1: the son is John D. Cook.** The initial is a clear cursive capital D. The 2026-11-26 retrieval summary's "John M." was a misreading. (The M-6 pass itself misread George's initial as "M."; on the image it is "W." — George W. Cook, as everywhere else. This clerk's capitals are a known trap in this hand; both corrections come from the lead's enlarged reading.) **Second reading:** an independent reader, given only the enlarged excerpt and no context, read the same three names — Thomas H. (probable), **John D. (certain — "a tall diagonal stem carrying a large, fully closed oval bowl… the classic cursive capital D, unlike any other capital in the document")**, George W. (probable; the reader's excerpt clipped the letter's left edge, which is complete in the full image the lead read). C1 therefore rests on two independent readings, not one.
- The will names **only the three sons** — no daughter is named, although Laura B. was living with Eliza when the will was made (1900 census, enumerated that June). Recorded as a fact; no explanation is offered by the document.
- **Repo plate defect (found during this question):** the pre-existing plates `sources/ky-adair/Adair_WBM_p133.png` and `Adair_WBM_p134.png` are byte-identical duplicates of a single spread showing book pages 134–135 (the probate certificate and unrelated wills). Neither contains the will text, and the p133 filename does not match the pages shown. The M-6 plates above supply the true pp. 132–133 and 134–135 spreads. Cleanup of the defective plates is recommended as a separate fix; this question does not delete files outside its question folder.
