## Transcription
Index lines read (85 dpi; Cook line re-read at 200 and 400 dpi): "Cook Harmon 28 - 4[overwritten digit, unreadable]" and "Cook James 41"; "Echols Abadiah 43"; "Philippins Jos. 43"; "Collins Mary 61", "Collins Elizabeth 50"; Cox John 39; King Elijah 46-47, King James 47, King Joseph 62; Clement Isaac 52. Under H the names run Hankins, Hairstone, Harrison, Hampton, Hardiman, Hammond, Hall, Harden, Hardy, Harris, Hailey, Haynes, Hargett, Hendrick, Henderson, Hill, Hodges, Hodnett, Horton, Hurt, Hutchings, Hunt (no Holloway). Under M the names run from Minier to Martin (no Musick, Music, Muzick). No Collie. No George, Elijah, Elisha or Zachariah Cook (Elijah King is a King). "Justices of the Court" are indexed on pp. 1, 45, 58, 62, 66 and 70.

## Notes
Harmon Cook's second page reads "4" followed by an overwritten digit; the body of the volume gives Harmon Cook on p. 44 (see S-pitt-claims-1782-p44-harmon-cook-assignee). Index pages for T-Z (leaves 22-25) not searched. The index is the clerk's own and may omit entries; pp. 9-27 and 30-39 of the body were scanned at 85 dpi only, so a missed entry on those pages is not excluded by the index alone.
