## Citation
Pittsylvania County, Virginia, marriage bonds, 1777, 1778 and 1785, images in "1767-1787 Pittsylvania County Marriage Licenses," *Internet Archive* (https://archive.org/details/1785_20250812 : accessed 28 Nov 2026), file 1777_1780.pdf pp. 5, 7, 10 and file 1785.pdf pp. 24, 29. Holding repository not stated in the item.

## Transcription
Names as read (uncertain letters marked). The spelling is a Prewitt type, not "Pruett". None is a Cook.
- 1777_1780.pdf p. 5: bond dated "26th Day of Novr Anno Dom 1777", "Joseph Austin and William Loggin" [second name uncertain: Loggin/Coggin], marriage "Joseph Austin and Wealthey Prewit" [? last letters faint: Prewit/Pruit].
- 1777_1780.pdf p. 7: bond dated "the 3[?] Day of [month illegible] Anno Dom 1777", "Edwd Covington and Jos Austin" [? first surname Covington; the second signer's first name is abbreviated], marriage "Edward Covington and Frances Prewitt" [? Prewett/Prewit; final letters torn by a fold].
- 1777_1780.pdf p. 10: bond dated "[day illegible] of Octr Anno Dom 1778", "Levy Prewitt? and Jos Akin", marriage "Levy Prewit[t?] and Elizabeth Fulkerson?" [bride surname uncertain: Fulkerson/Tulkerson].
- 1785.pdf p. 24: bond dated "17th Day of Octob 1785", "Zachariah Prewot [? Prewet/Prewit] & Benjn Thrashe[r]", marriage "Zach. Prewot [?] and Jane Burgess".
- 1785.pdf p. 29: bond dated "17th Day of October 1785", "Benjn Thrasher and Zachariah Prewet[t?]", marriage "Benjn Thrasher and Sarah White".

## Notes
- Read only to check for a Pruett/Prewitt link to a Ruth who married a Cook (an earlier lead says Samuell Pruett's daughter Ruth married a Cook; unverified). These pages name no Ruth and no Cook. They show only that Prewitt-type families were in Pittsylvania from 1777. Whether they are the same family as the testator Samuell Pruett (Deeds & Wills Book 11:240, 1801) is not shown.
- Jos. Akin (Joseph Akin) appears as co-bondsman on many 1777-1785 bonds; not the same as any Cook.
- Read from page images; handwriting is a clerk's or party's hand with faded ink; letters marked [?] are doubtful.
