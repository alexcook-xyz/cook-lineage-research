# Prince Edward & Amelia Counties, Virginia: Jurisdictional & Archival Guide

- **Amelia County Formation:** Created 1734/1735 from Prince George and Brunswick counties ([Amelia County Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amelia_County,_Virginia "Central Virginia county formed in 1735 from Prince George and Brunswick counties; originally included modern Prince Edward County.")).
- **Prince Edward County Formation:** Created 1754 from the western portion of Amelia County ([Prince Edward County Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Edward_County,_Virginia "Southside Virginia county with its seat at Farmville, formed from western Amelia County in 1754.")).
- **Key Watercourses & Vicinity:** **Harris's Creek** and **Vaughan's Creek** (upper [Appomattox River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appomattox_River "157-mile tributary of the James River flowing through central and southside Virginia past Farmville and Petersburg to City Point.") watershed in [Southside Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southside_%28Virginia%29 "Historic cultural and agricultural region of Virginia south of the James River, centered on tobacco farming during the colonial era."), near modern [Prospect, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prospect,_Virginia "Unincorporated community in western Prince Edward County, Virginia, near the Appomattox River drainage.")).

## 1. Relevance to the Cook Lineage

Amelia and Prince Edward counties anchor the **21-year documented Virginia residence (1750–1771)** of **George Cook (Generation 9 candidate)** and his wife **Sarah**:
- **3 November 1750 (*Patents 29:316–317*):** George Cook patented **400 acres** in western [Amelia County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amelia_County,_Virginia "Central Virginia county formed in 1735 from Prince George and Brunswick counties; originally included modern Prince Edward County.") on the head branches of **Harris's Creek** (40 shillings treasury right), adjoining **Charles Hudson** (*Patents 18:44–45*) and **Thomas Williamson** (*Patents 28:212–213*, sold in 1749 to **Thomas Lockett III**).
- **September Court 1751 (*Amelia Order Book 2, pp. 371–372*):** George Cook and his male [tithables](# "Colonial Virginia poll-tax unit (1620s–1780s): any free male, indentured servant, or enslaved person aged 16+, for whom the head of household paid annual colony, county, and parish taxes.") were ordered to assist surveyor James Withers on the [Rolling Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_road "Colonial-era dirt track used to roll heavy wooden casks (hogsheads) of cured tobacco from inland plantations down to river landings and inspection warehouses.") crossing **Vaughan's Creek** alongside neighbors **Peter Coffee**, **William Mills**, **Charles Simmons**, and **Elkanah Jennings**.
- **9 September 1760 (*Prince Edward Order Book 2*):** Following the 1754 division into [Prince Edward County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Edward_County,_Virginia "Southside Virginia county with its seat at Farmville, formed from western Amelia County in 1754.") ([St. Patrick's Parish](# "Anglican (Church of England) parish established 1 Sep 1755 coextensive with Prince Edward County; its vestry managed local poor relief, parish levies, and land processioning (1755–1774).")), `George Cook` was listed for **1 [tithable](# "Colonial Virginia poll-tax unit (1620s–1780s): any free male, indentured servant, or enslaved person aged 16+, for whom the head of household paid annual colony, county, and parish taxes.")** alongside `Peter Coffee` and in the same court session **`Excused from paying County Levy`** (indicating advanced age or infirmity).
- **1762–1769 Court Orders & 1765 Deed:** Suits by Glasgow credit houses ([Archibald Buchanan & Co.](# "Glasgow tobacco-lord syndicate (Archibald Buchanan, d. 1761, & partners) operating inland credit stores in colonial Virginia to trade British goods for cured tobacco."), [Alexander Speirs & Co.](# "One of Glasgow's wealthiest colonial tobacco syndicates led by Tobacco Lord Alexander Speirs (1714–1782), running store credit accounts across Prince Edward and Southside Virginia."), and [John Pleasants / Routledge & Co.](# "Virginia-Glasgow tobacco credit partnership (John Pleasants and Routledge & Co.) that extended store credit to Piedmont planters and sued on overdue bonds in Prince Edward court, 1769–1770.")) in *PE OB 2:131, 245*; witness for neighbor Charles Simmons on Bush Creek (*PE DB 2:277*, `13 Feb 1765`); bail posted by **William Mills** and **Peter Coffee** in `Pleasants vs. George Cooke` where George failed to appear in August 1769 (*PE OB 4:229*).
- **Complete Sale of the 400-Acre Patent (`1768 & 1771`):**
  - **18 April 1768 (*PE DB 3:366–368; OB 4:102, 110*):** Conveyed southern **150 acres** to **Alexander Guill** (£13); wife **Sarah** relinquished dower on **20 May 1768** (*OB 4:110*).
  - **6 April 1771 (*PE DB 5:67–68*):** Conveyed remaining **250 acres** (`"patent bearing date the third day of November MDCCL"`, `"all that tract of Land he the sd. George Cook now possesses and lives on"`) to **Lovel Wilkerson** (£30), proved June & 16 September 1771, no wife joining.
  - Both purchasers—**Alexander Guill** and **Lovell Wilkerson**—subsequently appeared side by side in the 1783 *Heads of Families* list for Prince Edward County (`p. 59`).

## 2. Metes-and-Bounds Bearing Correlation (1750 Patent → 1768 & 1771 Deeds)

| 1750 Amelia Patent (*Patents 29:316–317*, 400 ac) | 1768 Prince Edward Deed (*DB 3:366–368*, 150 ac to Guill) | 1771 Prince Edward Deed (*DB 5:67–68*, 250 ac to Wilkerson) |
|---|---|---|
| Beginning at corner white oak in **Charles Hudson's** line | Beginning at pointers in **Lockett's** line *(former Williamson tract)* | Explicitly conveys **"patent bearing date ... MDCCL [3 Nov 1750]"** |
| `S 10° W 263 poles` (crossing Harris's Creek) | `S 10° W 180 poles` (southern 180 poles) | Northern balance retained (`adjoining to George Cooke` in 1769 *DB 3:446*) |
| `W 6° S 120 poles` | `W 6° S 120 poles` (**Exact match**) | Sold `6 Apr 1771` for £30 (**250 acres**) |
| `N 3° W 351 poles` | `N 3° W 178 poles` + new `N 86° E 164 p.` partition line | Completes full disposition of all 400 acres (`150 + 250`) |

## 3. Separate County `Cook(e)` Clusters (Not Harris's Creek)

- **Raleigh Parish / Hurt's & Flatt Creeks (Amelia Co.):** **Shem Cooke** (`DB 5:402`, 1756, King William origin → Granville Co., NC & later Pittsylvania *Cavaliers* 7:216) and **John Cooke Sr.** & wife **Judith** (`DB 5:202, 8:399, 608`, → Pittsylvania Co. by 1773 per LVA Chancery `007-1773-010`).
- **Prince Edward `John` & `William Cook` (`1783–1800`):** Taxed on `1785` *Heads of Families* and `1789` Personal Property Tax (`John Cook & Willm Cook`, 5 enslaved, 4 horses); William Cooke sued Samuel Matthews's executors over 60 acres (*LVA Chancery 147-1788-005 & 147-1800-008*).
- **Cumberland / Briery Creek (`James Cooke`, d. 1782):** Son of `John Cooke Sr.` of Green Creek, Cumberland County (*WB 2:193*, 1775).
