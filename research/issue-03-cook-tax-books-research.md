# Issue #3: Cumberland County tax books for George and Henry Cook

Research checkpoint, 28 November 2026. The [GitHub issue](https://github.com/alexcook-xyz/cook-lineage-research/issues/3) asks for the brothers’ first Cumberland County tax appearances, George’s transition into Adair County, and evidence for when he turned 21. The deed-book images establish a November 1836 Cumberland connection. The 1833 tax return has been located, but no complete district search or verified Cook transcription has been completed. No poll count, acreage, or watercourse has been established from the tax books.

## What the deed images show

- **2 November 1836:** Pittsylvania County, Virginia, Deed Book 39, p. 242, identifies George Cook and Henry Cook as “of the County of Cumberland and State of Kentucky” and authorizes “our Brother James Cook of Adair County” to convey inherited land to James Nance. The power of attorney spells out “two hundred and twenty seven acres” in Pittsylvania County. That is not a Kentucky tax acreage, and it differs from the issue’s 277-acre description of the Virginia estate; compare with the 1831 deed before reconciling the figures. This is a dated Cumberland residence statement, not evidence of first arrival. [Deed book scan](https://github.com/alexcook-xyz/cook-lineage-research/blob/main/sources/va-pittsylvania/Pittsylvania_DB39_p242.png)
- **10 November 1836:** Pittsylvania County Deed Book 39, pp. 256–257, again identifies George and Henry with Cumberland and James with Adair. It recites that the two brothers “were infants” when the earlier deed was made. The documents support a minority-to-apparent-capacity interval if the earlier conveyance and applicable law are verified; the November date is not itself a date of majority. The repository’s estimate of circa 1813 is a working birth estimate, not proof of a 21st birthday in 1834. ## Record pull

- **Cumberland first:** Start at frame 37 of [DGS 007834420](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/film?dgs=007834420&cat=155130&i=0) and locate the 1837 marker and district boundaries. Continue with 1840–1845. If another copy is needed for an unreadable frame, check the [KHS inventory](https://kyhistory.com/digital/api/collection/p16602coll3/id/137/download), reels **73-0088A and 73-0089**.
- **Adair in parallel:** Pull 1835–1838 and 1840–1845, then check 1833 to establish a baseline. [FamilySearch catalog](https://www.familysearch.org/search/catalog/154727), [film 7852 inventory](https://www.mipolonia.net/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/Bloomfield-Hills_FHC_film-fiche_inventory_July_2016.pdf) (includes 1833, 1835–1838 and 1840–1844), [KHS inventory](https://kyhistory.com/digital/api/collection/p16602coll3/id/137/download) (county reel range 73-0001–73-0006), and [KDLA inventory](https://kdla.ky.gov/Archives-and-Reference/Documents/Inventory_Land_Records.pdf) (ask staff to confirm whether the 1834 original is actually present and whether a separate copy of the 1839 return survives). Track James separately as a known Adair comparator, not as a stand-in for either brother.
- **Clinton as a boundary check:** Clinton was formed from Cumberland and Wayne effective 1 April 1836. If a Cumberland entry disappears or a tract falls near that line, compare Clinton’s returns from 1837 onward before treating the disappearance as a move. [Newberry county chronologies](https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/documents/KY_Individual_County_Chronologies.htm) · [FamilySearch catalog](https://www.familysearch.org/search/catalog/155122) · [KHS inventory](https://kyhistory.com/digital/api/collection/p16602coll3/id/137/download) (73-0085–73-0086).
- **Capture and cite:** Check all districts, supplemental or delinquent lists, and Cook/Cooke, abbreviations, and initials; capture title and column headers, the full entry, and nearby names. Record tax year, district, roll/DGS and image/page, assessment date or effective date if given, exact name, exact poll/age column and value, acreage by tract, county where the land lies, watercourse, and any land-title or residence clues. Record blanks as blanks, not zero. Use “not found” only after logging the specific, complete and legible sections searched. [FamilySearch Kentucky taxation guide](https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Kentucky_Taxation) · [KHS tax-list guide and historical forms](https://history.ky.gov/kentucky-ancestors/tax-lists-1792-1840-an-overlooked-resource-for-kentucky-history-and-land-titles)
- **Interpret conservatively:** The county of assessment and the county where land lies can differ. Correlate any change in the personal poll entry with deeds, residence wording, watercourses, and associates before claiming a move. The first named poll entry is evidence of status at the applicable assessment date if the column and identity warrant it; it does not by itself establish a 21st birthday. Check the actual year’s headings and governing law, and seek corroboration such as a dated guardianship settlement, court record, or family record. [KHS discussion of land reporting, age columns and statutory changes](https://history.ky.gov/kentucky-ancestors/tax-lists-1792-1840-an-overlooked-resource-for-kentucky-history-and-land-titles)
