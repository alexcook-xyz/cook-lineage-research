# Cook Patrilineal Lineage Research: From South-Central Kentucky to Colonial Arrival in North America

This repository documents the direct patrilineal line of the Cook family from south-central Kentucky (Adair, Taylor, and Cumberland counties) back through Virginia to the family's first arrival in British North America. It is a shared, public research registry for anyone who encounters this line and wants to use, check, or extend the work.

The research applies the **Genealogical Proof Standard (GPS)** to every person, primary document, jurisdiction, and open question along the line. Each generational link is tracked with a probability and the records that would prove or disprove it.

## Research Standards & Acceptance Criteria

Every ancestral claim and person profile in this project must meet strict genealogical criteria before being marked as **Proven**:
1. **[Research Standards & Acceptance Criteria](wiki/standards/acceptance-standards.md):** Defines what constitutes a "Complete Profile" and what direct or indirect evidence is required for a person to be accepted as a verified patrilineal lineage member.
2. **[Methodology & Tools](wiki/methods/research-methods-and-tools.md):** Describes our research workflow, courthouse retrieval procedures, metes-and-bounds deed platting, same-name disambiguation matrices, and archival analysis tools.

## Research Tracking: Milestones & Issues

Work is organized as one **epic** that tracks every generational link to the GPS standard: **[Epic #32](https://github.com/alexcook-xyz/cook-lineage-research/issues/32)**. Each link has a current and target probability, and the issues that move it. Tags on each issue show access type (`in-person-archive`, `blocked-film`, `remote-request`, `online-free`, `online-paid`), priority (`priority-p0`–`p3`), and the link it advances (`link-N-N`).

- **[Milestones Overview](https://github.com/alexcook-xyz/cook-lineage-research/milestones):**
