## Transcription
p. 140: "Memorandum that on the 10th Day of July 1781 Peaceable and Quiet Possession of the Land within granted and sold was had and taken by the within Named Nathaniel Morry [or Murray] and by him delivered to the within Mentioned Jeremiah Stimson the usual Symbols of Livery &c According to [Force and Virtue]. Signed and Delivered in the presents of Daniel Everett George Cook / Elizah Cook Wm Cook" (signed Nathaniel Morry). "Received of Jeremiah Stimson twenty pounds Money being the Consideration in the within Deed Express witness my Hand this 10th Day of July 1781. Test Daniel Everett George Cook / Elizah Cook Wm Cook" (signed Nathaniel Morry). "At a Court held for Pittsylvania County the 17th Day of [July] 1781 The within Indenture together with the Memorandum of Livery and Seizen and Receipt hereon indorsed was Acknowledged by the within Named Nathaniel Murray to be his Several Acts & Deed and the same was Ordered to be Recorded by the Court. Test Will. Tunstall Clk."
The deed body (image 80, pp. 138-139) was read by Muse in the viewer only: 10 July 1781, Nathaniel Murray of Camden Parish to Jeremiah [Stimson, per the memorandum and receipt], 20 pounds, land on Little Double Creek, part of 251 acres. Acreage not resolved (20 or 120 acres). Not checked against a saved image.
