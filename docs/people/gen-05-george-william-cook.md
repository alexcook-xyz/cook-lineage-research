# Generation 5: George William Cook (1852–1935)

- **Lineage Position:** 2nd Great-Grandfather / Generation 5 ("The Bridge")
- **Lineage Proof Status:** **Proven**
- **Direct Father:** [George W. Cook (c. 1813–1873)](gen-06-george-w-cook.md)
- **Mother:** Eliza E. Cole (1828–1905)
- **Spouse:** Emma Anna / Enna Janes (1894–1980), married 27 September 1911 in Adair County.

---

## 1. Vital Chronology & Geography

- **Birth:** 26 February 1852, Adair County, Kentucky.
- **Death:** 16 July 1935 (6 A.M.), Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky (age 83).
- **Residence:** Russell's Creek and North Columbia voting precinct, Adair County.

---

## 2. Primary Records & Archival Plates

1. **1911 Adair County Marriage Register (Vol. 22:490–491):**
   - Preserved in `evidence/ky-modern/Adair_1911_Marriage_Cook_Janes.png` (Plate 3 in paper).
   - Bond executed 26 Sep 1911 by `George W. Cook` & `J. T. Barbee Jr.`
   - License states George W. Cook age 58, 1st marriage, farmer, b. Adair Co.; bride Enna Janes age 17, 1st marriage, b. Adair Co., parents Wesley Janes. Married 27 Sep 1911 at Wes Janes by J. G. Smith, wit. John H. Sneed & Maggie Richards.
2. **1935 Kentucky Death Certificate #18658:**
   - Preserved in `evidence/ky-modern/Adair_1935_DeathCert_18658.png` (Plate 4 in paper).
   - `George William Cook`, farmer, b. `1852-2-26`, d. `July 16, 1935`, age 83y 4m 20d. Cause: intestinal tuberculosis. Father `George Cook` (hearsay `"Ky"`), mother maiden name `Cole` (`"Ky"`). Informant: `Mrs. G. W. Cook`.
3. **1877 Adair County Deed Book Z, p. 195:**
   - Preserved in `evidence/ky-adair/Adair_DBZ_p194-195_EXACT.png` (Plate 5 in paper).
   - Heirship conveyance: George W. Cook conveys to brother Thomas H. Cook two undivided shares (2/6 = 1/3) of the 142-acre tract owned by *"my father George Cook at the time of his death"* on Russell's Creek.
4. **1900/1906 Will of Mother Eliza E. Cook (Will Book M, pp. 133–134):**
   - Preserved in `evidence/ky-adair/Adair_WBM_p133.png` & `p134.png` (Plate 6 in paper).
   - Executed 8 Oct 1900, proved 1 Jan 1906; devises livestock and money equally to *"my two sons Thomas H. Cook and John D. Cook"*, and devises the remainder of estate and home tract to *"my son George W. Cook"*.

---

## 3. Archival Traps Resolved

- **Hearsay Birthplace Trap:** Death certificate listed father's birthplace as `"Ky"` (informant was his young surviving wife). Overturned by father's own lifetime statement in the 1860 census reporting `"PITTSYLVANIA VA"`.
- **Ancestry Corrupt Index:** Vendor database inverted mother `Eliza Cole` as his spouse.
