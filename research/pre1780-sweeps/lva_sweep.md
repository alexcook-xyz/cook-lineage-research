# Library of Virginia (LVA) Primary-Archive Sweep: George Cook (1734–1785) & Pre-1750 Virginia Cook/Cooke Origins

**Scope & Methodology:** Exhaustive primary-record query across the Library of Virginia (LVA) digital repositories conducted 2026-11-27:
1. **Virginia Land Office Patents and Grants** via LVA Primo VE (`https://lva.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/primaws/rest/pub/pnxs`, `tab=Digital`, `scope=DIG_SP`, facet `lds04=Lan`) and direct inspection of digitized Patent/Grant Book page scans (`https://image.lva.virginia.gov/LONN/...` via `GetLONN.pl`).
2. **Chancery Records Index (CRI)** (`https://old.lva.virginia.gov/chancery/`) across 13 Southside/James River counties (`1730–1859` plus undated/all-years checks) and direct inspection of scanned PDF cause files (`case_images.asp`).
3. **Legislative Petitions Digital Collection** (`lds04=Legpt`) and **Revolutionary War Public Service Claims** (`lds04=Psc`), plus Colonial Papers (`Colpap`), Lost Records Localities (`Lost`), Military Records (`Mlt`), and Land Office Military Certificates (`Lomcdc`).

### 1.2 Verbatim Transcription of George Cook's 3 November 1750 Patent (Patents No. 29, pp. 316–317)

Inspected directly from LVA digitized microfilm scans:
- Page 316: `https://image.lva.virginia.gov/LONN/LO-1/027/027_0330.tif`
- Page 317: `https://image.lva.virginia.gov/LONN/LO-1/027/027_0331.tif`

**Marginalia (p. 316):** `Geo: Cook 400.` | `Exam^d.`

**Verbatim Text (pp. 316–317):**
> **George** the second by the Grace of God of Great Britain France and Ireland King Defender of the Faith &c. To all to whom these Presents shall come Greeting **Know ye** that for divers good Causes and Considerations but more especially for and in Consideration of the Sum of **Forty Shillings** of good and lawful Money for our Use paid to our Receiver General of our Revenues in this our Colony and Dominion of Virginia **We** have given granted and confirmed and by these Presents for us our heirs and Successors do give grant and confirm unto **George Cook** one certain Tract or Parcel of Land containing **four hundred Acres** lying and being in the **County of Amelia** on the **head Branches of Harriss's Creek** and bounded as followeth to wit **Beginning** at a Corner white Oak in **Hudson's Line** thence **East forty Degrees South one hundred and sixty Poles** to a Corner red Oak thence **South seven Degrees West fifty two Poles** to **Williamson's Line** thence **along his Lines West South West** `[end of p. 316]`
> `[top of p. 317]` **forty two Poles** to his Corner white Oak thence **South ten Degrees West two hundred and sixty three Poles** Crossing Harris's Creek to a Corner thence **West six Degrees South one hundred and twenty Poles** to a faced Corner thence **North three Degrees West three hundred and fifty one Poles** to **Hudson's Line** thence **along his Line East forty one Degrees North one hundred and forty two Poles** to the Beginning **With all** Woods Underwoods Swamps Marshes Lowgrounds Meadows Feedings and his due Share of all Veins Mines and Quarries as well discovered as not discovered within the Bounds aforesaid and being Part of the said Quantity of four hundred Acres of Land and the Rivers Waters and Water Courses therein contained together with the Privileges of Hunting Hawking Fishing Fowling and all other Profits Commodities and Hereditaments whatsoever to the same or any Part thereof belonging or in anywise appertaining **To have hold** possess and enjoy the said Tract or Parcel of Land and all other the before granted Premises and every Part thereof with their and every of their Appurtenances unto the said **George Cook** and to his Heirs and Assigns forever to the only Use and Behoof of him the said **George Cook** his Heirs and Assigns forever **To be held** of us our Heirs and Successors as of our Mannor of East Greenwich in the County of Kent in free and common Soccage and not in Capite or by Knight's Service **Yielding and Paying** unto us our Heirs and Successors for every fifty Acres of Land and so proportionably for a lesser or greater Quantity than fifty Acres the Fee Rent of one Shilling yearly to be paid upon the Feast of Saint Michael the Arch Angel and also cultivating and improving three Acres Part of every fifty of the Tract abovementioned within three Years after the Date of these Presents **Provided** always that if three Years of the said Fee Rent shall at any time be in Arrear and unpaid or if the said **George Cook** his Heirs or Assigns do not within the Space of three Years next coming after the Date of these Presents cultivate and improve three Acres Part of every fifty of the Tract abovementioned then the Estate hereby granted shall cease and be utterly determined and thereafter it shall and may be lawful to and for us our Heirs and Successors to grant the same Lands and Premises with the Appurtenances unto such other Person or Persons as we our Heirs and Successors shall think fit **In Witness** whereof we have caused these our Letters Patent to be made **Witness** our Trusty and Welbeloved **Thomas Lee Esqr.** President of our Council and Commander in Chief of our said Colony and Dominion at Williamsburgh under the Seal of our said Colony the **third Day of November One thousand seven hundred and fifty In the twenty fourth Year of our Reign.**
> **Exam^d.** | **Thomas Lee P.**

**Key Findings from Patents 29:316–317:**
- **Right type:** **Treasury right** (`Forty Shillings` paid to the Receiver General), **not** headrights.
- **Confirmed Direct Abutters:** Only two neighboring landowners are named in the patent's metes and bounds:
  1. **`Hudson's Line`** (northwest/north boundary, bearing `East forty one Degrees North one hundred and forty two Poles`).
  2. **`Williamson's Line`** (east/southeast boundary, bearing `West South West forty two Poles` and `South ten Degrees West two hundred and sixty three Poles Crossing Harris's Creek`).

### 2.2 Geometric & Topographical Reconstruction of George Cook's Neighbors

1. **Who "Hudson's Line" Was:**
   - George Cook's 3 Nov 1750 patent (`Patents 29:316–317`) begins at a corner white oak in **`Hudson's Line`**, runs southeast/south/west around the head branches of Harris's Creek, then runs `North three Degrees West three hundred and fifty one Poles to Hudson's Line thence along his Line East forty one Degrees North one hundred and forty two Poles to the Beginning`.
   - Checking ALL `Hudson` patents in Amelia/Prince Edward Counties (`15` patents returned in LVA Primo) reveals **only one** Hudson tract anywhere near the Harris's Creek / Vaughan's Creek divide: **Charles Hudson's** 2,000-acre patent on both sides of Vaughan's Creek (`Patents 18:44–45`).
   - Direct inspection of `Patents 18:44` (`016_0062.tif`) confirms Charles Hudson's southeastern closing course is **`East forty one Degrees North six hundred and thirty three Poles to the beginning`** — the exact `E 41° N` bearing that forms the northwestern boundary of George Cook's 400 acres.
   - Other Hudson patentees in Amelia County were on different creeks: `William Hudson` (1737, Deep Creek), `Samuel` and `John Hudson` (1739, Sandy River), `George Hudson` (1739, Buffalo River), `Hall Hudson` (1746, Winningham's and Hicks's Creeks), `James Hudson` (1743–1748, Woody Creek / Little Nottoway / Buffalo Bed Creek), `Isaac Hudson` (1737, Little Creek of Appomattox), `Irby Hudson` (1739, Flat Creek).
2. **Who "Williamson's Line" Was:**
   - **Thomas Williamson's** 400-acre patent of **20 August 1747** (`Patents 28:212–213`, `026_0231.tif`–`026_0232.tif`) lies `"on both sides of Harriss's Creek"`, adjoining **`Locketts Line`** and a faded `[?Hill's/Gill's]` corner red oak (`Patents 28:212`).
   - George Cook's east boundary (`South seven Degrees West fifty two Poles to Williamson's Line thence along his Lines West South West forty two Poles to his Corner white Oak thence South ten Degrees West two hundred and sixty three Poles Crossing Harris's Creek`) abuts directly onto Thomas Williamson's 1747 tract (`Williamsons` western edge also includes `South ten degrees East` / `West` courses on Harris's Creek).
3. **Neighboring Lockett, Holland, Venable, Harris, Gresham, Watson, Richey, and Cupples Tracts:**
   - **Thomas Lockett** held two Amelia County patents on Falling River / Falling Creek (`Patents 21:95`, 1742; `Patents 21:370`, 1743), **not** indexed under Harris's Creek — yet **`Locketts Line`** appears directly on Harris's Creek as an abutter in **Thomas Williamson** (1747, `Patents 28:213`), **William Watson** (1747, `Patents 28:269`), **Edward Gresham** (1749, `Patents 27:346`), and **Charles Smith** (1781, `Grants E:924`, "branches of Vaughans and Harrisson's Creek adjoining land of Elam, Lockett &c."). Thus Lockett's Harris's Creek holding came by private deed/mesne conveyance (county deed books) rather than a Crown patent in his own name on Harris's Creek.
   - **Michael Holland** (`Patents 18:46`, 20 July 1738, 2,630 acres on Vaughan's Creek) formed **`Hollands Line`** adjoining William Watson (1747), Edward Gresham (1749), John Richey (1749), and Charles Cupples (1753).
   - **Abraham Venable** (`Patents 18:122`, 1738; `Patents 20:493–494`, 1743, 3,300 acres) anchored the lower/eastern end of Harris's Creek down to the Appomattox River, bordering Edward Harris (1745), William Watson (1747), Edward Gresham (1749), John Richey (1749), and Charles Cupples (1753).
   - **Alexander Guill:** **Zero** patents or grants appear in LVA Land Office Patents & Grants for `Guill` or `Guille` anywhere in Virginia. Any land held by Alexander Guill in Prince Edward County was acquired by private deed or later transfer, not Crown patent.
   - **Jones:** Five grantee patents to `Jones` appear in Amelia/Prince Edward in Primo (`1319` total statewide), none matching `"Harris's Creek"` in patent abstracts.

### 4.2 High-Priority Findings from Direct Inspection of Scanned Chancery Case Files

1. **ZERO Suits Naming George Cook (Generation 9) or Harris's Creek Land:**
   - **No** chancery suit in Prince Edward, Amelia, Cumberland, Charlotte, Lunenburg, Halifax, Henrico, Chesterfield, Surry, or Isle of Wight names `George Cook` / `George Cooke` as plaintiff, defendant, or testator/decedent (1730–1859).
   - The **only** `Cook` + `Guill*` overlap (`037-1801-012`, Charlotte Co.) was inspected directly and **disambiguated**: it is **not** a Harris's Creek land or Alexander Guill matter (see #2 below).
   - The **only** `Prince Edward` suit indexing both `Cook` and `Harris` (`147-1834-008`) was inspected directly: `Abraham Cook` appears solely as one of several garnishees/debtors to `John E. Sharp` in `Samuel Wright, Admr. of Gabriel Wright vs. John E. Sharp` (`147_1834_008_0002.pdf`).

2. **Charlotte Co. `037-1801-012` — *Rhody Cook ETC vs. Edward Keeling* (Inspected PDFs `037_1801_012_0001.pdf`–`0003.pdf`):**
   - **Disambiguation:** Petition for Freedom (filed Dec 1795 / leave granted Nov 1801) by **`Rhody Cook, Charlotte Cook, Delphy Cook, Jenny Cook, John Cook, Nancy Cook & George Cook`**, the last two being children of Rhody, all infants under 21 by **`Nancy Cook the elder`** (their natural guardian), stating Nancy the elder was `"born free in the County of King & Queen & partly raised in this County"`, bound to `John Gale`, sold for her term to `Edward Keeling`, and that Keeling recently sold `John` to **`Robert Guilliam`** of Charlotte Co. (unrelated to Alexander Guill or George Cook of Harris's Creek).

3. **Prince Edward Co. `147-1766-004` — *James Cook vs. Hodnet* (Inspected PDFs `147_1766_004_0001.pdf`–`0003.pdf`):**
   - Earliest `Cook` chancery file in Prince Edward Co. (3 pages): subpoena issued 22 Oct `[1766]` (`6th year of our Reign` [George III]) by clerk `John LeNeve` to summon `Chs. Knight` to testify `"on behalf of James Cook in a certain Matter of controversy between the sd Cook & Ayon [?Aron] Hodnet"`; executed by `Fran: Watkins ds`.

4. **Prince Edward Co. `147-1788-005` (*William Cook vs. Samuel Matthews ETC*) & `147-1800-008` (*William Cooke vs. Samuel Matthews*) (Inspected `147_1788_005_0002.pdf`, `147_1800_008_0002.pdf`):**
   - Subpoena 24 Aug 1799 (`147-1788-005`) against `Samuel Matthews & Joseph Webster`; Bill of Complaint (`147-1800-008`) by **`William Cooke`** stating that before 1769 `Samuel Matthews` contracted to sell a tract of land in Prince Edward Co. to `Joseph Webster`, made his will in 1769 (bequeathing the money to his 5 daughters), Webster cancelled the contract, Matthews's executors sold the tract to `James Weekly`, who gave it to his son `William Weekly`, who sold `"at least Sixty Acres of the said Tract"` to `William Cooke`, who sues to perfect legal title.

5. **Prince Edward Co. `147-1807-005` — *Christopher H. Holland vs. John Morgan ETC* (Inspected `147_1807_005_0002.pdf`–`0003.pdf`):**
   - Bill sworn 25 July 1800 (`Holland vs. Morgan and Guill`): `Christopher H. Holland` was security for **`Reubin Guill`** *(son of Alexander Guill Sr.)* in a tobacco debt (~£20) to `John Morgan`. No Cook involvement.

6. **Amelia Co. `007-1773-010` — *John Cooke SR vs. David Irvine* (Inspected `007_1773_010_0003.pdf`):**
   - Crucial migration statement in Bill of Complaint: **`"your Orator John Cooke Senr. now of the County of Pittsylvania & late of this County of Amelia"`** states that about **1760**, his son **`Benjamin Cooke`** purchased a horse from **`David Irvine of the County of Bedford`** for £12 current money, and Irvine later sued Benjamin in **Cumberland County Court** and also sued John Cooke Sr. in Amelia County.

7. **Amelia Co. `007-1774-008` — *George Lazer Webster vs. John Cook* (Inspected `007_1774_008_0003.pdf`):**
   - Injunction bill stating that in 1760 `George Lazer Webster` owed `John Cook` £5 current money, levied by Sheriff `Ben: Ward` and later re-levied by Sheriff `Richard Jones`.

8. **Cumberland Co. `049-1771-004` (*William Cabell SR vs. John Cook*) & `049-1785-012` (*Stephen Cook ETC vs. Beverley Randolph ETC*):**
   - `049-1771-004` (`049_1771_004_0002.pdf`): Summons dated 27 June, 11th year of George III (1771) for `John Cook` to answer bill of `William Cabell`.
   - `049-1785-012` (`049_1785_012_0002.pdf`): Separate answer of `Beverley Randolph` to **`Stephen Cook and Johanna Sandefur`**, executor and executrix of `Abraham Sandefur` (who took assignment 19 Dec 1779 of `Bartholomew Dandridge`'s 21-year lease on two Appomattox River tracts in Cumberland Co.).

9. **Halifax Co. `083-1808-013` — *Heir(s) of John Cook vs. Heir(s) of John Cook* (Inspected `083_1808_013_0002.pdf`–`0003.pdf`):**
   - Bill filed by `Henry Cook`, `Edmund Cook`, `Richard Clayborne` (widower of `Polly Cook`), `John Cook`, `Elizabeth Cook`, `Jarratt W. Cook`, and `James Chambers` (guardian of `Richd. Cook`, `Rebecca Cook`, `Sally Cook`, `Patsy Cook`), children/heirs of **`John Cook decd.`** (died intestate in Halifax Co.), against `Nancy` (wife of `Isaac Medley`) and `Unity` (wife of `Robert Carter`), for division of personal estate (1799–1808; related to `083-1824-021` & `083-1824-022`).

## 6. Synthesis & Negative Evidence Summary for Generation 9 (George Cook, 1734–1785)

1. **George Cook's 3 Nov 1750 Amelia (Prince Edward) Patent (`Patents 29:316–317`)** is confirmed verbatim to be a **40-shilling treasury-right patent** (not headrights) for **400 acres on the head branches of Harris's Creek**, bounded solely by **Charles Hudson's Line** (`Patents 18:44–45`, `E 41° N`) on the north/northwest and **Thomas Williamson's Line** (`Patents 28:212–213`) on the east/southeast.
2. **Separation of Contemporaneous George Cooks in LVA Records:**
   - **Spotsylvania Co. George Cook(e)** (`Patents 21:145–146`, 12 Feb 1742/43; `Patents 21:584`, 25 Nov 1743) + **Culpeper PSC** (`George Cook`) and **Caroline PSC** (`George Cooke`) belong to the Rappahannock/Northern Piedmont region, distinct from Prince Edward/Amelia.
   - **Pittsylvania Co. George Cook** (`Grants P:455–456`, 30 Mar 1785, 44 acres on Upper Double Creek via 1782 Treasury Warrant assignment) and 1809/1827 Halifax/Pittsylvania petition token matches represent the Pittsylvania/Halifax cluster (where Amelia's `John Cooke Sr.` had already moved by 1773 per `007-1773-010`).
3. **Pre-1750 Southside Origins Context:** No `Cook`/`Cooke` patented in Amelia County before George Cook in Nov 1750. Prior to 1750, treasury-right `Cook`/`Cooke` patentees in the Appomattox/Southside corridor appear in **Goochland/Cumberland** (`William Cook` 430a Great Guinea Creek, 1740; `John Cook` 400a Green Creek, 1740; `John Cooke` 368a Green Creek, 1749), **Prince George** (`Richard Cooke` 350a Moccosoneck Creek, 1728; `Robert Cooke` 237a Buckskin Creek, 1733), **Henrico** (`Jacob Cook` 310a, 1745; `Robert Cook` 42a, 1746), **Surry**, **Isle of Wight**, and **Brunswick**.
4. **Chancery & Petition Negative Evidence:** LVA's surviving Prince Edward (`1754–1913`), Amelia (`1747–1913`), and Cumberland (`1764–1912`) chancery suites and Revolutionary Public Service Claims contain **zero** records for George Cook of Prince Edward/Amelia and **zero** suits involving Harris's Creek land disputes with Alexander Guill.
