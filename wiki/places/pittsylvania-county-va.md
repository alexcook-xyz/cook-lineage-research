# [Pittsylvania County, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pittsylvania_County,_Virginia "Virginia's largest county by land area, formed in 1767 from Halifax County along the Dan River borderlands with its seat at Chatham."): Jurisdictional & Archival Guide

- **Formation:** Created 1767 from Halifax County.
- **County Seat:** Chatham, Virginia.
- **Key Watercourses:** Sandy Creek, Dan River, Banister River, and Pigg River.

---

## 1. Relevance to the Cook Lineage

Pittsylvania County is the primary ancestral seat of Generations 6, 7, and 8 from the American Revolutionary era through the 1836 Kentucky migration:
- **Generation 8:** George Cook Sr. ("the Testator") acquired 97 acres on Sandy Creek from Electious Musick Sr. in 1780 (`DB 6:34–35`), acquired a 100-acre Sandy Creek tract from George Cook Junr. and wife Ruth on 9 August 1783 (`DB 7:106–107`), secured a 44-acre grant in 1785 (`Grants P:455`), and died testate in 1835 (`WB 1:293`).
- **Generation 7:** George Cook ("George B") died intestate before October 1831 owning 277 acres adjoining John Carter.
- **Generation 6:** George W. Cook was born circa 1813 in Pittsylvania County and inherited his share as an infant heir (`DB 33:101`; `DB 39:242, 256–257`).
