## Transcription
Muse read printed pp. 365-368. They hold (a) Thomas Blankenship to John Seay, September 1768, witnesses William Offill, George Morton, Rial Bowman, court 19 Sep 1768; (b) Theodrick Carter Junr to Waddill Carter, 15 Aug 1768, 272 acres, St Patrick's Parish; (c) John Rutledge to Dudley McDearman, 15 Jun 1768, 23 acres. No George Cook, Alexander Guill, 18 Apr 1768 or Harris's Creek appears on these pages.
