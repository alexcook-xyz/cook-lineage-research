# Cook Patrilineal Lineage Research Roadmap: Kentucky to Colonial Immigration

Tracking epic: [#32](https://github.com/alexcook-xyz/cook-lineage-research/issues/32). Phases map to milestones M1–M5.

This roadmap sets out the archival and methodological steps required to complete, verify, and document every link in the patrilineal ancestry of the living subject (Generation 1) back to the immigrant ancestor who arrived in British North America.

Every generational argument follows the **Genealogical Proof Standard (GPS)**:
1. Reasonably exhaustive research across all extant public and church jurisdictions.
2. Complete, accurate source citations (*Evidence Explained* / *Chicago Manual of Style*).
3. Critical correlation and analysis of primary, direct, and indirect evidence.
4. Resolution of conflicting evidence with explicit documentary proof.
5. A soundly reasoned, written conclusion.
