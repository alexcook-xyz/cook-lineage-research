## Transcription
Folio 27 opens: "Pursuant to an order of the Vestry of Camden Parish bearing date the 23d day of February 1776 for appointing Processioners it is Ordered ..." (folio 25 carries the 23 Feb 1776 vestry).
Verbatim of the Cook line: "Ordered that Thomas Black William Young Benjamin Cook Gideon Rucker William Eastis & William Jamison do Procession all the Pattented land from the mouth of Snow Creek up south sides of the said Creek to the head thereof and to the Ridges &c and that they return unto the Vestry an Account of every persons land in the said bounds ..."
No Musick, Holloway, Flippin, Echols, Collie, Hedgpeth on folios 26-30 other than as stated in the log.
