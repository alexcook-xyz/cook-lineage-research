## Citation
Pittsylvania County, Virginia, marriage bonds, consents and ministers' notes, 1767-1787 (loose papers), images in "1767-1787 Pittsylvania County Marriage Licenses," *Internet Archive* (https://archive.org/details/1785_20250812 : accessed 28 Nov 2026), files 1767_1769.pdf (21 pp.), 1770_1771.pdf (18 pp.), 1773_1776.pdf (16 pp.), 1777_1780.pdf (42 pp.), 1781_1782.pdf (21 pp.), 1783_1784.pdf (29 pp.), 1785.pdf (47 pp.), 1787.pdf (52 pp.). Item added 12 Aug 2025; the item description says "Scans of Pittsylvania County Virginia Marriage Licenses. There is not a database yet." No creator or holding repository is stated in the item (not verified; presumably the Pittsylvania County Clerk's loose marriage papers, Chatham). Cite pages as "PDF p. n" of the named file; the pages carry no printed numbers.

## Transcription
No Cook, Cooke, Cock or Koch (groom, bride, bondsman, surety, consenting parent or witness named in the part of each page read) found in any of the eight files. Names of the type that could be confused with Cook, read and excluded: "James Cox" (surety with Richd. Murrell, bond dated 26 Jan 1776, 1773_1776.pdf p. 15); "Drury Cross" (1787.pdf p. 9). No bride named Ruth seen. No groom or bride surnamed Pruett; Prewitt-type names are in [[S-pitt-marriage-bonds-ia-1777-1778-prewitt-variants]].

Bonds for the 1767-1787 span are in the item as: 1767-1769, 1770-1771, 1773-1776, 1777-1780, 1781-1782, 1783-1784, 1785, 1787. There is no file for 1772 and none for 1786 (not searched; file does not exist in the item). Some 1773-1776 file pages are dated 1777 (a 1777 bond sits in 1773_1776.pdf p. 13).

## Notes
- Method: every page rendered at 90 dpi and the top ~58% of each page (the bond preamble naming the bondsmen, and the "Whereas ... between" clause naming the couple) was viewed; the lower ~42% (signatures, witnesses, clerk endorsements) was NOT viewed on most pages. A Cook who appeared only as a witness or signer in the lower part would be missed. Pages viewed whole or partly by crop at higher resolution: 1773_1776 pp. 15-16 (200 dpi top), 1777_1780 pp. 5, 7, 10 (170 dpi).
- Many pages are fragmentary, torn, faint or rotated (e.g., 1767_1769 pp. 7, 9, 10, 13; 1770_1771 pp. 7, 8, 11-13; 1783_1784 p. 11). Names on those were read only as far as legible.
- The item's own OCR (djvu text) is garbage for handwriting and was not used.
- Negative result is limited to the part of the page read, and to what survives; a missing bond does not prove no marriage.
