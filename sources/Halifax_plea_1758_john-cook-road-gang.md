## Citation
Layer 1: Halifax County, Virginia, Plea Book 2 (court orders 1755-1759), p. 318, order of 17 March 1758, appointing Adam Winders surveyor of the road from Boyds Ferry to the Cow Ford.
Layer 2: Ann Brush Miller, *Halifax County Road Orders 1752-1767*, Historic Roads of Virginia series, VTRC 26-R33 (Charlottesville: Virginia Transportation Research Council, March 2026), entry dated 17 March 1758 "HCPB 2, p. 318"; index lists "Cook, John, 33"; PDF at https://rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/89319/dot_89319_DS1.pdf (accessed 2026-11-28).
Layer 3: original Halifax County Plea Book 2, County Court Clerk's Office, Halifax; Library of Virginia microfilm Halifax reel 54.

## Transcription (abstract as printed by Miller; not the original text; original image not seen)
17 March 1758, HCPB 2, p. 318
"Adam Winders is appointed Surveyor of the Road from Boyds Ferry to the Cow Foard & it is ordered that he with Richard Echols & his Tithables, William Carley, John Cook, James Vaughan, Richard Vaughan & his Hands, Richard Vaughan junr., Charles Vaughan, Hugh Currin, Moore Matlock, John Northern, Samuel Bently, Henry Strange, Micajah Estes, Richard Ashby and his Tithables, William Coleman, Elisha Estes, John Estes, Thomas Wynne, and Michael Vaughan be his Gang ..."

## Notes
- Informant: the court clerk's order; Miller is a modern abstractor who says she transcribed road orders only. Whether Miller reproduces spelling exactly is undetermined.
- The man named is John Cook. Relationship to George, Josiah, or Zachariah: undetermined. No inference made.
- Neighbor/FAN names in the gang are listed above (south side of Dan River near Boyds Ferry).
- The Miller volume's index (checked in the full text) has one Cook entry only (John, p. 33) and no Cooke, Zachariah, Josiah, or George Cook.
