## Notes

- The head's relation is written **"Mother"** — this enumerator wrote "Mother"/"Father" for heads (see dwellings 10 and 12). The FamilySearch and Ancestry indexes normalise it to Head/Self; the sheet word governs.
- **Correction to the M-1 pass report:** the pass reported "Supervisor's District No. 2"; the sheet header reads Supervisor's District No. **3**. The image governs.
- George's father's birthplace is written "Ky". This conflicts with the father's own lifetime record (George Sr.'s 1860 census statement, Pittsylvania, VA, per `people/gen-05-george-william-cook.md`) and matches the known hearsay pattern in George William's 1935 death certificate (informant: his young wife). Carried to analysis as a conflict item; it does not affect the household identification (name, age 28 ≙ b. 1852, mother Eliza, siblings).
- Eliza's age 51 implies birth about 1828–29, consistent with the c. 1828 used in the repo.
- Daughters Liddie (17), Laura (13) and Mattie (9) are new household members for the repo's family structure — sisters of George William, Thomas H. and John, not previously listed in the gen-05 file's summary of the will (the will names only the sons in the passages summarised there).
