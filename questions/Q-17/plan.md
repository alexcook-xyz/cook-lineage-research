# Plan: Q-17 — Direct parentage link, George Cook (Prince Edward/Amelia, Gen 9) → George Cook Sr. (Pittsylvania, Gen 8)

## Objective
Research question (quoted from issue #17): "Can we elevate the Generation 8 -> Generation 9 linkage from its current ~55% working hypothesis to >90% verified proof by locating an explicit documentary parentage statement or shared property conveyance between George Cook of Prince Edward County (d. post-1771) and George Cook Sr. of Pittsylvania County (d. 1835)?"

Done-when (issue acceptance criteria): a direct primary document (will, intestate administration, deed of gift, or sworn deposition) connecting George Cook (husband of Sarah) directly to George Cook Sr. of Pittsylvania; a GPS proof summary added to `people/gen-08-george-cook-sr.md`.

## Known facts (from repo, to be re-sourced)
- George Cook Sr.: will proved 21 Sep 1835 (Will Book 1:293–294; FamilySearch microfilm #007642302, restricted); names "Sarah & his children" in index.
- Sandy Creek, Pittsylvania: 1780 purchase from Electious Musick Sr. (DB 6:34–35) and 1783 sale to George Cook Sr. by George Cook Jr. and Ruth (DB 7:106–107).
- George Cook of Prince Edward: sold to Lovel Wilkerson 6 Apr 1771 (PE DB 5:67–68).

## Hypotheses
1. **H1 (working):** George Cook of Prince Edward/Amelia (Gen 9) is the father of George Cook Sr. of Pittsylvania (Gen 8).
2. **H0 (null, required):** No documentary link; the Cook men are unrelated or the link is unprovable from records found. A null result is resolved only after every group below is searched or blocked with no hit (R2).
3. **H2 (alternate):** George Cook Jr. (Pittsylvania, 1780–1783 grantee) is the Gen 9 man or a sibling, not a son of George Cook Sr.; to be tested by the same records.
