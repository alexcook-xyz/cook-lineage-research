# Internet Archive (Archive.org), HathiTrust, and Colonial Virginia Periodical Sweep: Generation 9 — George Cook(e) of Amelia & Prince Edward Counties, VA (1734–1785)

**Scope & Methodology:** Exhaustive sweep of Internet Archive (`archive.org`) via `advancedsearch.php`, `services/search/beta/page_production/?service_backend=fts`, `fulltext/inside.php`, and direct `_djvu.txt` full-text downloads, supplemented by HathiTrust (`catalog.hathitrust.org` / `babel.hathitrust.org`), FamilySearch Catalog, and Library of Virginia (LVA) catalog checks. Every hit cited below was verified against the underlying Archive.org item metadata, inside-book FTS highlights, or downloaded OCR text (`_djvu.txt`).

## 1. Executive Summary

1. **No Unindexed Record of Gen. 9 George Cook(e) in Digitized Archive.org/HathiTrust Abstracts:** Across all open and borrow-restricted Virginia county histories, marriage compilations, colonial council journals, statutory collections, land patent abstracts (available volumes), and historical quarterlies checked on Archive.org and HathiTrust, **zero entries** directly document George Cook(e) of Amelia and Prince Edward Counties (1734–1785) on Harris's Creek, and **zero entries** resolve the disposition of the remaining **250 acres** of the 400-acre Harris's Creek patent.
2. **Critical Coverage Gaps Verified on Archive.org & HathiTrust:**
   - **Bradshaw (1955):** Herbert Clarence Bradshaw's *History of Prince Edward County, Virginia* (Richmond: Dietz Press, 1955) is **not** digitized on Archive.org and is **not** open-view on HathiTrust (copyrighted).
   - **McConnaughey Amelia Series:** Gibson Jefferson McConnaughey's *Amelia County, Virginia Deed Books 1–12*, *Will Books 1–2*, *Court Order Books*, and *Tithables* are **not** on Archive.org (`numFound=0`) or open on HathiTrust. Only *Historical Notes on Amelia County, Virginia* (1982, borrow-restricted on Archive.org as `historicalnoteso0000unse_k2s7`) is available for inside-book FTS.
   - **Prince Edward County Deed Books (1754–1785) & Order Books (1754–1785):** **Zero** original Prince Edward Deed Books or Order Books—and zero T.L.C. Genealogy Prince Edward Deed/Order abstract volumes—are digitized on Archive.org or open on HathiTrust. Original microfilms/digital images reside at **FamilySearch** (e.g., DGS `008153028` for Deeds 1754–1771; Order Books 1754–1904, often restricted to FamilySearch Centers/Affiliate Libraries) and the **Library of Virginia (LVA)**.
   - ***Cavaliers and Pioneers* Gaps:** Archive.org holds **Vols. 1, 2, 3, 5, and 7** (borrow-restricted, FTS-searchable), plus 1929–1934 public-domain Vol. 1 installments. **Volume 4 (1732–1741)** and **Volume 6 (1749–1761, Patent Books 29–34)** are **not** on Archive.org.
   - ***Executive Journals of the Council of Colonial Virginia*:** Vols. 1–5 (1680–1754) are open on Archive.org; Vol. 6 (1754–1775) is **not** on Archive.org.
3. **Essential Disambiguation of Other Pre-1780 "George Cook(e)" Individuals in Virginia:** Archive.org FTS and Crozier's *Spotsylvania County* abstracts prove that the **George Cook who patented 400 acres in Spotsylvania County on 12 Feb 1742/43** (*Cavaliers and Pioneers*, Vol. 5) lived in St. George's Parish, Spotsylvania County with wife **Sarah** (1743–1744) and later wife **Mary** (1782–1783), and is **not** George Cook of Amelia and Prince Edward Counties. Five additional distinct colonial/pre-1780 George Cook(e) individuals (1617 Warwicksqueake servant; 1736 Prince William/King George inventory returner; 1751–1782 Germanna *Koch*; 1753 Norfolk minor grandson; and 1643 New England Capt. / 1768 English M.P.) are likewise documented and ruled out.
4. **Substantive Contextual Matches Found on Archive.org:**
   - **1758 Amelia County Militia (*Hening's Statutes at Large*, Vol. 7, p. 201):** `John Cooke` and `Shem Cooke` served together in Capt. Henry Anderson's Amelia County company alongside `James Lockett` and `William Hudson`.
   - **Amelia & Prince Edward Marriage Bonds (*WMQ* Vols. 16–17; Knorr 1950):** Document contemporaneous `John`, `James`, `Rains` (m. `Elizabeth Williamson`, 1784), `Thomas`, `William`, `Sylvia`, and `Conny` Cook(e) marriages, plus seven `Guill` marriages (including `Alexander Guill`'s probable daughter Elizabeth in 1790).
   - **Harris's Creek Patent Cluster (*Cavaliers and Pioneers*, Vol. 5; *Southern Kith and Kin*, Vol. 1):** Documents the neighboring Amelia/Prince Edward Harris's Creek patents (1745–1749) of Abraham Venable, Edward Harris, Thomas Lockett, Thomas Williamson, Edward Gresham, and Richard Phillips, and Alexander Guill's 1768 Prince Edward purchase and 1811 will (*Gill Abstracts*, 1939).

## 6. Task 2: Digitization Status of Prince Edward County Deed & Order Books (1754–1785) & Remaining 250 Acres

### Verification of Holdings
1. **Archive.org:** **No** original Prince Edward County Deed Books (1754–1785) or County Court Order Books (1754–1785) are digitized on Archive.org. Likewise, T.L.C. Genealogy's published abstracts (*Prince Edward County, Virginia, Deed Book 1 [1754–1759]*, *Deed Book 2 [1759–1765]*, etc.) are **not** on Archive.org.
2. **HathiTrust (`babel.hathitrust.org`):** Holds no open-view digitized Prince Edward County Deed Books or Order Books (1754–1785); only Burrell (1922), Knorr (1950), and family-specific compilations appear in catalog search.
3. **FamilySearch & Library of Virginia (LVA):**
   - **Deeds:** Original Prince Edward County Deed Books beginning with **Deed Book 1 (1754–1759)**, **Deed Book 2 (1759–1765)**, **Deed Book 3 (1765–1771)** (e.g., FamilySearch DGS `008153028`), and subsequent volumes through **1785** (Deed Books 4–7), plus *General Index to Deeds, 1754–1916*, are microfilmed and digitized in the **FamilySearch Catalog** under `Virginia, Prince Edward — Land and property` (some image groups restricted to FamilySearch Centers or Affiliate Libraries) and held on master microfilm at the **Library of Virginia** (accessible on-site or via ILL).
   - **Court Orders:** *County Court Order Books, 1754–1904* (starting with Order Book 1, May 1754) and *General Index to County Court Orders, 1754–1904* are likewise cataloged at **FamilySearch** (`Virginia, Prince Edward — Court records`) and **LVA**.
   - **LVA Online Indexes:** LVA's **Chancery Records Index** includes early Prince Edward *Cooke* equity files (e.g., *William Cooke vs. Samuel Matthews*, 1800-008), and LVA's **Virginia Land Office Patents and Grants** database holds the original patent book scans for the 1749–1761 gap not yet on Archive.org (*Cavaliers and Pioneers* Vol. 6).

### Implications for the Remaining 250 Acres of the 400-Acre Harris's Creek Patent
- Web-indexed secondary abstracts confirm only the **April 1768** Prince Edward County Court acknowledgment of a deed from **George Cooke** to **Alexander Guill** and Alexander Guill's later 1811 Prince Edward will.
- The disposition of the **remaining 250 acres** of George Cook's 400-acre Harris's Creek patent (as well as the exact Patent Book date/reference for the 400-acre grant itself) **cannot** be resolved from Archive.org or HathiTrust holdings. Specifically:
  - The 250-acre conveyance in *Southern Kith and Kin* Vol. 1, p. 181 (`Prince Edward D.B. 5, p. 358`, 9 May 1774) is **Thomas Lockett to Alexander Bass** (250 acres on the north side of Harris's Creek from Lockett's own patent), **not** George Cook's 250 acres.
  - Resolving the remaining 250 acres requires direct inspection of:
    1. **LVA Virginia Land Office Patents and Grants** (to retrieve the exact patent date, Patent Book/page, and metes-and-bounds of George Cook's 400-acre Harris's Creek patent).
    2. **Prince Edward County Deed Books 1–7 (1754–1785)** via FamilySearch (DGS `008153028` et seq.) or LVA microfilm—checking both George Cook(e) as grantor/grantee and adjoining Harris's Creek landowners (Venable, Harris, Lockett, Williamson, Gresham, Phillips, Guill, Brightwell, Pettus, Hudson).
    3. **Prince Edward County Court Order Books (1754–1785)** (for deed acknowledgments, road/tithable orders, and estate administration ~1784–1785).
    4. **Prince Edward County Land Tax Lists (1782–1786)** at LVA (which record annual acreage charges and transfers when George Cook's 250-acre balance leaves his name).

## 7. OCR & API Verification Caveats

- **18th-Century Typography & Long-s OCR:** Digitized `_djvu.txt` layers for Hening's *Statutes* (`statutesatlargeb07virg`) contain significant OCR distortion (e.g., `p. 201` read as `301`; `AMELIA COUNTY` read as `JlMELLl L'uuxrr`; `PRINCE-EDWARD COUNTY` read as `FJaXCE-EDWARD COUXTY`; `Shem` read as `Shein`). All Hening and periodical hits were verified by surrounding text context rather than bare keyword counts.
- **Handwritten / Manuscript OCR Failure:** The B.A. Chapman Amelia County notebooks (`b.-a.-chapman-collection-notebooks-amelia-county-2-of-3`) have unusable OCR and no hOCR/Abbyy search index; visual reading of the PDFs is required.
- **Archive.org Search API Behavior:** `advancedsearch.php` searches item metadata only; full-text searches require `services/search/beta/page_production/?service_backend=fts`. Inside `page_production`, terms not joined by explicit `AND` can match permissively across multi-page books, so every FTS hit reported here was checked against verbatim `highlight` snippets or downloaded `_djvu.txt`. Borrow-restricted items (`access-restricted-item: true`) block `inside.php` (HTTP 403) and `_djvu.txt` downloads, so only `page_production` FTS snippet verification is possible for those volumes.
