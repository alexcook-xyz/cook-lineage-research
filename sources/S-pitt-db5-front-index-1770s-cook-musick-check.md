## Citation
Pittsylvania County, Virginia, Deed Book 5, contemporary manuscript index at front of volume (alphabetical by first letter, grantee surname then "from" grantor, then page); item db5_20260520, leaves 3, 4, 5 (https://archive.org/download/db5_20260520/page/n3_w1600.jpg, n4, n5), accessed 28 Nov 2026.

## Transcription
Leaf 3, section C: "Cook from Ernn 208"; "Cook from Walaropc [=Waldrope] 209"; neighbours "Corbijn from White 170", "Chadwell from Stone 213", "Cooley from Fuller 94", "Crenshaw ...". No other Cook line in the C section. Leaf 4, section M: McKenie from Burton 19; McMurdey; Maddin; Matthews; Motley; Moorfield; Mullins; Markham; Mitchell from Walker 119; Montgomery Church; "McKinsey from Cook 163"; Minnis; Minters; Murphey; Morgan; Maqbe; Murray; McMillians; Motley; Morton; Moody ... (no Musick/Music/Mewsick line). Leaf 5: "Senter from Cook 160"; "Smith from Duncan 158" etc.
## Notes
- Index lists each deed under the grantee only in most cases; Harmon Cook's 1779 sales appear under the buyers. No George Cook, Elijah Cook or Musick entry. Index is a derivative aid for DB 5 only (about pp. 1-542). Names read at 1553 px width; page cuts at left edge of leaf 5 hide initial letters of some P/O lines.
