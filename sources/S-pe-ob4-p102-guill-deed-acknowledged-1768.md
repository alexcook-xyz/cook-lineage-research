## Citation
Prince Edward County, Virginia, Order Book 4: 102, court held 18 April 1768, entry for a deed from George Cook to Alexander Guill acknowledged and ordered recorded; County Court Clerk's Office, Farmville; image, *FamilySearch* (DGS 7897268, Orders 1-4, per Muse checkpoint 2026-11-27). ## Transcription
Page 102 heading: "April Court 1768"
"At a Court held for Prince Edward County April 18th 1768 / Present Jno Nash Esqr, Jno Nash Junr, Joel Watkins, Peter Le Grand, Henry Watkins, Nathl Venable, Gentlemen Justices"
Entry (verbatim as read): "A Deed from Gorge Cook to Alexander Guill was acknowledged by the said George ordered that the same be recorded"
Note the clerk's two spellings in one line: "Gorge" [read; first letters unclear, possibly "Geroge"] then "George".

## Notes
- Gives the Guill grantee, the date, and that George Cook personally acknowledged. Acreage, price, and place are not stated here; those come only from DB 3:366-368, cited secondhand (sweep file) and not yet verified against an image by this searcher.
