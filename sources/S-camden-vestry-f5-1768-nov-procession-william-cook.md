## Citation
Camden Parish (Pittsylvania County, Virginia), Vestry Book 1767-1852, vol. 1, folio (5) (clerk's number; first line of folio 4 is the continuation of the vestry of Nov 1768 whose header is on folio 3); image, *Internet Archive*, item camden-parish-vestry-book-1 (https://archive.org/details/camden-parish-vestry-book-1 : accessed 28 Nov 2026), file Camden_Parish_Vestry_Book_1767-1852_Vol_1_Pages_001-025.pdf, scan p. 5 of 25 in file Pages_001-025. ## Transcription
Folio 3 header: "At a Vestry held for Camboen Parish at Pittsylvania Courthouse the ... day of November. 1768 for Laying the Parish Levy" (year read as 1768 at 150 dpi; about 90 percent sure; reading as 1769 rejected because folio 6 opens a vestry of 14 July 1769 after these orders).
Folio 5 (verbatim of the line concerned): "Robert Hill Thomas Jones Joshua Barton and William Cook is appointed to Possession all the Pattent Land from the mouth of [word struck out] Chesnutt Creek up Pigg River on booth sides to the head thereof and to the Ridges &c"
Same folio lists, not Q-15 names: Thomas Bennett, William Bobbit, William Lawson, William Justice, Tully Choice, John Kirby Jun, Benjamin Potter, Amos Richardson, Lewis Jenkins, Benj Jenkens, John Law, Isham Hodges, Samuel Patterson, Lewis Morgan, John Cox, John Watson, William McDaniel, George Lumpkin and others. No Cook other than William; no Musick, Holloway, Flippin, Echols, Collie, Hedgpeth on folios 4-6.
