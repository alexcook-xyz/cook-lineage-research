## Citation
Pittsylvania County, Virginia, "Grantor Index to Deeds, Trust Deeds, Liens, Etc." (Cott Family Name Index, AC-50, 1934 format), letter M, ledger p. 36, surname "Musick," two entries dated of record 15 Aug 1780 and 18 Apr 1781; County Court Clerk's Office, Chatham; image (PDF page 110 of 115 in the "grantor-M" file), *Internet Archive* (https://archive.org/details/grantor-index-june-1765-1889 : accessed 28 Nov 2026). Index only; the deeds themselves (Deed Book 6:34 and 6:93) were not seen through this source.

## Transcription
Columns: date of record / surname / given names / grantee / kind of instrument / vol. / page. Verbatim, including the typed lines:

| Date of record | Surname | Given names (grantor) | Grantee | Instrument | Vol. | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aug 15 1780 | Musick | Electious Sr | George Cooke Jr | B & S | 6 | 34 |
| Apr 18 1781 | do | (Elexicous / Elixicous or [sic, as typed: "(Elexicous" on one line, "Elixicous or" on the next]) | John Johnson | B & S | 6 | 93 |

The 1781 given-name cell is typed on two lines: "(Elexicous" above "Elixicous or". Whether a closing parenthesis was intended is not clear.
Entries sit between "Musgrove" (last 1886) and "Mustain (Land Patent) 1756 / Oct 15 1782"; red rule lines above and below the Musick group.

## Notes
- Informant: the indexer (1930s compilation from the deed books); not a party. Typed index, legible at 110 dpi. Surname run Muse, Musgrove, Musick, Mustain; no other Musick/Music/Muzick line exists in the grantor index.
- "Date of record" is the recording date. The repo's anchor deed (DB 6:34-35) is dated 2 Mar 1780 in its text, so recording on 15 Aug 1780 is consistent but the recording date has not been checked against the deed image's endorsement.
- Same volume/page matches the repo file `sources/S-pittsylvania-db6-p34-35-musick-to-cooke-1780.md` (DB 6:34).
- The 1781 grantee John Johnson matches a "John Johnson 304 Abram Musick" row in the 1783 land alterations list ([[S-pitt-land-alterations-1783-vagenweb]]); that is a lead only, not established by this index.
