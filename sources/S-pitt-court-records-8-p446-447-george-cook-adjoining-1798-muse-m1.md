## Transcription
The entry reports commissioners acting "In obedience of an order of the Court of Pittsylvania of August 1777": "adjoining the said of Nimrod Scott, James Woody, George Cook, Thomas Hardy and Pleasant Shields, which was the freehold of the said William Harrison in his lifetime". Muse reads this as a 450-acre dower allotment on upper Double Creek, Camden Parish.
