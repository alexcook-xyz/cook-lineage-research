# Primary Evidence Archive Guide

Every file inside `evidence/` is an original courthouse scan, state vital certificate, military draft card, obituary clipping, or official patent/grant image. Secondary vendor family trees are never stored here as proof.

---

## Folder Directory

### 1. [`ky-modern/`](ky-modern/) — Modern Kentucky Records (Generations 1–5)
- `Arlin Cook Obituary.jpg` — *Columbia News-Statesman* (Dec 1991), Sunday 15 Dec 1991 death, age 56.
- `Elsie Cook Obituary.jpg` — *Campbellsville News-Journal* (Feb 1986), Friday 28 Feb 1986 death, age 51.

- `Adair_1935_DeathCert_18658.png` — KY Death Certificate #18658 (16 Jul 1935), George William Cook, b. 26 Feb 1852.

### 2. [`ky-adair/`](ky-adair/) — Adair County, Kentucky Courthouse (Generations 5–6)
- `Adair_DBZ_p194-195_EXACT.png` — Deed Book Z:195 (19 Feb 1877), George W. Cook to Thos. H. Cook, 2/6 (`1/3`) share of father George Cook's 142 acres on Russell's Creek.
- `Adair_WBM_p133.png` & `Adair_WBM_p134.png` — Will Book M:133–134, will of Eliza E. Cook (dated 8 Oct 1900, proved 1 Jan 1906) naming Thomas H., John D., and George W. Cook.
- `Adair_WBM_p7.png` — Will Book M:7, will of uncle James Cook (18 Oct 1890 / 1 May 1893).

### 3. [`va-pittsylvania/`](va-pittsylvania/) — Pittsylvania County, Virginia (Generations 6–8)

- `Pittsylvania_DB39_p242.png` — Deed Book 39:242 (2 Nov 1836), Power of Attorney from adult brothers George and Henry Cook of Cumberland County, KY, to brother James Cook of Adair County, KY.
- `Pittsylvania_DB39_p256-257.png` — Deed Book 39:256–257 (10 Nov / 19 Dec 1836), deed confirming George and Henry were infants when the 1831 deed was made.

- `george-cook-jr-deed-clark-1812-db18-p68.jpg` — Deed Book 18:68 (1810/11, proved 17 Aug 1812), George & Ruth Cook to John Clark, witnessed by a second George Cook.

### 4. [`va-colonial-lva/`](va-colonial-lva/) — Colonial & Piedmont Virginia (Pre-1780 & Generation 9)
- **Spotsylvania Co. (`1742–1748`):**
  - `Spotsylvania_OB_p166-167_1742.jpg` — Order Book (4 May 1742), Stubblefield & Sartin deeds acknowledged to George Cook.
  - `Spotsylvania_OB_p236-237_1743.jpg` — Order Book (6 Sep 1743), **George Cook and Sarah his wife** acknowledge sale to Henry Lewis.
  - `Spotsylvania_OB_p466-467_1748.jpg` — Order Book (7 Jun 1748), George Cook runaway-slave claim immediately above **Elexius Musick** (later of Sandy Creek, Pittsylvania Co.).
- **Amelia & Prince Edward Cos. (`1750–1789`):**
  - `LVA_Patents29_p316.png` & `LVA_Patents29_p317.png` — Patents 29:316–317 (3 Nov 1750), 400 acres on Harris's Creek to George Cook (`Thomas Lee P.`, adj. Hudson & Williamson).
  - `Amelia_OB2_p370-371_RoadOrder_1751.jpg` & `Amelia_OB2_p372-373_GeorgeCook_1751.jpg` — Amelia Order Book 2 (Sep 1751), Rolling Road crew across Vaughan's Creek naming `George Cook` beside Peter Coffee and William Mills (clerk separates him from Sheriff `Abraham Cock`).
  - `PE_Vestry_img73_GeorgeCook_Processioning_1760.jpg` — St. Patrick's Parish Vestry Book, p. 62 (4 Jun 1760), land processioning naming `George Cook` beside Harris's Creek neighbors Edward Grisham & Lockett (`PE_Vestry_img66_JohnCook_Processioning.jpg` shows a separate John Cook).
  - `PE_OB2_1760_Tithables_GeorgeCook.jpg` & `PE_OB2_1760_Levy_Excused_GeorgeCook.jpg` — Prince Edward Order Book 2 (9 Sep 1760), `George Cook one` added to [tithables](# "Colonial Virginia poll-tax unit (1620s–1780s): any free male, indentured servant, or enslaved person aged 16+, for whom the head of household paid annual colony, county, and parish taxes.") beside Peter Coffee, and `George Cook Excused from paying County Levy`.
  - `PE_OB2_p130-131_Buchanan_v_Cook_Dismissed_1762.jpg` & `PE_OB2_p244-245_Speirs_v_Cook-Campbell_1763.jpg` — Merchant debt suits (`1762–1763`).
  - `PE_DB2_Simmons_to_Spencer_1765_deed.jpg` & `_memo.jpg` — Deed Book 2:277 (13 Feb 1765), George Cook witnesses Charles Simmons deed.
  - `PrinceEdward_1768_Cook_to_Guill_Deed.png` & `_p367-368.png` — Deed Book 3:366–368 (18 Apr 1768), 150 acres of the 1750 patent sold to Alexander Guill.
  - `PE_OB4_p101-102_Guill_Deed_Acknowledged_1768.jpg` & `PE_OB4_p109-110_Sarah_Dower_Release_1768.jpg` — Acknowledgment (`18 Apr 1768`) and dower release (`20 May 1768`) by **`Sarah the wife of George Cook`**.
  - `PE_OB4_p229-230_Pleasants_v_GeorgeCooke_1769.jpg` — Pleasants debt suit (`Aug 1769`), bailed by William Mills & Peter Coffee; George fails to appear.
  - `PE_DB3_p445-446_Guill_to_Buchanan_1769.jpg` & `_p447-448.jpg` — Guill's 3 Oct 1769 trust deed confirming his tract lay *"adjoining to George Cooke"*.
  - `PE_DB5_p67-68_Cook_to_Wilkerson_1771.jpg` — Deed Book 5:67–68 (6 Apr / 16 Sep 1771), George Cook sells remaining **250 acres** (`"patent bearing date the third day of November MDCCL [1750]"`) to Lovel Wilkerson.
  - `PE_WB2_p42-43_Sarah_Cook_Will_1764_1785.jpg`, `PE_PPTax_1787_Alexander_Guill.jpg`, `PE_PPTax_1789_John_William_Cook.jpg` — Post-1771 Prince Edward collateral checks.
- **Pittsylvania Early Grant (`1780`):**
  - `LVA_GrantsA_p403.png` & `LVA_GrantsA_p404.png` — Grants A:403–404 (12 Jun 1780), 261 acres on Sandy Creek to Josiah Cook (`Thomas Jefferson`).
