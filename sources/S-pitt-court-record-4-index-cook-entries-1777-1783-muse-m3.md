## Transcription
Index page, C section (image 8), as written, with the page number in the right column. Cook lines: "Cook v [Dalton or Ballon]" 100; "Same Appd [?] v Morrison" 100; "Cook v Tears" 100; "Cook for a Mill" 116; "Cook v Crist" 116; "Same v Same" 116; "Same v Same" 116; "Cook v Simmons" 119. Also on image 9 (read by Muse only): "Cooke appd Guardian of Lucy's [?]" 138; "Cook v Ward" 159. Other C lines on the page (Cox v Clever 98, Callaway v Payne 98, Cox v Clever 118) are not Cook. The index gives surnames only.
No Musick line (any spelling) was seen on index images 7-16 (A to S).

## Notes
- Retrieved by Muse on 2026-11-28 (request M-3, user-run). Checked against a screenshot only by Claude, 2026-11-28. Image 8 was compared with Muse's reading; Muse's lines for image 9 (the entries at pp. 138 and 159) were not checked against an image.
- No first names, so the index does not say which Cook (George, Elijah or William) is meant. "Cook for a Mill" (p. 116, probably a petition to build a mill) and the suits are the first sign that a Cook was active at the Pittsylvania court in 1777-1783. The entries are not dated in the index.
- The order pages (pp. 100, 116, 119, 138, 159) were not opened. Image-to-page calibration was not found: the opened spreads (images 65, 70-74) show May Court 1778 and 28 July 1778 with no printed page numbers.
- The absence of a Musick entry is a weak negative: the volume's index lists court business only, and Electious Musick may have gone to Henry County before 1780.
- Court-order indexes for 1767-1904 (Films 33304-33307, DGS 8191463-8191466) are viewable only at a FamilySearch Center.
