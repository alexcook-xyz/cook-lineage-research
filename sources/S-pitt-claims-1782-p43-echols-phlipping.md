## Transcription
Page 43 (read at 100 dpi): "To Abadiah Echols for the damage of a horse impressed into Continental Service . . . 3. 10." and, immediately after, "To Jos. Phlipping for 30 lb Bacon @ 1/. 6 Bushels Corn @ 15/. 500 lb fodder @ 5/. 6 1/2 Bushels Oats @ 2/. 1/2 Bushel Meal 1/6. forrage 3/. 3 Dietts @ 1/. impressed for Continental use . . . 4. 13. 6." Other claimants on the page: Robert Rogers, John Worsham, Ambrose Fitzgerald, John Waller, Hez[?] Kelley, George Peak, Jeremiah Simpson, Sherwood Loney (as read).
