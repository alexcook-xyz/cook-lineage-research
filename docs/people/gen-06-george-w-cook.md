# Generation 6: George W. Cook (c. 1813 – by Sep 1873)

- **Lineage Position:** 3rd Great-Grandfather / Generation 6 ("The Virginia Bridge")
- **Lineage Proof Status:** **Proven**
- **Direct Father:** [George Cook ("George B," d. 1831)](gen-07-george-cook-b.md)
- **Mother:** Catharine (maiden name under investigation; Gen 6 maternity unassigned)
- **Spouse:** Eliza E. Cole (1828–1905), married c. 1850 in Kentucky.

---

## 1. Vital Chronology & Geography

- **Birth:** circa 1813, Pittsylvania County, Virginia.
- **Migration:** Pittsylvania County, VA → Cumberland County, KY (c. 1834–1836) → Adair County, KY (by 1850).
- **Death:** by September 1873, Adair County, Kentucky (interstate estate administration; wife Eliza heads 1873 tax household).
- **Land:** 142 acres on Russell's Creek, Adair County.

---

## 2. Primary Records & Archival Plates

1. **1860 Federal Census (Adair County, KY, p. 200, dw. 727):**
   - Head of household: `George W. Cook`, age 47, farmer, born **`PITTSYLVANIA VA`**. Wife `Eliza`, age 32, born KY; son `George W.`, age 8.
   - Sibling correlation: Brother `James Cook`, age 56, born `PITTSYLVANIA VA` living nearby.
2. **1870 Federal Census (Adair County, KY, p. 11B, dw. 39):**
   - Head of household: `George Cook`, age 58, farmer, born `VIRGINIA`. Wife Eliza age 42, son George W. age 18.
3. **1836 Cumberland County, Kentucky Power of Attorney (Pittsylvania DB 39:242):**
   - Preserved in `evidence/va-pittsylvania/Pittsylvania_DB39_p242.png` (Plate 9 in paper).
   - Dated 2 Nov 1836: `George Cook` and `Henry Cook` of Cumberland Co., KY empower *"our Brother James Cook of Adair County"* to convey their shares in 227/277 acres in Pittsylvania County, VA descending to them *"as heirs at law of George Cook Deceased"*.
4. **1836 Pittsylvania County Follow-up Deed (DB 39:256–257):**
   - Preserved in `evidence/va-pittsylvania/Pittsylvania_DB39_p256-257.png` (Plate 10 in paper).
   - James Cook conveys to James Nance; recites that when the 1831 deed was made, George and Henry Cook *"were infants"*.
5. **1877 Adair County Heirship Deed (DB Z:195):**
   - Preserved in `evidence/ky-adair/Adair_DBZ_p194-195_EXACT.png` (Plate 5 in paper).
   - Recites 142 acres owned by father George Cook at the time of his death.

---

## 3. Father-to-Child Link

Proved conclusively by the 1836 Power of Attorney (Pittsylvania DB 39:242) and reciprocal deed (DB 39:256–257) connecting George Cook of Cumberland/Adair County, KY directly as heir-at-law to George Cook deceased of Pittsylvania County, VA.
