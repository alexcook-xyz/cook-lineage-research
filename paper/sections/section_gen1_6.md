## Methodological Scope and Evidentiary Standards

This section establishes the patrilineal descent of the living subject (Generation 1; personal details withheld) backward across six generations to **George W. Cook** (born circa 1813, Pittsylvania County, Virginia; died by September 1873, Adair County, Kentucky). Every parent–child link is evaluated against the five elements of the Genealogical Proof Standard (GPS): (1) reasonably exhaustive research, (2) complete and accurate source citations following *The Chicago Manual of Style* (notes and bibliography) and *Evidence Explained*, (3) analysis and correlation of independent evidence, (4) resolution of conflicting evidence, and (5) a soundly reasoned, coherently written conclusion.[^1]

To maintain strict documentary integrity, this section distinguishes throughout between **original images and verbatim transcriptions directly inspected during this review** and **secondary or uninspected citations carried forward from the project's prior working papers**—specifically the 25 November 2026 working proof paper and the Second-Pass peer-review audit.[^2] Earlier workspace drafts containing fabricated vital dates, invented Kentucky death certificate numbers, and non-existent numerical Adair County deed and will books (`DB 3:116`, `DB 4:214`, `WB G:342`—Adair County uses lettered books `T`, `X`, `Z`, and `M`) have been quarantined and explicitly refuted where necessary.[^3]

## Reverse-Chronological Proof Arguments (Generations 1 → 6)

### Generation 1 → Generation 2: Living subject to living line member

The subject of this study and his father are living persons. Their vital dates, places, parents, and identity records are withheld from this public section. The father-to-child link is supported by records held privately by the project. Researchers who need that evidence may request it through a GitHub issue.

### Generation 2 → Generation 3: Living line member to Arlin Cook (1935–1991)

The Generation 2 man is the son of **Arlin Cook** (born 24 August 1935, Adair County, Kentucky; died 15 December 1991, Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky) and **Elsie Mae Hunt** (born circa 1934–1935; died 1986, Campbellsville, Taylor County, Kentucky). His own birth and personal records are withheld as he is living. The family's local archive preserves two primary newspaper obituary clippings that anchor Arlin and Elsie's dates of death and community locations with calendar precision:

1. **Arlin Cook's 1991 Obituary:** A clipping headed *"COLUMBIA — Arlin Cook, 56, died here Sunday. His wife, Shelia, survives. Funeral, 11 a.m. Tuesday, Grissom Funeral Home. Visitation after 2:30 p.m. Monday."*[^8] A birth on 24 August 1935 places Arlin at age fifty-six on **15 December 1991**, which fell on a **Sunday**, confirming the clipping's day-of-week and age statement. An earlier First-Pass draft that gave a different death date and certificate number is quarantined as an error.[^9]
2. **Elsie Cook's 1986 Obituary:** A clipping headed *"CAMPBELLSVILLE — Elsie Cook, 51, died here Friday. Funeral, 2 p.m. Sunday, Parrott & Ramsey Funeral Home."*[^10] At age fifty-one, Elsie's Friday death accords with Friday, **28 February 1986**. An earlier First-Pass draft that placed her death in 2017 under a non-existent memorial is quarantined as an error.[^11]

The Generation 2 → 3 link therefore rests on privately held family records together with these public obituaries, without material conflict.

### Generation 3 → Generation 4: Arlin Cook (1935–1991) to Russell Cook (1916–1982)

**Arlin Cook** is the son of **Russell Cook** (born 28 November 1916, Adair County, Kentucky; died 8 January 1982, Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky). Direct inspection of Russell Cook's original World War II Selective Service Registration Card (`Russell Cook WWII Draft Card .png`, D.S.S. Form 1) provides primary, self-reported proof of Russell's identity, birth date, birthplace, residence, and maternal household:[^13]

- **Registrant:** `Russell Cook`, Serial Number `1836`, Order Number `1578`, residing at `Columbia, Adair, Kentucky`.
- **Age & Birth:** `23` years of age, born **`Nov. 28, 1916`**, in **`Adair Co., Kentucky`**.
- **Employment:** `Goff's Furniture Store`, `Columbia, Adair, Kentucky`.
- **Next of Kin:** **`Mrs. Anna Cook`**, Relationship: **`Mother`**, residing at `Columbia, Adair, Kentucky`.
- **Signature:** `Russell Cook`.

This single original federal record accomplishes two critical proofs. First, at age 23 with a 28 November 1916 birth date, Russell registered in the initial October 1940 draft (shortly before turning 24) and explicitly confirmed his mother as `Mrs. Anna Cook` of Columbia—exposing an earlier First-Pass draft error that fabricated Russell's birth date as `"10 May 1916"` and erroneously inverted his wife `Anna B. Sneed` into his mother.[^14] Second, as documented in the 25 November 2026 working paper, Arlin's own parentage is independently established across three federal and state record systems (uninspected directly as scans in this review): his Social Security **Numident** record naming father **`Russel Cook`** and mother **`Anna B. Sneed`**; the **1935 Kentucky Birth Index** recording his 24 August 1935 Adair County birth to mother **`Annie Sneed`** (indexed under the phonetic aspiration **`Harlin Cook`**); and the **1940 and 1950 U.S. Censuses** of Adair County, placing Arlin (age 5) in his mother Anna's household in 1940 and (age 14) in his divorced father Russell's household in 1950.[^15]

> **Segregated Methodological Note on Maternal Surname (*Sneed* vs. *Hunt*):**  
> While Arlin Cook's patrilineal descent from Russell Cook is conclusively established across Social Security, vital index, and census records, a minor maternal surname discrepancy exists in the family's non-patrilineal holdings and should be noted transparently for future collateral work. Both the Social Security Numident and the 1935 Kentucky Birth Index record Arlin's mother as **Anna B. Sneed** (`Annie Sneed`), whereas a family tree image preserved in the local archive (`Freeman Hunt Profile Photo.jpg`, an oval porcelain gravestone portrait of an older couple) is associated in family notes with the **Hunt** surname.[^16] (Notably, Elsie Mae's maiden surname was *Hunt*, and a **John H. Sneed** served as one of the two witnesses at the 1911 marriage of Russell's parents in Adair County.[^17]) Reconciling whether *Sneed* and *Hunt* reflect a remarriage, step-parentage, or tree-label crossover on Anna Bell's maternal line requires pulling her marriage record to Russell Cook and her own birth/death records, without affecting the proven patrilineal Cook chain.

### Generation 4 → Generation 5: Russell Cook (1916–1982) to George William Cook (1852–1935)

**Russell Cook** is the son of **George William Cook** (born 26 February 1852, Adair County, Kentucky; died 16 July 1935, Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky) and **Emma Anna / Enna Janes** (1894–1980). Direct visual verification of two primary Adair County records, correlated with Russell's 1940 draft card and Find a Grave cemetery documentation, establishes this link beyond doubt:

1. **1911 Adair County Marriage Bond, License, and Certificate (`Adair_1911_Marriage_Cook_Janes.png`, Vol. 22, pp. 490–491):**
   - *Marriage Bond (p. 490):* Executed at Columbia, Adair County, on **26 September 1911** for $100 by `George W. Cook` as principal and `J. T. Barbee` (signing `J. T. Barbee Jr`) as surety, naming the intended bride **`Enna Janes`**, attested by Clerk `Walker Bryant` by `L. O. Taylor`, D.C.[^18]
   - *License Particulars (p. 491):* Groom **`George W. Cook`**, residence `Adair County`, age **`58`**, number of marriage **`1st`**, occupation `Farmer`, birthplace `Adair Co.` (parent fields left blank). Bride **`Enna Janes`** (also read in later records as `Emma Janes`), residence `Adair Co.`, age **`17 yrs`**, number of marriage **`1st`**, birthplace `Adair County`, father and mother **`Wesley Janes`**, `Adair Co.`, with remarks `"bride's parents consenting in writing"`, to be married at `"Brides Home"` on 27 September 1911, signed `G W Cook` on 26 September 1911.[^19]
   - *Marriage Certificate (p. 491):* Solemnized on **27 September 1911** between `George W. Cook` and `Enna` [or `Emma`] `Janes` at `Wes Janes` in Adair County by `J. G. Smith`, in the presence of **`John H. Sneed`** and **`Maggie Richards`**.[^20]
2. **1935 Kentucky Certificate of Death No. 18658 (`Adair_1935_DeathCert_18658.png`, Registered No. 2000):**
   - *Identity & Dates:* **`George William Cook`**, white married male, farmer, residing in `N. Columbia` voting precinct, Adair County (`83 yrs.` length of residence), born **`1852 - 2 - 26`** (26 February 1852), died **`July 16, 1935`** at 6 a.m., age **`83 Years, [—] Months, 20 Days`**.[^21]
   - *Medical & Burial:* Attended from May 1935 to 16 July 1935 by `Thos. M. Russell, M.D.`, of Columbia; principal cause of death **`Intestinal tuberculosis`**. Buried at **`Cook G Yard`** (`July 16, 1935`), undertaker `M. L. Grissom` of Columbia; filed 17 July 1935 by Registrar `Mrs. S. P. Miller`.[^22]
   - *Parentage & Informant:* Birthplace `Ky`; father **`George Cook`** (birthplace recorded as `Ky`); mother's maiden name **`Cole`** (no given name; birthplace `Ky`); informant **`Mrs. G. W. Cook`**, `Columbia Ky`.[^23]
3. **Corroboration from Russell's Draft Card, Cemetery Records, and Sibling Vital Records:**
   - Russell Cook's WWII Draft Card confirms his birth on **28 November 1916** in Adair County and names his mother as **`Mrs. Anna Cook`** of Columbia—aligning with `Emma Anna Janes Cook` (1894–1980), George William's 1911 bride.[^24]
   - **Find a Grave Memorial `#96831073`** (Cook Cemetery, Columbia, Adair County) records `George William Cook` (`26 Feb 1852 – 16 Jul 1935`, *"Son of George W. Cook and Eliza Cole"*), spouse `Emma Anna Janes Cook` (`1894–1980`), and children **`Russell Cook` (`1916–1982`)** and **`James Robert Cook` (`1920–1949`)**.[^25]
   - Prior project citations record the 1916 Kentucky Birth Index entry for Russell Cook (28 November 1916, mother `"Anna Jeans"`), the 1920 and 1930 Adair County censuses showing Russell in George and Anna Cook's household, and the July 1949 Indiana death certificate and *Shelbyville News* obituary of brother James Robert Cook naming parents George and Anna (Janes) Cook of Columbia and surviving brother Russell.[^26]

Together, the 1911 first-marriage declaration, the 1935 death certificate, the 1940 draft card, and the collateral sibling records eliminate earlier First-Pass draft fabrications (which invented a `"12 Feb 1852 – 10 Feb 1935"` life span under `"File 2728"` and shifted James Robert Cook's dates to `"1913–1971"`).[^27]

### Generation 5 → Generation 6 ("The Bridge"): George William Cook (1852–1935) to George W. Cook (c. 1813–by Sept 1873)

**George William Cook** is the son of **George W. Cook** (born circa 1813 in Pittsylvania County, Virginia; died by September 1873 in Adair County, Kentucky) and **Eliza E. Cole** (born 8 May 1828; died December 1905, Adair County, Kentucky). Because the 1935 death certificate named his father only as `"George Cook"` and his mother only as `"Cole"`, bridging Generation 5 to Generation 6 rests on a tight convergence of original Adair County deed and probate records, federal census schedules, and collateral family records.

#### 1. Direct Proof from Original Adair County Courthouse Scans

- **1877 Heirship Deed — Adair County Deed Book Z, p. 195 (`Adair_DBZ_p194-195_EXACT.png`):**  
  Executed on the **`19th day of February 1877`** (not 17 February 1877 as reported in the 25 November 2026 paper), **`George W. Cook`** conveyed to **`Thos. H. Cook`** for **`Three hundred & fifty six Dollars`** ($356):  
  > *"two undivided interests or shares in the land owned by my father George Cook at the time of his death Containing 142 Acres in the whole tract Lying & being in Adair County Ky on Russells Creek being 2/6 or 1/3 of said tract of land hereby conveyed One undivided interest being the share that descended to me as heir at law of my father & the other being the share conveyed to me by the party of the second part."*[^28]  
  Signed **`George W. Cook`**, with the clerk's certificate by `W. F. Neat` beginning at the foot of page 195. This instrument is primary, firsthand testimony by George William Cook himself naming his deceased father (`"my father George Cook"`), his brother (`Thos. H. Cook`), the exact Russell's Creek tract (`142 Acres`), and the fractional division (`2/6 or 1/3`, implying six inheriting shares among George W. Cook Sr.'s children).
- **1900/1906 Will of Eliza E. Cook — Adair County Will Book M, pp. 133–134 (`Adair_WBM_p132-133start.png` & `Adair_WBM_p133.png`):**  
  Executed by mark on the **`8th day of October 1900`** and proved at the January Term on **`Jan 1st 1906`** upon the oaths of subscribing witnesses `Jas Garnett Jr` and `W. A. Coffey` (certified by Clerk `T. R. Stults`), the full original text on page 133 reads:  
  > *"I Eliza E. Cook of Adair County Kentucky make and publish this my last will and testament First I will and bequeath to my two sons Thomas H. Cook and John D. Cook, to be divided equally between them, all the live stock that I may own at my death, and all the money that I may have at my death, Second, I will and bequeath to my son George W. Cook all the remainder of my estate, real and personal, including my interest in the tract of land I now live on, Witness my hand this 8th day of October 1900."*[^29]  
  This original record explicitly ties all three surviving sons—**Thomas H. Cook**, **John D. Cook**, and **George W. Cook**—to their mother Eliza E. Cook and the family homeplace, matching her December 1905 *Adair County News* obituary quoted on Find a Grave Memorial `#121437185`: *"Died of pneumonia. Three children survive: George of Columbia, John of Dallas and Thomas of Illinois. Husband's death date from her obit in Adair County News."*[^30]

#### 3. Collateral Corroboration Tying George W. Cook (Gen 6) to Pittsylvania County, Virginia

Crucially, the Adair County records of George W. 1. **1860 Adair Census Sibling Birthplaces:** In the same District 1 enumeration of Adair County, **`COOKE JAMES`** (age 56, born **`PITTSYLVANIA VA`**, dwelling 312, p. 138 [orig. 46]) lives with `COOKE SUSANNAH` (56, `PITTSYLVANIA VA`), `CURRY GEORGE` (17, `CUMBERLAND KY`), `CURRY SUSANNAH` (9, `CUMBERLAND KY`), and `ROSS REBECCA` (21, `CUMBERLAND KY`), while **`COOK HENRY`** (age 50, born **`VIRGINIA`**, dwelling 702, p. 197 [orig. 105]) lives nearby with `MARIAH` (45, `ADAIR KY`) and `NANCY` (20, `CUMBERLAND KY`).[^36]
2. **1870 Inter-Household Kinship:** By 1870 (`c70_1870adairpart26.txt`, Leatherwood District, P.O. Curry`**, whom he also appointed executor.[^38] This instrument confirms unbroken household continuity from `CURRY GEORGE` (age 17) in James and Susannah's 1860 household through James's 1893 probate, while verifying the exact sibling triad—**James (c. 1804)**, **Henry (c. 1810)**, and **George W.
