Layer 1: "A List of Tithables Taken by Hamon Critz, Gent, for the Year 1767," Pittsylvania County, Virginia (original list date within 1767 not given on the page).
Layer 2: "First List of Tithables of Pittsylvania County, Year 1767," Pittsylvania County VAGenWeb (https://usgenwebsites.org/vagenweb/pittsylvania/towns/tith.htm : accessed 2026-11-28); page credits Maud Carter Clement, *The History of Pittsylvania County, Virginia* (Clement's page number not seen).
Layer 3: original list not seen; its repository undetermined.

## Transcription (as on the web page; spelling kept)
In the Critz list (after "Joseph Cameron", "John Pluk"): "Zacheriah Cook" followed by "Thos. Harbour". List ends "Haman Critz". Separate list "taken by Hugh Innes ... Anno. Dom. 1767" contains "William Cook" (between "Phillip Smith" and "John Fushon"). No other Cook on the page (checked by text search for "cook").

## Notes
- The tithable lists carry no first-person death information. Whether Zachariah was alive when this list was taken is undetermined; the tithable list date vs. the Sept 1767 "dead" order is not reconciled.
- No Zachariah entry carries a land or acreage note. Relationship to any other Cook: undetermined.
