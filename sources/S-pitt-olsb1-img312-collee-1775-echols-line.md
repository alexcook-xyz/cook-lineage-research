## Transcription
Heading as read at about 190 dpi: "25. March 1775 Surv.d for James Co[l?]lee 304 Ac of Land on the Branches of Sandy Creek and Bounded as follows (to wit) Beginning at William Twedwell Corner pointers and thence along Echols Line N 69 E 246 po. to a W.O. thence along Wynns line N 47 W 66 po. to pine N 80 po. to a pine N 49 W 56 po. to a W.O. in the said Line thence New Lines N 57 W 16 po. to Wm Owens Cor. pine thence along the same [Owens line] S 32 W 162 po. to a pine S 16 W 72 po. to pointers S 12 E 28 po. to the Beginning"
Plot label: "James [Co?lee] 304 Ac" (same hand).

## Notes
- **Uncertain name.** The surname is written "Co?ee": the middle letters are a loop and a tall stroke; readings "Collee", "Colkee", "Colbee" and "Cotlee" all fit the letter shapes. It is NOT established that this is Collie. The question's FAN list names a William Collie; this man is "James". A second reader is needed before any use.
- "Echols Line" (no given name) and "Wynns line", "Wm Owens" and "William Twedwell" are the neighbors. William Owen's own 1774 survey is on the same creek ([[S-pitt-olsb1-img249-owen-1774-musick-corner]]).
- No Cook, Musick or Holloway on this entry. No chain carriers are named in this book.
