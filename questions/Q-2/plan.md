# Q-2 Plan — Adair County Census Households, 1880–1930 (George W. Cook Collaterals)

## Objective

How do the collateral households of George William Cook (1852–1935), Thomas H. Cook, and John D. Cook track across Adair County between 1880 and 1930? (Issue #2, quoted verbatim.)

Purpose from the issue: corroborate the family structure in Eliza E. Cook's will (Will Book M, pp. 133–134) and verify that George William Cook remained continuously on the family's Russell's Creek home tract.

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** All three brothers head (or appear in) households in Adair County District 1/2 (Columbia / Russell's Creek) in every surviving census 1880–1930, and George William's household stays on or beside the Russell's Creek home tract throughout.
- **H2 (null):** One or more of the three is absent from Adair County, or outside District 1/2, in one or more census years (moved, missed, or died between enumerations).
- **H3:** The will's son is John **D.** Cook (repo reading) — against the alternative John **M.** Cook (2026-11-26 retrieval reading). Settled by the will image, then tested against the census households found.

## Jurisdictions

Adair County, Kentucky (formed 1801–02 from Green County; county seat Columbia) held all relevant records for the whole period. Census geography: Columbia magisterial districts / enumeration districts 1 and 2 and the Russell's Creek precinct; ED numbers change every census and are taken from each year's lead search, not assumed. The 1890 U.S. census schedules are destroyed — a coverage gap, logged as such, never as a negative result.
