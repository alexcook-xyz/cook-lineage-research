## Transcription
Page 208 (margin note, left): "Cook / from / Evnn [the contemporary index reads Ernn] / Power of / Attorney."
"Know all men by these presents that wee George Evnn [?] and Crestine Loddiany of Pittsylvania County and state of Virginia for divers good Causes and Considerations we hereunto Moving have made ordaind constituted and appointed our trusty friend Harmon Cook Gent of the same county & dominion of Virginia our true and lawfull attorney for us in our names and to our use to take, and [interlined: follow] pursue in the same manner &c as ourselves might or could do were we personally Present to ask Receive or demand the same to claim for any money due or contract to make or hasbeen made in our names and under receipt of the same Acquittance and other dicharges for us and in our names to make sine seal & deliver as also [interlined: one] attorney or attorney under him to subsitute or appoint and again at his pleasure, and further to do perform and finish for us and in our names all and singular thing or things which shall or may be necessary Touching and concerning the premises as fully throughly and entirely as we the said George Evnn and Cristiana Loddinney in our own persons ought or could do in and about the same Ratefying allowing and confirming whatsoever our said attorney shall Lawfully do or cause to be done in and about the said premises by virtue of these presents. In Witness whereof we have hereunto set our hands and seals this [day number overwritten, illegible] day of June one thousand seven hundred and seventy nine."
Signed: "Geo. Evnn L.S." / "Cristina X [her mark] Loddeany L.S." [last name spelled variously Loddiany, Loddinney, Loddeany on the page]
"Sign'd seal'd and Deliver'd In presence of us / Syrus L Roberts [reading of 'Cyrus L. Roberts' about 60%] / Abraham Evnn [?]" (bottom of the page image is cut at this line; further witness lines, if any, are not visible on this leaf).

Page 209, top: "Virginia At a Court held for Pittsylvania County the 20th day of July 1779 The above power of attorney from george Evnn and Cristina Loddiany to Hermon Cook was proved by the oaths of the Witnesses thereto, to be the act and deed of the said George & Cristina and the same was ordered to be recorded By the Court. Test Will. Tunstall Clk" Margin: "Examin'd & deliv'd".
Below it on p. 209 begins an unrelated-to-Cook-kin deed: John Waldrope Sen. and Hermon Cook, 6 Nov 1778 (see [[S-pitt-db5-p209-210-waldrope-to-harmon-cook-1778]]).

## Notes
- Image seen (original deed book), not just the index. Grantor surname is the uncertain item: first letters read Ev/Er; index also reads "Ernn". Alternatives Erwin/Ervin/Evans are NOT supported by the letter count; do not normalize. The question (George Cook or Musick link) is not served: neither a George Cook, Elijah Cook nor Musick appears on these pages. Harmon Cook is the attorney (grantee in the index).
- The index (leaf 3) lists this as "Cook from Ernn 208".
