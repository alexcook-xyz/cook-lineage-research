# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes researchers, descendants, archivists, and curious members of the public. We want it to be a courteous, evidence-based place to work.

## Our standards
- Disagree with evidence, not with people. Cite a record when you challenge a conclusion.
- Respect the living and the dead. Do not post details about living people. Treat enslaved, marginalized, and vulnerable ancestors with dignity, and use the names the records give.
- Credit others' work. Cite sources and contributors.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and unwelcome contact.

## Scope
This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement
Report concerns privately through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" option on the Security tab. Maintainers will review reports promptly and privately and may remove content or restrict participation. We aim to respond within 7 days.
