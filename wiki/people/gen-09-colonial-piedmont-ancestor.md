## Biographical Overview & Verified Chronology (1750–1771)

1. **3 November 1750 (*Virginia Land Patents No. 29, pp. 316–317*):** Granted **400 acres** in western Amelia County on the head branches of **Harris's Creek** for **40 shillings** (treasury right), adjoining **Charles Hudson's** 1738 Vaughan's Creek patent (*Patents 18:44–45*, `E 41° N`) and **Thomas Williamson's** 1747 patent (*Patents 28:212–213*, sold in 1749 to **Thomas Lockett III**).
2. **September Court 1751 (*Amelia County Order Book 2, pp. 371–372*):** Ordered with his male tithables to assist surveyor James Withers in clearing the *"old Roling Road"* across **Vaughan's Creek** to the mouth of Sawney's Creek alongside neighbors **Peter Coffee**, **William Mills**, **Charles Simmons**, and **Elkanah Jennings**. *(The clerk on p. 372 explicitly distinguishes `George Cook` from Sheriff `Abraham Cock`, ruling out surname confusion.)*
3. **9 September 1760 (*Prince Edward County Order Book 2*, Images 94 & 96, *DGS 7897268*):** Ordered added to the list of tithables as **`George Cook one`** (one white tithable in household, i.e., no resident sons aged 16+ in Sept 1760) alongside **`Peter Coffee`**, and in the same court term **`George Cook Excused from paying County Levy`**—typically granted for advanced age (c. 60+) or permanent infirmity.
4. **January 1762 – August 1765 (*Prince Edward Order Book 2, pp. 130–131, 245; Order Book 3, pp. 54–55*):** Debt suit by `Messrs. Archd. Buchanan, Jno. Bowman &c.` against `George Cooke` dismissed (Jan 1762, pp. 130–131); confessed judgment with co-defendant `William Campbell` to `Messrs. Alexander Speirs, John Bowman & Co.` for £5.10s.10d. (July 1763, p. 245); subsequent *scire facias* in August Court 1765 (*OB 3:54–55*) entered judgment against Campbell and *"dismissed as to the Deft George."*
5. **13 February 1765 (*Prince Edward Deed Book 2*, p. 277, *DGS 8153028* Images 256–257):** Witnessed a 290-acre Bush Creek deed from **Charles Simmons** (now of Bedford County) to William Spencer alongside **Elkanah Jennings** and **Joel Watkins**.
6. **18 April – 20 May 1768 (*Prince Edward Deed Book 3, pp. 366–368; Order Book 4, folios 102, 107, 110*):** Conveyed the southern **150 acres** of the 1750 patent (`S 10° W 180 p.`, `W 6° S 120 p.`, `N 3° W 178 p.`, closing `N 86° E 164 p.`) adjoining Lockett and Jones to **Alexander Guill** for £13 (*ack. 18 Apr 1768, OB 4:102*). At the same April/May session (*OB 4:107*), Obadiah Wright was ordered to pay `Georg. Cooke` 150 lbs tobacco for 6 days' witness attendance (*Wright v. Pleasants*). On **20 May 1768** (*OB 4:109–110*), **`Sarah the wife of George Cooke`** appeared in court, was privily examined, and relinquished her right of dower.
7. **August 1769 – June 1770 (*Prince Edward Order Book 4, pp. 229–230, 296–297; Deed Book 3, pp. 445–447*):** On 10 October 1766 he contracted a £12.16s. bond to `John Pleasants & Co.`; at August Court 1769 (*OB 4:229–230*) and June Court 1770 (*OB 4:296–297*, £30.14s. dischargeable at half, sureties **William Mills**, **Peter Coffee**, and **Henry Walker**), he failed to appear, leaving **June 1770** as his final Order Book entry (*Orders 1, 5, 6, 7 = 0 hits*). Meanwhile on **3 October 1769** (*DB 3:445–447*), Alexander Guill's trust deed to Neil Buchanan Jr. & Co. confirms his 150-acre tract still lay *"adjoining to George Cooke"*.
8. **6 April 1771 (*Prince Edward Deed Book 5, pp. 67–68*, Film `33225`, *DGS 8153025*, Item 3, Image 58; proved June & 16 September 1771):** **`George Cook of the county of prince Edward`** conveyed the remaining **250 acres** on the head branches of Harris's Creek—explicitly reciting **"with patent bearing date the third day of November MDCCL" [1750]** and **"all that tract of Land he the sd. George Cook now possesses and lives on"**—to **Lovel Wilkerson** for **£30**, witnessed by Henry Walker, William Anderson, Ben Johns, and Abram Venable (*no wife joining or relinquishing dower*).

## Remaining Research Questions (To Advance to "Proven Lineage Member")

1. **Gen 8 ← Gen 9 Migration Bridge (Post-September 1771):** Where did George Cook (and/or any minor male children born after Sept 1744) go after selling his last 250 acres on 16 September 1771? Exhaustive sweeps of LVA Chancery, *Publick Claims*, and *1782–1785 Heads of Families* confirm he vanished from Prince Edward. Testing whether he or his son settled on Sandy Creek in Pittsylvania County requires inspection of **Pittsylvania `Will Book 1:293–294` (Film #007642302)** and **1771–1785 Prince Edward / Halifax / Pittsylvania Court Order & Tax lists**.
2. **Pre-1750 Origin Test (`Spotsylvania County 1741–1748` + `Elexius Musick` FAN-Club Link):** **George Cook and wife Sarah** acquired 800 acres in St. George's Parish, Spotsylvania County (`1741–1743`, *Patents 21:145–146*; Stubblefield & Sartin deeds acknowledged **4 May 1742**, *Spotsylvania OB, pp. 166–167*), acknowledged sale of their 400-acre tract to Henry Lewis on **6 September 1743** (*Spotsylvania OB, p. 236*), and completed their Spotsylvania land liquidation by **4 March 1744[/45]** (*Spotsylvania DB D*). Newly recovered *Spotsylvania Court Order Book, p. 467* (**Tuesday, 7 June 1748**) proves **George Cook** was still in Spotsylvania County presenting a public claim for taking up a runaway slave—appearing on the **exact same court page (`p. 467`) immediately above `Charles Gordon and Elexius Musick`**. Remarkably, **`Electious Musick Sr.`** later migrated to **Sandy Creek in Pittsylvania County**, where he sold **97 acres** on Sandy Creek to **`George Cooke Junior`** on **2 March 1780** (*Pittsylvania DB 6:34–35*). Both the husband-and-wife match (**`George Cook` & `Sarah`** in Spotsylvania 1743 and Prince Edward May 1768) and the 1748 → 1780 **`Elexius Musick`** neighbor link make **Spotsylvania `Order Books (July 1748–1755)`** the highest-priority pre-1750 test: if George Cook vanishes from Spotsylvania between June 1748 and 1750 while Amelia `OB 1–2 (1735–Aug 1751)` catches his arrival, the migration link strengthens dramatically.
3. **Note on `Sarah Cook`'s Prince Edward Will (`WB 2:42–43`):** A will of `Sarah [her + mark] Cook of Prince Edward County` dated **1 February 1784** (close visual inspection confirms `1784`, not `1764`) and proved **17 October 1785** (admin. *c.t.a.* `William Mitchel`), bequeathing household pewter, cattle, and beds to daughters **`Conney Micel`** (who married Mitchell in March 1781 per Knorr) and **`Ester Michell`** (`"I give and [be]queth Ester Michell..."`), is recorded in *Prince Edward Will Book 2, p. 43*. No husband, sons, or land are named; whether this 1784/1785 Sarah Cook relates to George Cook's wife Sarah (living May 1768) or belongs to another Prince Edward Cook household remains unestablished.
