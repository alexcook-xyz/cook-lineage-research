## Transcription
Verbatim from the image; line breaks not preserved. `[ ]` = my uncertainty. The deed starts at the foot of p. 34, below an unrelated court endorsement, and ends mid-p. 35. Text above the deed on p. 34 (a memorandum of livery of seisin by Bryan Ward Norton to David Dalton, with a receipt of 10 Aug 1780 and a court note of 15 Aug 1780) belongs to another instrument and is not transcribed. After this deed's court endorsement, p. 35 begins another instrument (a sheriff's bond, Crispin Shelton), also not transcribed.

Margin, p. 34 (left of the deed's opening): "Cooke from Musick Deed"

p. 34: "This Indenture made the Second Day of March in the year of Our Lord Christ one thousand Seven hundred and eighty Between Electious Musick Senior of the one part and George Cooke Junior of the County of Pittsylvania and State of Virginia of the other part Witnesseth that the Said Electious Musick Senior for and in Consideration of the Sum of Seventy five pounds Current money of Virginia hath Bargained Sold Aliened Released and delivered unto the [interlined: above] Said George Cooke Junior his heirs and Assigns forever a certain Tract or parcell of Land lying and being in the above Said County of Pittsylvania and State of Virginia aforesaid Heard and Bounded in manner and form following to wit Beginning at a pine on a Ridge by Coll. [M?]ay[o?]'s place and Running South towards a branch of Sandy Creek Sixty four Degrees two Hundred and Seven pole to a pine thence west S Seven and one Half [?] thirty one pole to a pine thence Sixty Six West One Hundred and twenty two pole to a pine thence North Sixty five West [fifteen? bottom of p. 34 and first word of p. 35 read together]"

p. 35: "[fifteen?] pole to a pine thence along the dividing line North twenty two West One Hundred and Twenty five pole to the Beginning Containing Ninety Seven Acres more or Less with all Woods and underwoods Swamps Marshes Low Grounds Meadows Feeding and his due Share of all [Mines?] Mines and Quarries as well discovered as not discovered within the bounds aforesaid and being part of the Said Quantity of what belonged to the above Said Electious Musick and the Rivers waters and watercourses therein Contained together with the priviledges of Hunting fishing and fowling and all other Commodities whatsoever Hereditaments to the Same or any part belonging or in anyways appertaining To have hold possess and enjoy the Said Tract or parcell of Land and all other the before granted Premises and every part thereof with their and every of their appurtenances unto the Said George Cooke Junior his Heirs for ever In Witness whereof the Said Electious Musick hath hereunto Sett his Hand and affixed his Seale the Day and year above written"

Signatures, p. 35:
"William Collie
Elisha [his / f-shaped mark] Cooke
[Mark] Ben Echols [reading of surname: "Echols" probable; E-c/s-h-o-l-s]"
Right: "Electious [his / mark: a bracket-like stroke] Musick L.S. / Mark [and seal?]"

Court endorsement, p. 35: "At a Court held for Pittsylvania County the 15th day of August 1780 This Indenture of Bargain and Sale from Electious Musick to George Cooke was proved by the Oaths of the Witnesses thereto to be the Act and Deed of the Said Electious Musick and the Same was Ordered to be Recorded by the Court Test Will Tunstall Clk"

Left margin, p. 35, beside the court endorsement: "Examined" (also "Examined" beside the p. 34 endorsement). A further small marginal note at the left of the sheriff's bond is outside this deed.

## Notes
- Informant: the clerk copying the instrument into the deed book (derivative copy of the signed deed, made after the 15 Aug 1780 proof). The consideration, bounds, and parties were supplied by the parties in the original indenture. Book copy = original record for the county's recording purposes; the signed deed is not seen.
- Differences from the Muse findings file (comparison only):
  1. First corner: I read "a pine on a Ridge", not "a white Oak". The bounds contain no white oak that I can see.
  2. The neighbor at the first corner is legible in part: "Coll. [M?]ay[o?]'s place"; the first letters are partly blotted (ink blot over the second to fourth letters). The findings file called the name blotted or illegible. Not read with confidence.
  3. The findings file reports the grantor's name spelled both "Electious" and "Elicious". I could not find "Elicious" in the deed; I read "Electious" at every occurrence in the preamble, the recital, the testimonium, the signature, and the court note. Not every occurrence is equally clear.
  4. Two courses: the findings file left two middle bearings as partly legible. I read the legible words above; the second course ("west S Seven and one Half ... thirty one pole") is odd as a bearing and may be a clerk's error or a misread; marked [?].
  5. "Mark" annotation near the witnesses: the word "Mark" is written between Elisha's line and Ben Echols's line, nearer Echols. The findings file attributes the mark to Elisha (his mark, f-shaped stroke) and treats Echols as a plain signature. I read "his" and the f-shaped stroke on Elisha's line; whether the separate word "Mark" belongs to Elisha or Echols is uncertain.
  6. Other readings agree: parties, the 2 Mar 1780 date, 75 pounds Current money of Virginia, 97 acres, "State of Virginia", the witnesses William Collie, Elisha Cooke, Ben Echols (surname probable), and the court proof 15 Aug 1780 by the witnesses' oaths, Test Will Tunstall Clk.
- Name spellings as written: "Cooke" for the grantee throughout (court note: "Cooke"; margin: "Cooke"). Grantee described as Junior; the document states no relationship to any other George Cook.
- Name readings I am less than high on: "Echols" (medium); "Collie" (medium, final letters); "Coll. [M?]ay[o?]" (low).
- Image condition: clear, slightly low contrast (reversed microfilm style); lower corners of p. 34 and 35 fold shadows; no loss of text in the deed.
