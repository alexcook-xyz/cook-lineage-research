# Q-2 closing summary (for the PR body — issue #2 was deleted 2026-11-28, so no issue comment is posted)

**Question:** How do the collateral households of George William Cook (1852–1935), Thomas H. Cook, and John D. Cook track across Adair County between 1880 and 1930?

**Result — proven (scoped), after nine adversarial review cycles (cycle 9: pass):**

- George William Cook is present in the same part of Adair County at every enumeration 1880–1930 — Columbia (ED 1) with his mother Eliza in 1880; West Columbia with Eliza in 1900; head alone in 1910; head with wife Anna and son Russell in 1920; head with Anna, Russell and **James (a second son, b. ~1920–21, new to the repo)** in 1930. District-level continuity is proven; continuity on the Russell's Creek home tract itself is a probable inference from the will and the 1877 Deed Book Z conveyances, labelled as such.
- Neither Thomas H. nor John D. Cook appears in Adair County in any of the five censuses (county-wide Cook/Cooke surveys every year, all logged). Thomas has an unexamined White County, Illinois candidate (index leads only) — proposed as a future question. John's whereabouts from 1880 onward are unknown.
- Eliza E. Cook's recorded will (Will Book M, pp. 132–133, proved 1 Jan 1906) was retrieved and settles the middle initial: **John D. Cook** (two independent readings of the image).
- All seven record images (five census sheets, two will plates) are committed in `sources/ky-adair/`; transcriptions, analysis, proof and the full review history are in `questions/Q-2/`. James Cook and the census set were added to `people/gen-05-george-william-cook.md`.
