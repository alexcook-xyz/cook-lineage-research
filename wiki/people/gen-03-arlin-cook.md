# Generation 3: Arlin Cook (1935–1991)

- **Lineage Position:** Paternal Grandfather / Generation 3

- **Spouses:** 1) Elsie Mae Hunt (c. 1934/35 – 1986); 2) Shelia (surviving 1991)

## 1. Vital Chronology & Geography

- **Birth:** 24 August 1935, Adair County, Kentucky (recorded as *Harlin Cook* in Kentucky Birth Index).
- **Death:** 15 December 1991 (Sunday), Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky (age 56).
- **Burial:** Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky.

---

## 2. Primary Records & Archival Plates

1. **Newspaper Obituary:** *Columbia News-Statesman* (Dec 1991), clipping preserved in `evidence/ky-modern/Arlin Cook Obituary.jpg` (Plate 1 in paper): *"COLUMBIA — Arlin Cook, 56, died here Sunday. His wife, Shelia, survives. Funeral, 11 a.m. Tuesday, Grissom Funeral Home."* (15 Dec 1991 was a Sunday, matching age 56 precisely).
2. **Social Security Numident File:** Records Arlin Cook, b. 24 Aug 1935, father **`Russel Cook`**, mother **`Anna B. Sneed`**.
3. **Kentucky Birth Index:** Recorded under phonetic aspiration as `Harlin Cook`, b. 24 Aug 1935, Adair Co., mother `Annie Sneed`.
4. **Federal Censuses:** 1940 Adair Co. census (age 5, household of mother Anna); 1950 Adair Co. census (age 14, household of father Russell Cook).

---

## 3. Archival Traps Resolved

- **Phonetic Aspiration Trap (*Harlin* vs. *Arlin*):** Common Kentucky South-Central phonetic aspiration prefixed an "H" in the state birth index, whereas Social Security and lifelong adult records used *Arlin*.
- **Maternal Surname Discrepancy (*Sneed* vs. *Hunt*):** Vital index and Numident cite *Sneed*, while a family photo inscription cites *Hunt*. Tracked in GitHub Issue #1.
