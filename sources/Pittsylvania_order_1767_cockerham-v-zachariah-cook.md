## Transcription (verbatim as read; my reading, high confidence on "Zachariah Cook" and "dead")
Page head: "17" (page number, top left), "September Court 1767"
Margin: "Cockerham / vs / Cook / Abated"
Body:
"David Cockerham . . . Plaintiff
against } On a Petition
Zachariah Cook . . . Defendant
The Defendant being dead Ordered that this suit be abated."
(The given name is written "Zachariah"; the first letters look like "Zachariah" with a flourished Z. Confidence high but not certain on the "ch" letters.)

## Notes
- The exact day of September Court 1767 was NOT read. Page 11 is headed "August Court 1767"; page 13 onward "September Court 1767". Pages 12 and earlier day-header not located. The paper's "25 Sept 1767" date is therefore unverified by me.
- What this shows: Zachariah Cook was a defendant in a petition and was reported dead by the Sept 1767 court. It supports "dead by Sept 1767". It does not give a death date, an estate, an heir, or any residence, and does not show he was the man in Halifax records.
- Adjacent page 15 has a "Jenkins v. Cockerham" chancery, defendant Abner(?) Cockerham: different Cockerham, not read closely.
- Not examined: pp. 18-200 and later leaves for administration, inventory, or appraisal of Zachariah Cook (see log).
