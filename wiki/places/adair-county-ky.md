# Adair County, Kentucky: Jurisdictional & Archival Guide

- **Formation:** Created 1802 from Green County.
- **County Seat:** Columbia, Kentucky.
- **Key Watercourse:** Russell's Creek (tributary of the Green River).

---

## 1. Relevance to the Cook Lineage

Adair County served as the primary homestead of the Cook patrilineage for five generations:
- **Generation 6:** George W. Cook (c. 1813–1873) settled on 142 acres on Russell's Creek prior to 1850 after migrating through Cumberland County.
- **Generation 5:** George William Cook (1852–1935) lived his entire life in the North Columbia voting precinct and died on the Russell's Creek family farm.
- **Generation 4:** Russell Cook (1916–1982) was born and raised in Columbia, working at Goff's Furniture Store.
- **Generation 3:** Arlin Cook (1935–1991) was born in Adair County and died in Columbia.
