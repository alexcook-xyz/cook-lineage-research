## Transcription
p. 209 margin: "Cook from Waldrope Deed Exam'd." "This Indenture made this 6th day of november in the year of our Lord one thous and seven hundred and seventy eight Between John Waldrope sen^r and Hermon Cook both of Pittsylvania County, the said Waldrope does for himself &c bargain unto the sd Hermon Cook his heirs or assigns one certain tract or parcel of Land Lying and being on pigg river Beginning at a beech on the river Bank thence up the river to a rock called King Castle thence a streight course to the mouth of the frying pan creek ... containing 50 Acres more or less ..."
p. 210: signed "Jn^o his [mark] Waldrope L.S."; "In presence of James Mitchell / Dav^d his [mark] Wade / Stephen his [mark] Bennet". Memorandum of livery, same witnesses (James Mitchell, Stephen Bennet, Dav^d Wade). Receipt: "Rec^d of Herman Cook Three hundred pound in full of all demands for the Land the s^d Cook bought of me" signed John [mark] Waldrope, witnesses James Mitchell, David Wade, Stephen Bennet. Dower relinquishment: "I Anne Waldrope do give up all my right of Dower to the land my husband John Waldrope sold to Herman Cook ... 6th day of november 1778", witnesses (James Mitchell, Dav^d Wade ..., remainder cut at page foot).
