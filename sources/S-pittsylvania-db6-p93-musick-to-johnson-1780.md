Verbatim from the image; line breaks not preserved. `[ ]` = my uncertainty.

Margin, left of the deed: "Johnson from Musick Deed" ; at far left, rotated: "Examined"

Top of page (court endorsement, written ABOVE the deed): "At a Court held for Pittsylvania County the 17th Day of April 1781 The within Indenture was proved by the Oaths of three of the Witnesses thereto, to be the Act and Deed of the within named [John Mack? / Johnmack?] and Sarah his Wife and the Same was Ordered to be recorded by the Court Test Will Tunstall Clk"

Deed: "This Indenture made this 29th Day of July in the Year of Our Lord Christ one thousand Seven Hundred and eighty Between Elixeous [?] Musick of Henry County on the one part and John Johnson of Cumberland County of the other part Witnesseth that the said Musick for and in Consideration of the Sum of Two thousand pounds Virginia Currency to him in Hand paid by the said John Johnson the Receipt whereof the said Musick doth Hereby Acknowledge Hath given, granted Bargained and Sold and by these Presents doth give grant Bargain and Sell unto the said Johnson his heirs and Assigns forever One Certain Tract or Parcel of Land Situate lying and being in the County of Pittsylvania on Sandy Creek Containing by Estimation Ninety Seven Acres of Land be the Same more or Less and bounded as followeth, Viz, Beginning at a large pine Corner of said Musick Standing on the main Ridge about a Quarter of a Mile below Coll. Wynnes and running thence a Dividing line South twenty two Degrees East to a pine in Ash by [a?] line now James Collins [?] and running thence on his North Sixty five West two Hundred and eight poles to a pine thence North eight West three poles to a pine thence North fifty Degrees East one Hundred and sixty poles to a pine thence South Seven Degrees East thirty four poles to the Begining with all and Singular the Priviledges thereunto belonging or in any wise Appertaining unto the said John Johnson to have and to hold, Occupy, Possess and Enjoy the above granted land and Premises with all Appurtenances thereunto belonging or in any wise Appertaining and the said Elixeous [?] Musick doth for himself and his Heirs and Assigns Warrant and forever Defend the above mentioned Land and Premises unto the said John Johnson his Heirs and Assigns forever against the Lawfull Claim or Claims of all and every Person or Persons whatsoever In Witness whereof the said Elixeous [?] Musick hath set his hand and Seal this Day and Year first above written."

"Signed Sealed and Delivered / In Presence of / [R?] Williams, John Cox, John Dickinson / Haynes Morgan"  Signature: "Elixeous [?] [his] [mark: 5-shaped] Musick L.S. / mark"

Memorandum: "Memorandum that on the Day and Year within written full Quiet and Peaceable possession of Seisin was given and made by the within named Elixeous [?] Musick unto him the above[?] John Johnson according to the full Purport true Intent and meaning of the within Indenture. Signed Sealed and Delivered In Presence of [R?] Williams, John Cox, John Dickinson, Haynes Morgan" ; signed again "Elixeous [?] [his] [mark] Musick L.S. / mark"

## Notes
- Informant: clerk copying the signed deed into the deed book; terms supplied by the parties. Book copy; signed original not seen. Musick signed by mark.
- Given-name spelling: the same form recurs at every occurrence (preamble, testimonium, signature x2, memorandum). I read it as E-l-i-x-e-o-u-s, with the "x" and the following "e" the uncertain letters (alternative: "Elixcous"/"Elexeous"). It is NOT the "Electious" (c-t) form written on the same man's 1780 deed at DB 6:34-35; the middle letters differ in the hand. The Grantor Index typed "Elexicous / Elixicous". Confidence in "Eli/Ele-x-...-ous": medium; exact vowels: low-medium.
- Date discrepancies: the Grantor Index gives 18 Apr 1781 as date of record; the court endorsement reads 17 Apr 1781 (the "17th" is clear, with "th" superscript; year "1781" read with high confidence). The index date may be the date entered, or the index is off by one; not resolved.
- The endorsement names "the within named John Mack [?] and Sarah his Wife", which does not match the deed's parties (a single grantor, no wife). Read with medium confidence; possibly a clerk's formula error. Do not rely on the endorsement for a wife "Sarah".
- Acreage "Ninety Seven" (97) equals the acreage of the 2 Mar 1780 sale to George Cooke Jr. (DB 6:34-35, 97 acres). The bounds are written differently (here: calls S 22 E, N 65 W 208 p, N 8 W 3 p, N 50 E 160 p, S 7 E 34 p; DB 6:34-35: S 64 [deg] 207 p, W 31 p, 66 W 122 p, N 65 W 15 p, N 22 W 125 p), and both mention Coll. Wayne/Wynne's place. This is recorded as an observation only; no inference drawn here.
- Neighbors: "Coll. Wynnes" (Col. Wynne; also "Coll Wayns/Wayne" at DB 6:34); "James Collins" [medium-low; could be Collier]. Witnesses: [R?] Williams (first initial doubtful), John Cox, John Dickinson, Haynes Morgan.
- Musick is "of Henry County" on 29 Jul 1780, though DB 6:34-35 (2 Mar 1780) has him dealing in Pittsylvania; Johnson "of Cumberland County".
