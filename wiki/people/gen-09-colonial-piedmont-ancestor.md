## Biographical Overview & Verified Chronology (1750–1771)

1. **3 November 1750 (*Virginia Land Patents No. 29, pp. 316–317*):** Granted **400 acres** in western Amelia County on the head branches of **Harris's Creek** for **40 shillings** (treasury right), adjoining **Charles Hudson's** 1738 Vaughan's Creek patent (*Patents 18:44–45*, `E 41° N`) and **Thomas Williamson's** 1747 patent (*Patents 28:212–213*, sold in 1749 to **Thomas Lockett III**).
2. **September Court 1751 (*Amelia County Order Book 2, pp. 371–372*):** Ordered with his male tithables to assist surveyor James Withers in clearing the *"old Roling Road"* across **Vaughan's Creek** to the mouth of Sawney's Creek alongside neighbors **Peter Coffee**, **William Mills**, **Charles Simmons**, and **Elkanah Jennings**. *(The clerk on p. 372 explicitly distinguishes `George Cook` from Sheriff `Abraham Cock`, ruling out surname confusion.)*
3. **9 September 1760 (*Prince Edward County Order Book 2*, September Court 1760):** Ordered added to the list of tithables as **`George Cook one`** (one white tithable in household, i.e., no resident sons aged 16+ in Sept 1760) alongside **`Peter Coffee`**, and in the same court term **`George Cook Excused from paying County Levy`**—typically granted for advanced age (c. 60+) or permanent infirmity.
4. **January 1762 & July 1763 (*Prince Edward Order Book 2, pp. 131, 245*):** Debt suit by `Messrs. Archd. Buchanan, Jno. Bowman &c.` against `George Cooke` dismissed (Jan 1762, p. 131); confessed judgment with co-defendant `William Campbell` to `Messrs. Alexander Speirs, John Bowman & Co.` for £5.10s.10d. (July 1763, p. 245).
5. **13 February 1765 (*Prince Edward Deed Book 2*, p. 277):** Witnessed a 290-acre Bush Creek deed from **Charles Simmons** (now of Bedford County) to William Spencer alongside **Elkanah Jennings** and **Joel Watkins**.
6. **18 April & 20 May 1768 (*Prince Edward Deed Book 3, pp. 366–368; Order Book 4, pp. 102, 110*):** Conveyed the southern **150 acres** of the 1750 patent (`S 10° W 180 p.`, `W 6° S 120 p.`, `N 3° W 178 p.`, closing `N 86° E 164 p.`) adjoining Lockett and Jones to **Alexander Guill** for £13. On **20 May 1768** (*OB 4:110*), **`Sarah the wife of George Cook`** appeared in court, was privily examined, and relinquished her right of dower.
7. **August & October 1769 (*Prince Edward Order Book 4, p. 229; Deed Book 3, pp. 445–447*):** On 10 October 1766 he contracted a £12.16s. bond to `John Pleasants & Co.`; at August Court 1769 (*OB 4:229*), he failed to appear, triggering a conditional judgment against his appearance sureties **William Mills** and **Peter Coffee**. On **3 October 1769** (*DB 3:445–447*), Alexander Guill's trust deed to Neil Buchanan Jr. & Co. confirms his 150-acre tract still lay *"adjoining to George Cooke"*.
8. **6 April 1771 (*Prince Edward Deed Book 5, pp. 67–68*, proved June & 16 September 1771):** **`George Cook of the county of prince Edward`** conveyed the remaining **250 acres** on the head branches of Harris's Creek—explicitly reciting **"with patent bearing date the third day of November MDCCL" [1750]** and **"all that tract of Land he the sd. George Cook now possesses and lives on"**—to **Lovel Wilkerson** for **£30**, witnessed by Henry Walker, William Anderson, Ben Johns, and Abram Venable (*no wife joining or relinquishing dower*).

## Remaining Research Questions (To Advance to "Proven Lineage Member")

1. **Gen 8 ← Gen 9 Migration Bridge (Post-September 1771):** Where did George Cook (and/or any minor male children born after Sept 1744) go after selling his last 250 acres on 16 September 1771? Exhaustive sweeps of LVA Chancery, *Publick Claims*, and *1782–1785 Heads of Families* confirm he vanished from Prince Edward. Testing whether he or his son settled on Sandy Creek in Pittsylvania County requires inspection of **Pittsylvania `Will Book 1:293–294` (Film #007642302)** and **1771–1785 Prince Edward / Halifax / Pittsylvania Court Order & Tax lists**.
2. **Pre-1750 Origin Test (`Spotsylvania County 1741–1744`):** **George Cook and wife Sarah** acquired 800 acres in St. George's Parish, Spotsylvania County (`1741–1743`, *Patents 21:145–146*), liquidated every acre by **4 March 1744[/45]** (*Spotsylvania DB D*), and vanished from Spotsylvania for 33 years (`1744–1777`). Both the husband (`George Cook`) and wife (**`Sarah`**) names match the **May 1768 Prince Edward couple**, making Spotsylvania `Order Books (1738–1749)` and `Amelia OB 1–2 (1735–1751)` the top pre-1750 archival test.
3. **Note on `Sarah Cook`'s Prince Edward Will (`WB 2:43`):** A will of `Sarah [+] Cook of Prince Edward County` dated **1 February 1764** and proved **17 October 1785** (admin. `William Mitchel`, naming daughters `Conney Micel` and `Ester Michell`) is recorded in *Prince Edward Will Book 2, p. 43*—though Knorr's marriage bonds show `Conny Cook` married `Mitchell` in March 1781. Under Virginia coverture, a married wife could not make a valid will in 1764 while George Cook was living (1768), indicating either a clerical year miscopy (`1764` for `1784`) or a distinct Prince Edward widow named Sarah Cook.
