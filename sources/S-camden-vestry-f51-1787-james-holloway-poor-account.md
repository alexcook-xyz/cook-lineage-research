## Transcription
Verbatim line (80 dpi): "To James Holoway for burying W. Shadrack Gossett .... 250". Spelling "Holoway" (the first "o" and "w" are clear; 80 percent sure there is a single "l").
Context: folio 51 headed "At a meeting of the Overseers of the Poor for the County of Pittsylvania, held at the Court House the 1st Day of September 1788, To Assess the Poor Rates &c. as the Law Directs" and the list is "for 1787". Outside the 1767-1785 window of Q-15; recorded because it names a Holloway-type surname in the county. No residence is stated.
