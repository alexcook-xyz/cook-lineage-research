# Pittsylvania County Deed Books 6 & 7 — George Cook Jr./Sr. Deeds
Research date: 2026-11-27. Standard: GPS, original images only, no user trees. Transcriptions verbatim from the record images; the Deed Book 7 spread was also read independently by Muse from the downloaded frame.

## DEED 2 — Deed Book 7, pp. 106–107 (read first; completed 2026-11-27)
**Citation:** Pittsylvania County, Virginia, Deed Book 7 (1783–1786), pp. 106–107. FamilySearch Catalog "Deed books, 1767–1901 (with wills 1767–1820); indexes to deeds, 1767–1934" (koha:415497), Item 2, Film 33265, DGS 7646007, Image 65 of 383 (Item 2), ARK 3Q9M-C9PX-G5GK. (Note: NOT DGS 7646030.)
**Image file:** PITT_DB7_p106-107_CookJr_to_CookSr_1783.jpg — vision-verified.

**Parties/preamble:** "This Indenture Made this 9th day of August in the year of our Lord Christ one thousand Seven hundred and Eighty Three Between George Cook Junr. of the County of Pittsylvania and Parish of Camden of one part and George Cook Senor. of the County and Parish aforesaid of the other part." The granting clause then names "the said George Cook Junr. and Ruth his Wife" as grantors. Margin: "Cook Senr. from Cook Junr. — Deed."

**Consideration (verbatim):** "for and in consideration of the Sum twenty five pounds good and Lawfull Money of the Colony of Virginia all Ready in hand paid them by the said George Cook Senr." The endorsed receipt repeats the £25, signed "George Cook Junr. LS." A money sale — NOT a love-and-affection or gift deed.

**Land (verbatim, no metes-and-bounds are given):** "a Certain Tract Plantation or parcell of Land Situate lying and being on Sandy Creek of Dan River in the County and Parrish aforesaid and adjoins the Land of James Holloway and is part of the same Track the said George Cook Junr. purchased of Electus Musick being by Estimation one hundred Acres more or less," with "all Houses out houses Orchards Gardens Waters ways Privileges woods & Low Grounds Marshes" and the usual reversions/rents clauses. Only neighbor named: James Holloway. No courses, distances, or corners.

**Signatures:** Only George Cook Junr. signs (deed and receipt), each with "LS." Ruth does NOT sign or make a mark anywhere, although the testimonium reads "In Witness whereof the said George Cook Junr. and Ruth his Wife have hereunto Set there Hands and Seals." No privy examination of Ruth is recorded.

**Witnesses (both the deed and the receipt):** Wm. Hedgeth, Joseph Flippen, James Holloway, **Elijah Cook**, Orlando Smith.

**Court:** "At a Court held for Pittsylvania County the 19th Day of August 1783 The within Indenture together with the Receipt hereon endorsed was proved by the Oaths of three of the Witnesses thereto to be the Acts & Deed of the within Named George Cook Junr. and the same was Ordered to be Recorded By the Court — Test Willm. Tunstall Cl."

### Answers — Deed Book 7
- **Q1 (relationship Jr. ↔ Sr.):** NONE stated. No "son," "father," love-and-affection, or inheritance language anywhere in the deed. The only differentiators are "Junr." and "Senor.," and the consideration is £25 cash. This deed does NOT prove a father-son relationship.
- **Q2 (other Cooks):** George Cook Junr. (grantor), Ruth Cook (his wife, named grantor, did not sign), George Cook Senor. (grantee), and **Elijah Cook (witness to both deed and receipt)** — the only other Cook in the instrument.
- **Q3 (Prince Edward / prior occupancy):** No mention of a George Cook of Prince Edward, and no prior-occupancy statement for any George Cook. The only title recital is Jr.'s purchase "of Electus Musick." The "Houses out houses Orchards Gardens" language shows an improved plantation but names no occupant.

## DEED 1 — Deed Book 6, pp. 34–35 (completed 2026-11-27)
**Citation:** Pittsylvania County, Virginia, Deed Book 6 (1780–1783), pp. 34–35. Same catalog record (koha:415497), Item 1, Film 33265, DGS 7646007, Image 28, ARK 3Q9M-C9PX-GGLS. (The prompt's DGS 7646030 is vol. 11 of the deed books, not Books 6–7.) The deed begins at the foot of p. 34 and ends mid-p. 35.
**Image file:** PITT_DB6_p34-35_Musick_to_CookeJr_1780.jpg — vision-verified by Muse; the readings below combine the pass's enlarged-viewer transcription with Muse's independent read of the delivered frame.

**Parties/preamble:** "This Indenture made the Second Day of March in the year of Our Lord Christ one thousand Seven hundred and eighty Between Electious Musick Senior of the one part and George Cooke Junior of the County of Pittsylvania and State of Virginia of the other part." (The grantor's name is spelled both "Electious" and "Elicious" within the instrument.) Margin: "Cooke from Musick — Deed."

**Consideration (verbatim):** "for and in Consideration of the Sum of Seventy five pounds Current money of Virginia hath Bargained Sold Aliened Released and Confirmed unto the Said George Cooke Junior, his heirs and Assigns for ever." A cash sale.

**Land:** "a certain Tract or Parcell of Land lying and being in the above Said County of Pittsylvania and State of Virginia aforesaid … Bounded in manner and form following Vizt. Beginning at a white Oak [first corner tree partly uncertain at delivery resolution] on a Ridge by Col. [name blotted/illegible] and runing South towards a branch of Sandy Creek Sixty four Degrees two Hundred and Seven pole to a pine thence west … thirty one pole to a pine thence … Six West One Hundred and twenty two pole to a pine … fifteen pole to a pine thence along the dividing line North twenty two West One Hundred and Twenty five pole to the Beginning Containing Ninety Seven Acres more or less." Two middle bearings are only partly legible; corners are pines, the Sandy Creek branch, and "the dividing line." No neighbour surname is legible in the bounds.
**Recital:** the tract was "part of the said Quantity of what belonged to the above Said Electious Musick," conveyed "together with the priviledges of Hunting fishing and fowling," with woods, underwoods, swamps, marshes, low grounds, meadows, feedings, and mineral ("Veins Mines and Quarries") rights.

**Signatures/witnesses:** Grantor signed by mark: "Electious [his mark] Musick L.S." Witnesses: William Collie; **Elisha Cooke, by "his mark"**; and **Ben Echols** (surname read from the delivered frame by Muse; the transcription pass had left it illegible).

**Court:** "At a Court held for Pittsylvania County the 15th day of August 1780 This Indenture of Bargain and Sale from Electious Musick to George Cooke was proved by the Oaths of the Witnesses thereto to be the Act and Deed of the said Electious Musick and the same was Ordered to be recorded by the Court. Test Will Tunstall Clk." Proved by witnesses, not acknowledged in person.

### Answers — Deed Book 6
- **Q1 (relationship):** NONE stated — no father/son words, no "natural love and affection," no inheritance language; consideration is £75 cash. George Cook Sr. does not appear in this deed at all.
- **Q2 (other Cooks):** George Cooke Junior (grantee) and **Elisha Cooke (witness, signed by mark)** — the only Cooks in the instrument.
- **Q3 (Prince Edward / prior occupancy):** No mention of a "George Cook of Prince Edward," and no prior occupancy by any George Cook. The only prior title is Musick's own ownership.

## Combined conclusion (both deeds)
The 1780–1783 Sandy Creek chain is documentary and complete: Electious Musick Sr. → George Cooke Jr. (2 Mar 1780, £75, 97 acres with full bounds) → George Cook Jr. & wife Ruth → George Cook Sr. (9 Aug 1783, £25, ~100 acres, no bounds, reciting Jr.'s Musick purchase). **Neither deed states any relationship between Jr. and Sr.** — both are arm's-length money sales, so the Jr. = son-of-Sr. identification still rests on the "Junr/Senor" styling and circumstantial evidence alone, not on deed language. Neither deed mentions Prince Edward or any prior George Cook occupancy. Two further Cook cluster names appear as witnesses: Elisha Cooke (1780, by mark) and Elijah Cook (1783).
