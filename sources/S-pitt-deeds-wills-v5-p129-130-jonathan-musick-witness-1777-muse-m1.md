## Transcription
Will dated January 1777, proved "At a Court held for Pixlvania County, the 27th Day of March 1777". Witness list as OCR'd: "Ted Jeremiah Stimson L S. Richd Echoles John Stamps Daniel Everett Jonathan Musick".
