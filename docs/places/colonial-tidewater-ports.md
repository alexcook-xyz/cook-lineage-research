# Colonial Tidewater & Chesapeake Ports of Entry: Transatlantic Arrival Guide

- **Target Geographic Focus:** Tidewater Virginia (James River, Appomattox River, York River) and Chesapeake Bay ports of entry.
- **Parent Jurisdictions:** Prince George County (parent of Amelia), Surry, Isle of Wight, Charles City, and Henrico counties.

---

## 1. Relevance to the Cook Lineage

To advance the lineage beyond the 1750 Amelia County patentee (Generation 9) to the immigrant ancestor who arrived from the British Isles:
- Land in the Virginia Piedmont was settled by second- and third-generation Tidewater planters moving westward along river systems.
- The 1750 patentee George Cook purchased his 400 acres on Harris's Creek by cash treasury payment (40 shillings), a privilege typical of established Virginia planters.
- The immediate parent county of Amelia was **Prince George County** (formed 1703), which in turn was created from Charles City County (one of the original eight shires of 1634).
