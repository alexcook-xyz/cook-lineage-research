# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the **Cook Patrilineal Lineage Research** repository.

## Editorial Standards

All contributions, proposed additions, and issue discussions must adhere to the **Genealogical Proof Standard (GPS)**:
1. **Primary Records Preferred:** Cite original or microfilm courthouse, parish, and state archive records. Secondary vendor family trees (Ancestry, FamilySearch trees) are not acceptable as proof.
2. **Explicit Disambiguation:** When documenting individuals named *George Cook*, always specify the associated waterway (Sandy Creek, Banister River, Pigg River, Dan River), spouses, and known land patents/deeds.
3. **No Unsubstantiated Lineages:** Avoid attaching speculative parents without metes-and-bounds deed continuity, probate distribution records, or court order attestations.
4. **Style Guide:** Format all citations in accordance with Elizabeth Shown Mills, *Evidence Explained: Citing History Sources from Artifacts to Cyberspace*, or the *Chicago Manual of Style*.
