## Transcription
Folio 12 header: "At a Vestry held for Camboen Parish at Pittsylvania Courthouse the 28th day of November 1771 For Laying the Parish Levy". Folio 13 (continuing): "Pursuant to an Order of the Worshipfull Court of Pittsylvania County bearing date the 27 day of August last past for the Vestry to appoint Processioners according to Law ..." The order year of the Court is not stated on the page; the 28 Nov 1771 attribution is by position (inference).
Folio 14, verbatim: "Ordero that James Talbot James Dillard John Cook and William Hind Procession all the Pattent Land from the mouth of snow Creek up b with sides to the head thereof and to the ridges &c &c"
"Ordero that Robert Hill Thomas Jones William Cook and Sinkfield Hill Procession all the Pattent Land from the mouth of Chesnutt Creek up Pigg River on bowth sides to the head thereof and to the ridges &c &c"
Also on folio 13: "Ordero that John Wheeler William Collier and Thomas Collier Procession all the Pattent Land from the old womans Creek up Stanton River to Pigg River ..." (Collier, not Collie). Folio 15 (same orders): "that David Terry Benjamin Terry Jun Joseph Metcalf and William King Procession all the Patten Land from where the County line Crosses Sandy Creek to the head of the said [word?] and to the ridges on bowth sides &c to Banister"; no Cook, Musick, Holloway, Flippin, Echols, Collie or Hedgpeth in that Sandy Creek precinct.
