## Citation
"Oaths of Allegiance - 1777 Pittsylvania County, VA," transcription by Marian Dodson Chiarito, *The Magazine of Virginia Genealogy* 23, no. 1 (Feb. 1985), from a typescript made about 1930 by S. H. F. Jones from manuscripts in the Pittsylvania County Clerk's Office, Chatham, certified 8 Nov 1939; online copy by Cynthia Hubbard Headen, *Pittsylvania County, VAGenWeb* (https://usgenwebsites.org/vagenweb/pittsylvania/court/Oaths.html : accessed 28 Nov 2026). Original manuscripts not seen; the typescript preface says some names were faded and could not be deciphered.

## Transcription
Names relevant to Q-15, verbatim, with the list heading each appears under:
- William Ward's List: "Michael Mullings / Benjamin Mullins / Thomas Music / John Neal"; also "Richard Halloway" (same list)
- Charles Kennon's List: "Charles Collie / William Collie"; also "Joseph Echols / Richard Echols"
- Capt. Hankin's List: "John Cook / William Cook"
- Capt. Jas. Roberts' List: "John Cock / Benjamin Cook"
- John Wilson's List: "Joseph Cook"

Not found anywhere in the transcribed lists (read in full by this searcher): Electious, Elexius, Elixius, or any Musick/Music other than "Thomas Music"; George Cook (Sr. or Jr.); Elijah Cook; Elisha Cook(e); Flippen/Flippin; James Holloway (only "Richard Halloway" in Ward's list). The lists end with "Last name of next three unknown: Geo.-- / James-- / Joseph--".

## Notes
- Three-step derivative: 1777 manuscripts, 1930 typescript, 1985 article, web copy. Each step can introduce errors, so absence of a name is weak evidence; the list is also known to be incomplete (three surnames unknown, refusals listed separately).
- William Collie witnessed the 1780 Musick to Cooke Jr. deed (DB 6:34-35); the 1777 "William Collie" and "Charles Collie" under Kennon's list place a Collie in Pittsylvania in 1777. A lead on the FAN cluster, not an identity.
- Thomas Music (Ward's list) is a possible Musick relative; no relationship is stated.
