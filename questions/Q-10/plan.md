# Plan: Did George, Josiah, or Zachariah Cook own land or litigate in Halifax County, Virginia before 1767?

## Objective
Determine whether George Cook, Josiah Cook, or Zachariah Cook held land, appeared in a deed, or litigated in Halifax County, Virginia (created 1752 from Lunenburg; the western part became Pittsylvania in 1767), between 1752 and 1775, and what those records show about the Dan River and Sandy Creek Cook presence before Pittsylvania existed.

Flagged as unsourced: the Zachariah death date and the Josiah grant details come from a wiki page with no source file. They must be checked against the originals before they are used as facts.

## Hypotheses
1. **H1 (presence):** George, Josiah, or Zachariah Cook held land or appeared in Halifax deeds, orders, or pleas between 1752 and 1767 (Dan River or Sandy Creek area).
2. **H2 (no pre-1767 presence):** None of the three appears in Halifax records before 1767; the Cook presence on Sandy Creek begins in Pittsylvania records after 1767.
3. **H3 (same-name separation):** Any "Cook" entry in Halifax refers to a different Cook (for example John, Henry, Edmund, or Richard Cook), so the three named men are absent even though Cook names occur.

H2 and H3 are the null outcomes. A search that finds only other Cooks supports H2 and H3 and does not, by itself, rule out H1.
