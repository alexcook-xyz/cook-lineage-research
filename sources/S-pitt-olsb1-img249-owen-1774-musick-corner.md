## Citation
Pittsylvania County, Virginia, Old Land Survey Book 1, p. 253 (red-pencil page number, read as 253), survey for William Owen, 1 Apr 1774, by John Donelson Jr., assistant surveyor [signature flourish seen; initials not independently checked]; Clerk of the Circuit Court's office, Chatham; image 249 of 367, *Internet Archive* (https://archive.org/details/old-land-survey-book-1 : accessed 28 Nov 2026; page image n248); file "Old Land Survey Book 1.pdf".

## Transcription
"The 1.st April 1774 Surv.d for William Owen 400 Ac.s of Land on the Branches of Sandy creek & Bounded as follows Viz.t Beginning at Roger Adkin- sons Corner pine Thence along Walthers Line S 80 1/2 W 28 po. to a white Oak in standing on a pond thence S 6 [?] W 40 po. to a post White Oak thence New Lines S 27 W 80 po. to a pine S 53 E 110 po. to pointers east 166 po. to a pine on a Branch N 77 E 30 po. to Musick Corner Red Oak thence along his Lines N 3 E 266 po. crossing Bold branch to Robert Walthers Corner in S.d Line thence N .. 200 po. to pointers in Adkensons Order line Thence along his Line N 80 W 184 po. to the first Station"
Plot label: "William Owen 400 Ac."

## Notes
- "Musick Corner Red Oak" and "his Lines" are the Sandy Creek adjoiner; no given name. Same-day survey as Walthers (1 Apr 1774, [[S-pitt-olsb1-img251-walthers-1774-george-musick-line]]), which names "George Musick Line".
- Some bearings uncertain (marked [?]); the "Musick" reading is clear at 150 dpi.
- No chain carriers named. No Cook.
