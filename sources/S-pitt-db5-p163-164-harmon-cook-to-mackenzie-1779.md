## Transcription
p. 163 margin: "McKinsey from Cook Deed Ex^d". "This Indenture made this 20th day of April in the third year of the Common Wealth and in the year of our Lord Christ one thousand seven hundred and Seventy nine. Between Harmon Cook of the County of Pittsylvania of the one part and Aaron Mackenzie of the County of Henry in the Parish of Cartruck [?] of the other part Witnesseth ... fifty nine Pounds Current money of Virginia ... all that Devident Tract [interlined: piece] or parcel of Land ... in the parish & County of Pittsylvania on both sides of Turkey Cock Creek Containing by Estimation one hundred and sixty two Acres ... which said Tract was Conveyed by George Jefferson to William Tredaway [?] by Indenture bearing Date the Twentyeth Day of March 1771 and Duly Recorded in the said County Court of Pittsylvania ... Beginning at Author Hopkins Corner White oak ..."
p. 164: signed "Harmon Cook L.S." / "Maryan Cook L.S."; memorandum of livery ("In presence of" followed by a brace and no names legible); "At a Court held for Pittsylvania County the 20th Day of April 1779 This Indenture Together with the memorandum ... was Acknowledged by the above named Harmon Cook & Maryann the Wife of the said Harmon being Prively Examined ... Relinquished her right of dower in and to the ..." [continues, not read].
