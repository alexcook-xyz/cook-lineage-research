## Transcription
Page 44 (read at 85 dpi): "To Harmon Cook assign of John Lucas for 350 lb Beef @ 16/8 for State Troops . . . 2. 15." / "the same [assign of Jeremiah Potect (?)] for 1 Beef impressed for the State Troops . . . 2. 15." / "the same for 340 lb Beef @ 16/8 impressed for the Continental Troops . . . 2. 16. 8." Surname of the second assignor read "Potect" at 85 dpi (could be "Poteet"); first name "Jeremiah" clear.

## Notes
The only entry found that names a relationship of assignment. Court sitting date not on page: p. 45 begins a new sitting "the 5th Day of October 1782", which appears below the end of p. 44's entries, so p. 44 is in the sitting headed before it (earlier header not located in pp. 9-39; see S-pitt-claims-1782-p28-harmon-cook).
