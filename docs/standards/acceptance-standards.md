# Acceptance Standards for Lineage Profiles & Generational Proof

This document sets forth the official standards of evidence and completion required for any individual profile to be accepted into the **Cook Patrilineal Lineage Research Project**.

---

## 1. The Core Standard: The Genealogical Proof Standard (GPS)

All genealogical conclusions in this repository must satisfy the five criteria of the **Genealogical Proof Standard (GPS)** established by the Board for Certification of Genealogists (BCG):

1. **Reasonably Exhaustive Research:** Every relevant record repository and record group (vital registers, wills, administrations, deeds, mortgages, court order books, tax lists, census schedules, military files, church minutes, and cemetery registers) must be searched across the individual's known residential lifetime.
2. **Complete & Accurate Citations:** Every asserted fact must link directly to a specific source citation created in accordance with Elizabeth Shown Mills, *Evidence Explained: Citing History Sources from Artifacts to Cyberspace*, or the *Chicago Manual of Style*.
3. **Thorough Analysis & Correlation:** All evidence—direct, indirect, and negative—must be systematically analyzed for authenticity, date of creation, relationship to the event, and potential informant bias.
4. **Resolution of Conflicting Evidence:** Any discrepancies (such as conflicting ages, misspelled names, hearsay birthplace statements, or competing public family trees) must be explicitly addressed, tested against primary documents, and resolved.
5. **A Soundly Reasoned, Coherent Written Conclusion:** The connection between generations must be set forth in a clear, unambiguous written narrative demonstrating that no other reasonable interpretation exists.

---

## 2. Definition of a "Complete Profile"

An individual profile in the project wiki is classified as **Complete** only when all of the following required elements are populated and backed by primary documentation:

- **Full Name & Known Aliases:** Complete legal name along with any recorded spelling variations (e.g., phonetic shifts like *Harlin* vs. *Arlin*, or middle initials like *George B*).
- **Vital Chronology (Anchored Dates):**
  - Birth date (exact date from vital certificate/Bible/gravestone, or calculated range from census/tax lists).
  - Marriage date(s) and full name of spouse(s) with primary bond, license, or church return citation.
  - Death date (exact date from death certificate/obituary/probate record, or *terminus ante quem* from court proving date).
- **Residential Timeline & Geographic Loci:** A chronological table of the individual's residence, citing tax lists, deed transactions, court appearances, and federal censuses.
- **Land & Property Records:** Watercourse location (creek/river), tract acreage, neighboring landowners, and chain-of-title references (grantor/grantee deeds, patents, or grants).
- **Probate & Estate Settlement:** Will citations, administration bonds, inventories, account audits, and orphan/guardian records.
- **Primary Source Plate/Image Links:** Direct file references to digitized scans of original county courthouse records.
- **Explicit Disambiguation Matrix:** If the individual shares a common name (e.g., *George Cook*), the profile must include a disambiguation section distinguishing him from concurrent contemporaries living in the same county or adjoining watersheds.
- **Open Research Questions:** A prioritized list of remaining archival records to be pulled or inspected.

## 3. Standard for Accepting a Person as a "Proven Lineage Member"

For a father-to-child generational link to be formally marked as **Proven**, one of the following two evidentiary burdens must be fulfilled:

### Standard A: Direct Documentary Proof (Primary Record Link)
A primary document created near the time of the event by a person with direct knowledge that explicitly names the relationship:
- A birth certificate, baptismal record, or Bible record naming the father and mother.
- A father's will or deed of gift explicitly stating *"my son [Name]"* or *"to my children [Names]"*.
- An intestate heirship conveyance wherein the heirs-at-law join together to sell inherited land, explicitly identifying themselves as *"heirs at law of [Father's Name], deceased"* (e.g., Adair County DB Z:195; Pittsylvania County DB 33:101 & DB 39:242).

### Standard B: Indirect Evidence & Metes-and-Bounds Assemblage (The "Virginia Standard")
In the absence of a surviving will or explicit birth record (common in burned counties or unrecorded frontier marriages), parentage may be proven through an assemblage of indirect evidence that collectively eliminates all other alternatives:
1. **Chain of Title Continuity:** The child inherits, occupies, pays taxes on, or sells the exact metes-and-bounds acreage originally patented or purchased by the father.
2. **Tax List Tracking:** The son appears on the father's personal property tax [tithable](# "Colonial Virginia poll-tax unit (1620s–1780s): any free male, indentured servant, or enslaved person aged 16+, for whom the head of household paid annual colony, county, and parish taxes.") entry upon reaching age 16 or 21 before establishing his own separate household on the same watercourse.
3. **Estate Inventory / Administration Connection:** The son serves as administrator, purchaser at the estate sale, or bondsman for the father's estate administration.
4. **Negative Searches:** Comprehensive searches of all other men bearing the surname in the same county confirm that no other candidate father existed who could have fathered the subject during the relevant timeframe.

---

## 4. Status Classifications

Every profile in this repository is assigned one of four official evidentiary statuses:

- **Proven:** Meets all criteria of the Genealogical Proof Standard with primary direct documentation or an airtight indirect chain of title.
- **Working Hypothesis (~75–80% Probability):** Strong circumstantial and geographical correlation supported by court/deed records, but lacking an explicit naming document (e.g., the link between George B and George Cook Sr. pending in-person inspection of Will Book 1:293–294).
- **Under Investigation:** Active archival inquiry in progress with specific open retrieval issues.
- **Refuted / Excluded:** Candidate individuals or lineages that have been disproven through primary records and quarantined as false leads.
