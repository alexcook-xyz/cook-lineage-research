# Cook Patrilineal Lineage Research Roadmap: Alex Haley Cook (b. 1991) to Colonial Immigration

This roadmap sets out the archival and methodological steps required to complete, verify, and document every link in the patrilineal ancestry of **Alex Haley Cook** (b. 1991) back to the immigrant ancestor who arrived in British North America.

Every generational argument follows the **Genealogical Proof Standard (GPS)**:
1. Reasonably exhaustive research across all extant public and church jurisdictions.
2. Complete, accurate source citations (*Evidence Explained* / *Chicago Manual of Style*).
3. Critical correlation and analysis of primary, direct, and indirect evidence.
4. Resolution of conflicting evidence with explicit documentary proof.
5. A soundly reasoned, written conclusion.

---

## Phase 1: Kentucky Line Completeness & Collateral Verification (Gen 1–6)
**Goal:** Solidify every collateral, census, and vital record between Adair/Taylor Counties, Kentucky, and the 1836 migration out of Virginia.

- [x] Prove Gen 1 → 2: Alex Haley Cook to Brian Allen Cook (Personal knowledge, 1991 & 1962 Birth Indexes, 2025 Mike Cook obituary).
- [x] Prove Gen 2 → 3: Brian Allen Cook to Arlin Cook (1991 Columbia obit, 1986 Campbellsville obit, family archive).
- [x] Prove Gen 3 → 4: Arlin Cook to Russell Cook (1991 obit, SSA Numident `Russel Cook` / `Anna B. Sneed`, 1935 Birth Index `Harlin Cook`, 1940 & 1950 censuses).

- [x] Prove Gen 5 → 6: George William Cook to George W. Cook (1877 Adair `DB Z:195` 2/6 heirship deed for 142 acres on Russell's Creek, Eliza E. Cook 1900/1906 will `WB M:133–134`, 1860 & 1870 censuses).

## Phase 2: Resolve the Generation 7 → 8 Pittsylvania Bridge (1780–1837)
**Goal:** Prove or disprove whether George Cook ("George B," d. 1831) was the son of George Cook Sr. ("the Testator," d. 1835).

- [x] Prove Gen 6 → 7: George W. Cook of Adair Co., KY to George Cook ("George B") of Pittsylvania Co., VA via `DB 33:101` (1831), `DB 39:242` (1836 POA), and `DB 39:256–257` (1836 deed reciting infancy in 1831).
- [ ] **Task 2.1 (P0 In-Person Pull):** Inspect Pittsylvania County Will Book 1, pages 293–294 at Library of Virginia or a FamilySearch Center (Film #007642302) to read the full text of George Cook Sr.'s will (proved 21 Sep 1835). Determine whether George B or his orphaned children (George W., Henry, etc.) are named.
- [ ] **Task 2.2 (WKU In-Person Check):** Search print volume *Marriages of Pittsylvania County, Virginia, 1806–1830* (Kathleen Booth Williams, 1980) at Western Kentucky University Special Collections (Bowling Green, KY) for George Cook × Catharine.
- [ ] **Task 2.3:** Inspect Pittsylvania County Mixed Probate Books 3 and 7, and Accounts Current Book 3, page 264 (1804 division of Elijah Cook) to reconstruct probate distributions and estate accounts.
- [ ] **Task 2.4:** Examine Pittsylvania County Personal Property and Land Tax Lists (1782–1837) annually to isolate and track each concurrent adult George Cook by watershed (Sandy Creek vs. Dan River vs. Pigg River vs. Banister).
- [ ] **Task 2.5:** Examine Pittsylvania County Order Books 1831–1837 for guardian appointments or apprentice bonds for George and Henry Cook following the death of George Cook in 1831.

## Phase 3: Pre-1780 Southside Piedmont Migration Bridge (1750–1780)
**Goal:** Track the migration of George Cook Sr. backward from Pittsylvania County across Halifax, Lunenburg, Prince Edward, and Amelia Counties, Virginia.

- [x] Establish survey bearing continuity between 3 Nov 1750 Amelia Patent (`Patents 29:316–317`, 400 acres Harris's Creek) and 18 Apr 1768 Prince Edward Deed (`DB 3:366–368`, 150 acres to Alexander Guill).
- [ ] **Task 3.1:** Trace disposition of the remaining 250 acres of George Cook's 1750 patent on Harris's Creek across Prince Edward County Deed Books 3 through 7 (1768–1785).
- [ ] **Task 3.2:** Search Prince Edward County Court Order Books 1 through 7 (1754–1780) for civil suits, tithables, road orders, and estate proceedings mentioning George Cook.
- [ ] **Task 3.3:** Search Amelia County Deed Books (1735–1754) and Court Order Books for George Cook prior to the 1754 formation of Prince Edward County.
- [ ] **Task 3.4:** Search Halifax County, Virginia Deed Books 1–10 (1752–1775) and Court Pleas Books 1–9 for records of George Cook, Josiah Cook, and Zachariah Cook prior to and during the 1767 formation of Pittsylvania County.
- [ ] **Task 3.5:** Search Lunenburg County tithable lists (Landon C. Bell, *Sunlight on the Southside*, 1748–1783) and deed books for Cook families along the Staunton, Banister, and Dan Rivers.
- [ ] **Task 3.6:** Search Caswell, Orange, and Granville Counties, North Carolina borderland deed and court records (1750–1785) directly adjoining Sandy Creek and the Dan River.

## Phase 4: Colonial Roots to Immigrant Arrival (Pre-1750)
**Goal:** Determine parentage and transatlantic arrival of the earliest Virginia colonial Cook ancestor.

- [ ] **Task 4.1:** Search Virginia Land Patents 1 through 28 (1623–1749) via Library of Virginia Archives for early Cook headright patents along the Appomattox, James, and Nottoway river basins.
- [ ] **Task 4.2:** Examine Prince George County (parent county of Amelia, formed 1703) surviving court and deed records (1710–1740) for George Cook and related patrilineal kinsmen.
- [ ] **Task 4.3:** Examine Bristol Parish Vestry Book and Register (Henrico, Charles City, Prince George, Dinwiddie Cos., 1720–1789) for Cook births, baptisms, and processioning orders.
- [ ] **Task 4.4:** Correlate Chesapeake Maryland vs. Tidewater Virginia colonial arrival records (Coldham, *Complete Book of Emigrants*; Skordas, *Early Settlers of Maryland*; Tepper, *Passengers to America*).
- [ ] **Task 4.5:** Identify candidate immigrant ancestor by correlating British port departure records (London, Bristol, Liverpool) with colonial land patent headrights in early Virginia.
