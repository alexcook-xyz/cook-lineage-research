## Transcription
Verbatim from the image; line breaks not preserved. `[ ]` = my uncertainty.

Page 67, margin: "Cook to Wilkerson"

"This Indenture made this sixth day of April and in the year of our Lord one Thousand seven hundred and Seventy one Between George Cook of the County of Prince Edward of the one part and Lovel Wil[k?]erson of the sd. County of the other part Witnesseth that the said George Cook for & in consideration of the sum of Thirty pounds good & Lawfull money to him in hand paid by the sd. Lovel Wil[k?]erson the receipt whereof the sd. George Cook doth hereby Acknowledg hath granted Bargained and sold and by these presents doth grant Bargain sell Alien enfeof and Confirm unto the sd. Lovel Wil[k?]erson and to his heirs and Assigns forever one certain Tract or parcel of Land lying and being in the the County of Prince Edward on the head Branches of Harrisses Creek and containing by Estimation two hundred and fifty Acres with patent bearing date the third day of November MDCCL & it being all that tract of Land he the sd. George Cook now Possesses and lives on To have and to hold the above mentioned Tract of Land and all and Singular the premises above mentioned unto the sd. Lovel Wil[k?]erson and to his heirs and Assigns for ever to the only proper use and Behoof of him the sd. Lovel Wil[k?]erson and to his heirs and Assigns and the sd. George Cook for himself his heirs the sd. Tract or parcel of Land and every part thereof to the sd. Lovel Wil[k?]erson and to his heirs shall and will warrant and ever[?] by these presents Defend against all person or persons whatsoever In Witness whereof the sd. George Cook hath hereunto set his hand and affixt seal the day and year first above written
Signed [sealed interlined] and delivered in presents off
Henry Walker
Wm Anderson
Ben Johns
Ab Venable
[signature] George Cook (seal)"

Page 68:
"Memorandum that on the Sixth Day of April one thousand seven hundred and Seventy one Quiet and peasible possession and Seisen of the within granted Land and premises was had done and delivered by the within named George Cook to the within named Lovel Wil[k?]erson according to the form and Effect of the within Written deed
Test Henry Walker / Wil[liam?] Anderson / Ben Johns / Ab Venable
[signature] George Cook (seal)

Then Recd of Lovell Wil[k?]erson Thirty Pounds Curr. Money of Virga which is the Consideration for the within Mentioned Lands and premises as witness [flourish]

At a court held for Prince Edward County June 177[1; final digit appears overwritten or corrected, not certain] this deed was proved in court by Abram Venable & Ben Johns and at an other court held for the sd. County Sept. 16th. 1771 this deed was further proved by Henry Walker & ordered to be recorded. &c
Test F. Watkins D C"

(The lower part of p. 68 begins the next deed, Harris to Cheadle, 18 Jan 1770 [year as read: "seventy"]; not transcribed.)

## Notes
- Informant: George Cook (grantor), signing by his own name in a clerk-written hand; he was in a position to know his own residence ("now Possesses and lives on").
- Spelling of the grantee: the clerk's k is written like an h, so the grantee reads "Wilherson" at first glance. Muse and the earlier sweep read "Wilkerson"; I give my reading as [k?]. Name is not load-bearing for Q-15 (the grantor is).
- No wife is named and no dower release appears on these pages. No metes and bounds, no neighbors.
- Patent date 3 Nov 1750 matches Patents 29:316-317 (George Cook, 400 ac, Harris's Creek).
- Task description called this "the sale attributed to George Cook"; the image shows George Cook as grantor, so no attribution issue on the face of the deed. The page shows the date of signing (6 Apr 1771) and two proof dates (June and 16 Sep 1771); there is no separate recording date.
