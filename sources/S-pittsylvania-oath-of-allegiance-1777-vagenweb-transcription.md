## Citation
"Oaths of Allegiance - 1777 Pittsylvania County, VA," transcribed by Marian Dodson Chiarito, *The Magazine of Virginia Genealogy* 23, no. 1 (Feb. 1985); web copy by Cynthia Hubbard Headen, *Pittsylvania County VAGenWeb* (https://usgenwebsites.org/vagenweb/pittsylvania/court/Oaths.html : accessed 28 Nov 2026). The magazine text reproduces a typewritten copy held in the Clerk's Office, Pittsylvania County, Chatham, made by S. H. F. Jones about 1930 from the manuscript lists then in the clerk's office (certified by Jones 8 Nov 1939; his wife's oath certified by E. E. Friend, Clerk, 8 Nov 1939). Underlying originals: Pittsylvania Co. 1777 oath lists, manuscripts; microfilm: Library of Virginia, Pittsylvania County, Reel 221, "Persons Taking Revolutionary Oath of Allegiance, Authenticated by Clerk, Vol. 1, 1777-1778, 17 p." (LVA catalog: https://www.lva.virginia.gov/collections/ccmf/VA/VA213). Original manuscripts and the film were NOT viewed. This is a transcription of a 1930 typescript of the manuscripts (three removes).

## Transcription
Whole page read twice (page text, 28 Nov 2026). It has 19 headed lists plus a final fragment. No page numbers or dates of administration appear. Heading order: List of George Carter; Persons Refusing to take Oath; Thomas Dillard, List; Stephen Coleman's List; Capt. Hankin's List; Lankford's List; Jas. Morton's List; Robert Payne's List; Reuben Pain's List; Charles Kennon's List; Capt. Jas. Roberts' List; Abraham Shelton's List; Crispin Shelton's List; William Short's List; William Ward's List; John Wilson's List; William Witcher's List; "Last name of next three unknown".

Entries for the surnames and names sought (verbatim):
- Capt. Hankin's List: "John Cook"; "William Cook"
- Capt. Jas. Roberts' List: "John Cock"; "Benjamin Cook"
- John Wilson's List: "Joseph Cook"
- William Ward's List: "Thomas Music"
- Final fragment, verbatim: "Geo.--" / "James--" / "Joseph--" (surnames not given)
- Elsewhere: "Malachiah (unknown)" (end of "List of George Carter"); "(unknown) Perdew" (William Short's List); "(unknown) Mahaney" (John Wilson's List)

No entry reads George Cook, George Cooke, Geo. Cook Jr., Elijah Cook, Electious/Elexius/Eleckshus Musick, or any Musick/Mussick spelling. Near-neighbor surnames present: "Avery Mustain, Jesse Mustain, Thomas Mustain" (Crispin Shelton's List); these are Mustain, not Musick, and are not treated as variants here.

## Notes
- Informant: unknown. The oath was sworn by each man before county justices; the lists here are headed by militia captains' or justices' names, not by a stated place, so Sandy Creek residents cannot be identified from the headings.
- Transcriber caveat from the 1939 certificate: "The writing was faded and a few names could not be deciphered." Some names may be omitted or misread, and one list's sequence is not alphabetical.
- Cannot show whether this transcription is the whole 17-page volume. Absence of a name here is not proof the man did not swear the oath.
- "Thomas Music" (William Ward's List) is a possible spelling of Musick; no evidence ties him to Electious Musick. Lead only.
