## Citation
Pittsylvania County, Virginia, "Grantor Index to Deeds, Trust Deeds, Liens, Etc." (Cott Family Name Index, AC-50), letter C, sheet 27 (red sheet number at top right; PDF page 90 of 120), surname Cook, entries dated 23 Sept 1773 through 15 Aug 1814; County Court Clerk's Office, Chatham; digital image, *Internet Archive* ("grantor-index-june-1765-1889", file for letter C), downloaded by the user and read by records-searcher 2026-11-28 from the local PDF. Index only: a typed clerk's index compiled after 1934 (form copyright 1934); the "Date of Record" column gives the date each deed was recorded, not the deed date. Layer 3: Pittsylvania Deed Books 3-18 as cited in the Vol./Page columns (not opened for this source).

## Notes
- Informant: the clerk who indexed the deed books; no knowledge of the parties. The index does not give the deed date, acreage, place, or witnesses.
- The 19 Aug 1783 row matches the date of proving of DB 7:106-107 ([[S-pittsylvania-db7-p106-107-cook-jr-to-cook-sr-1783]]). It confirms the indexer read the grantors as George Jr and Ruth (wf) and the grantee as George Cook Sr.
- No row on this sheet for a grantor George (not Jr), Elijah, Elisha, or Zachariah in 1767-1790. Elisha (with Ann, wf) appears 16 Sept 1806 and George ("Comr, al") 19 Jan 1807, out of range.
- The index lists Cook grantors only. Grantees named Cook are in the separate grantee index.
