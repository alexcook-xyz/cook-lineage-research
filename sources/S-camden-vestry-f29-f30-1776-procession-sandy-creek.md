## Transcription
Folio 29, verbatim: "Ordered that George Young Samuel Shields James Shields James Cox? Joseph Cuningham John Morton & Randolph Gibson do procession all the Pattented land from Jos Cuningham up Sandy River on boath sides to the head thereof and to the Ridges &c" ("Cox" read about 70 percent; could be "Cox" or "Coe").
Folio 30, verbatim: "Ordered that Benjamin Terry Elijah King John Kirby and Henry Harden do procession all the Pattented Land from where the County line Crosses Sandy Creek up Boath sides to the head thereof and to the Ridges &c" (150 dpi re-read). "Ordered that George Carter Joseph Terry Jun Robert Watters & David Terry do procession all the Pattented land from where the County line Crosses Burches Creek up boath sides to the head thereof ..." Orders signed by John Donelson, Hugh Innes, Crispin Shelton, John Willson, Thomas Dillard, William Witcher, William Tunstall.
The Sandy Creek precinct here is on the Virginia-North Carolina line; the Sandy Creek of the Cook-Musick deeds (Pittsylvania DB 6:34-35) is not located by this entry (inference only if the analyst finds a deed bound).

## Notes
Image seen (color scan, read at 80 dpi, key lines re-read at 150 dpi). The scan is a manuscript volume (a vestry minute book, apparently a fair copy attested by the clerk: "A Copy Test" signature lines occur). Page order inside the IA file is not always folio order. Folio numbers are the clerk's parenthesised page numbers at the top of each leaf. Informant: the vestry clerk (Benjamin Lankford to 1779, then Vincent Shelton). Source_class "original" is provisional: the volume may be a clerk's fair copy of earlier minutes (analyst to decide).
