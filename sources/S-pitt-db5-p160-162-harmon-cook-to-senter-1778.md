## Transcription
p. 160 margin: "Senter from Cook Deed". "This Indenture made this twenty sixth day of November in the year of our Lord Christ one Thousand seven hundred and seventy eight Between Harmon Cooke of Pittsylvania County and Colony of Virginia of the one part and Stephen Senter of the above County and Colony of the other part Witnesseth that the said Harmon Cooke for and in Consideration of the sum of one hundred and thirty pounds ... all that piece parcell or Tract of Land which was granted to Eliska Eastis by way of patent bearing date the sixtenth day of August one thous and seven hundred [text continues onto p. 161: ...] Sixty four and afterwards sold by the said Eastis to the s^d Harmon Cook Situate lying and being in the County and Colony aforesaid on both side of Turkey Coack Creeke ... Beginning at a white oak on the east side of the Creeke near Read and Companys line ... in Owens Line ... Little Turkey Cock Creek ... containing two hundred and Sixty Acres ..."
p. 162: "In Witness whereof the said Harmon Cook and Mary his wife hath hereunto set their hands and seals the day and year first above written. Sealed and Delivered In the presence of [no witness names written] / Harmon Cook L.S. [signs in his own hand] / Maryann X Cook L.S." Memorandum of livery of seisin signed Harmon Cook; receipt of 26 Nov 1778 for 130 pounds. "At a Court held for Pittsylvania County the 20th Day of April 1779 The within Indenture together with Memorandum of Livery & Seizen and Receipt hereon indorsed was Acknowledged by the within named Harmon Cook to be his several acts and Deed & Maryann his wife being freely Examined ... Relinquished her right of Dower ..." Margin "Examin'd".

## Notes
- Original image seen. "Eliska Eastis" matches the index's "Elisha Eastes" (grantor to Harmon Cook, DB 1:219, 1769). No George, Elijah or Musick on pp. 160-162. No witnesses named to the Cook-Senter deed on p. 162.
