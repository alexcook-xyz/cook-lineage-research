## Citation
Pittsylvania County, Virginia, Old Land Survey Book 1, p. 255 (clerk's red-pencil page number; printed number read as 255), survey for Robert Walthers, 1 Apr 1774, by John Donelson Jr., assistant surveyor; Clerk of the Circuit Court's office, Chatham; image 251 of 367, *Internet Archive* (https://archive.org/details/old-land-survey-book-1 : accessed 28 Nov 2026; page image n250); file "Old Land Survey Book 1.pdf". Whether this is the surveyor's original record or a clerk's copy is not stated on the page.

## Transcription
"the 1.st April 1774 Surv.d for Robert Walthers 400 Ac. of Land on the branches of Sandy creek & Bounded as follows Viz.t Beginning at a pine in Kings Line thence New Lines N 122 po. crossing a branch to a White Oak in the S.d Kings Line thence along his lines the S.d Kings N 20 W 161 po. to a White Oak in Adkenson Order Line thence along his Line S 38 W 235 po. to a White Oak N 80 W 142 po. to pointers in the S.d Order thence New Lines S 200 po. to a pine in George Musick Line thence along his line N 83 E 134 po. to a pine in the Aff.d Kings line & thence along his Line N 23 1/2 E 180 po. crossing a branch to the Beginning / p Jn.o Donelson jr Ass.t"
Plot label: "Robert Walthers 400 Ac."

## Notes
- Read at about 150 dpi on a crop of the heading and body; the bearings are not independently checked. "122 po." as written (no bearing seen before it).
- "George Musick" is a neighbor's line in 1774; no given-name or relationship information. The LVA index of Patents 40:861 names a George Musick as adjoiner of Electious Musick in 1772 ([[S-va-land-patents40-p861-musick-1772-index]]). No chain carriers are named in this book.
- No Cook on the page.
