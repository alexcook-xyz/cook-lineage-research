## Notes
- Informant: a twentieth-century clerk's or commercial typed index compiled from the deed books; derivative. Names may be misspelled or mis-typed (see the 1787/1789 date oddity above).
- No George Cook entry appears between 1768 and 1783 on this page. The index lists no Cook grantee of any given name Elisha or Zachariah. The only Elijah entry is 20 Jun 1785, 7:453.
- Read from a 90 dpi rendering of the downloaded PDF; handwriting is not involved (typed). Some columns are narrow, so assignment of a given name to a column was read by eye.
