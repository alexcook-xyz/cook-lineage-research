## Citation
"Oath of Allegiance 1777," Pittsylvania County, Virginia, handwritten copy of the 1777-1778 oath lists on printed "Muster-Roll of ___ of Pittsylvania County, Virginia, in the War in Defense of Virginia, 1861-1865" sheets (Everett Waddey Co. form 68209), sheets stamped 275-293 (Internet Archive file leaves _0000 to _0018); copied by S. H. F. Jones about 1930, certified by her 8 Nov 1939 (sheet 292) and by E. E. Friend, Clerk of the Circuit Court, Chatham, 8 Nov 1939; digitized from a Library of Virginia microfilm (camera stamp on sheet 275: "Microfilmed, Virginia State Library, Archives Division, Camera Operator L. D. Francis, Reel No. 265 [reading uncertain], Date 7-17-23 [year digit uncertain]"); image set (000141000001_jp2.zip, 19 leaves), *Internet Archive*, item "1777oath" (https://archive.org/details/1777oath : accessed 28 Nov 2026; IA metadata names the creator "S.H. Jones", date 1930-11-08). Underlying originals: the 1777 manuscript lists then held in the Pittsylvania County Clerk's Office. The manuscripts were NOT seen; this is a copy of them (two removes at least).

## Notes
- Informant: unknown for the names; the oath lists were created by justices/captains in 1777-78. The copyist is S. H. F. Jones (called "Mrs." in the clerk's certificate). The copy was made about 1930, certified 1939.
- The hand is a neat uniform copy hand, not a clerk's 1777 hand; spelling is the copyist's rendering ("Cock" and "Cook" both appear; "Allegience").
- Read from 1800-pixel renderings of all 19 leaves, with full-resolution crops for the Cook, Musick, Halloway, and fragment rows. "Echols" readings were taken from the 1800-pixel rendering only (about 90% sure).
- Does not show where on Sandy Creek or in what district any man lived.
