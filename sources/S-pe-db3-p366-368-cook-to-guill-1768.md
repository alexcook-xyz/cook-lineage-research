## Transcription
Margin note p. 366: "Cook to Guill". p. 366: "This Indenture made the Eighteenth day of April one thousand seven hundred & Sixty Eight Between George Cook of Prince Edward county of the One part and Alexander Guill of the same county of the other part Witnesseth that the said George Cook for and In consideration of the sum of Thirteen pounds Current money of Virginia to him in hand paid or Secured to be paid by the said Alexander Guill ... one Certain Tract or parcel of land Situate lying and being on the Branches of Harris's Creek in Prince Edward County Containing by Estimation one hundred and fifty Acres be the same more or less and bounded as followeth (to wit) Begining at a [illegible] corner in [illegible] line Thence on his lines South Ten degrees West One hundred and eighty Poles to a corner Thence west Six degrees South One hundred and Twenty poles to a [illegible] corner Thence on Jones's line North Three Degrees West One hundred and Seventy Eight Poles to a [illegible] Corner Thence by a new Division line North Eighty Six degrees East One hundred and Sixty four poles to the Begining."
p. 367: warranty by "the said George Cook" for himself and his heirs; "In Witness whereof the said George Cook hath hereunto set his hand and seal the date above mentioned"; "George Cook (Seal)"; "Sealed and Delivered in presents of" followed by an empty brace (no witness named).
p. 368: "Memorandum that Livery and Seison of the lands and appurtenances within mentioned was given To the within named Alexander Guill by the within named George Cook this Eighteenth day of April one thousand seven hundred and Sixty Eight. Sealed and Delivered in presents of" (empty brace). "Prince Edward April Court 1768 The within deed from George Cooke to Alexander Guill was presented in Court and acknowledged by the said George Cooke party thereto, & ordered to be recorded Test T. Watkins D.C[?]". The next deed on p. 368 (Gilliam to Buchanan, 17 Sep 1769) is unrelated.
