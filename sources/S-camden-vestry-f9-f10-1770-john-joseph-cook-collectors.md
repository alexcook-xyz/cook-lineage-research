## Transcription
Folio 9 (vestry of 26 Jan 1770), verbatim: "John Cook is appointed Collector of the levy for 1768. in the Stead of Geo Rowland and Enters into bond with John Salmon and Joseph Cook his Securitys in the Sum of three hundred pounds Current money for his faithfull Collecting and paying the same"
Folio 10 (vestry of 29 Nov 1770, levy): "To ... Abram Shelton ... D. for 113 Insolvents returned by Jon Cook 1769 ... 4746"; "To Joseph Cook for 138. D. returned for 1768. @ 13 Tob. ... 1794"; credits "By Joseph Cook for Do ... 120"; "By John Cook Collector for the Deposcitum for 1768 ... 5000"; "By John Cook Collector for 1768 a fraction in his hands of ... 337" (figures read at 80 dpi, not exact).
Related: folio 3 (Nov 1768 levy) carries the marginal note "(Jn. Cook Collected this levy)"; folio 3 reading is about 85 percent sure.

## Notes
Image seen (color scan, read at 80 dpi, key lines re-read at 150 dpi). The scan is a manuscript volume (a vestry minute book, apparently a fair copy attested by the clerk: "A Copy Test" signature lines occur). Page order inside the IA file is not always folio order. Folio numbers are the clerk's parenthesised page numbers at the top of each leaf. Informant: the vestry clerk (Benjamin Lankford to 1779, then Vincent Shelton). Source_class "original" is provisional: the volume may be a clerk's fair copy of earlier minutes (analyst to decide).
