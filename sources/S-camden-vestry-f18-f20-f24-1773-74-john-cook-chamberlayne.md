## Transcription
Folio 18 (vestry 25 Mar 1773): "Ordered that John Cook former Collector of this Parish pay unto the said John Pigg attorney for Richard Chamberlayne after the said Piggs making a Good & Lawfull Deed for the said Land to the vestry the Sume of five thousand three hundred and thirty seven pounds of Tobacco ... in part of the Glebe Land it being supposed the Ballance in his hands as Parish Collector for the year 1768".
Folio 18 (vestry 28 May 1773): collectors Abram Shelton, John Salmon, Jesse Hind and Richard White "Entered into Bond with the Revd Lewis Gwillam Edmund Lyne William Thomas Danl Hankins John Cook and James Cowden? their securitys" ("Cowden" doubtful, name partly illegible at 80 dpi).
Folio 20 (Nov 1773): "Whereas at a vestry held ... the 26th day of November 1772 It was Ordered that John Cook Collector for 1768 should pay unto John Pigg Attorney for Richard Chamberlayne the Sum of 5337 of Tobacco ... Ordered that the said Jno Cook pay unto the said Shelton the Tobo aforesaid".
Folio 24 (Oct 1774): "It appearing to this Vestry that John Cook Collector for the Year 1768 has in his hands 5337 pounds of Tobacco which was Ordered to be paid to Abraham Shelton and whereas the said Cook hath not paid the Tobacco according to the Order Ordered that the Church Wardens bring a suit against the said Cook and his securitys for the said Tobacco".
This John Cook is the Camden Parish collector; no place of residence is stated.
