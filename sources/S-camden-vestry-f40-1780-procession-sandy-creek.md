## Transcription
Folio 36 records the vestry of 22 Mar 1780 (levy) and folio 37 continues with the processioning order ("Pursuant to an order of the Worshipfull Court of Pittsylvania County ..." with return date 16 May next).
Folio 40, verbatim (150 dpi): "Ordered That Jese Dodson John Kirby Mark Shelton Thomas Shelton Lazt Dodson & Charles Lewis do Procession all the patented land, Beginning where the County line Crosses Sandy Creek Thence along the said line to Burches Cr Thence up the said Creek to the Head, Thence a Cross to Harrises Road Thence Down the said road to Sandy Creek thence Down the said to the Beginning, and that they Return to the said Vestry by the 16th day of May Next an account of all the lands they Shall Procession in the said bounds, and the persons present at the Same & what land they fail to procession & the Reason of such failure" ("Lazt Dodson": reading of the given name uncertain, 60 percent; possibly "Lazarus").
Folio 41 (Sandy River precincts), not Cook-related: names Southerland, McDaniel, Burton, Aston, Quinn; Cunningham, Hampton, Morton, Oakes, Mark, Gresham, Burnett, Conn, Lanford, Mosley; Pennon?, Stokes, Hutchings, Pigg, Dillard, Smith, Fulton, Shields; Wilson, Harrison, Perkins, Owen, Adams. No Cook.
