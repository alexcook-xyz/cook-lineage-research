## Notes
Name spelled "Harmon Cook" here; the Pittsylvania DB 5 deeds in this repo spell "Harmon Cook". No George, Elijah, Elisha, Zachariah or other Cook on this page. This is the first page of a run of Cook entries; the claimant is the claimant himself, not an assignee. The sitting date is not on p. 28; the nearest earlier court header read is p. 1 ("At a Court of Claims held at Pittsylvania County, the 18th day of March 1782 for adjusting Claims for property impressed or taken for Public Service"); no later header was noted on pp. 1-44 but pp. 9-27 and 30-39 were scanned at 85 dpi without a line-by-line re-read, so a sitting between 18 Mar and 5 Oct 1782 is not excluded. Informant: the clerk recording the claimant's account. No residence or relationship is stated.
