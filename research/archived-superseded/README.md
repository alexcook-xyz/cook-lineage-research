# Archived & Superseded Research Drafts

> [!WARNING]
> The files in this folder represent preliminary, unverified, or superseded working drafts. They contain known factual and documentary errors that have been refuted by subsequent direct inspection of original county courthouse records.

---

## Known Errors Quarantined in These Drafts

1. **Chancery Cause 143-1840-024 Fabricated Will:** Early working drafts alleged that Pittsylvania Chancery cause *143-1840-024* referenced a `"17 Sep 1831"` will of George Cook. Direct inspection of original Library of Virginia scans proved the plaintiff George Cook died intestate in the county poorhouse c. 1835–1836, leaving Sally Daly as his sole heir.
2. **Deed Book 19:228 & Deed Book 18:68 Confusions:** Preliminary summaries confused dates, parties, and witnesses across multiple unrelated Cook conveyances.
3. **Eliza E. Cook Will Date & Heirs:** Superseded drafts gave an incorrect execution date of `"1 Oct 1890"` and omitted son John D. Cook. The original record (`Adair WB M:133–134`) proves the date was **8 Oct 1900** and explicitly names **Thomas H. Cook**, **John D. Cook**, and **George W. Cook**.
4. **Invented Numerical Books:** Adair County courthouse records do not use numerical identifiers like `DB 3` or `DB 4` for nineteenth-century records; Adair uses lettered series (`T`, `X`, `Z`, `M`).
