# [Cumberland County, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumberland_County,_Kentucky "Southern Kentucky county in the Pennyroyal Plateau formed in 1798 along the Cumberland River, with its county seat at Burkesville."): Jurisdictional & Archival Guide

- **Formation:** Created 1798 from Green County.
- **County Seat:** Burkesville, Kentucky.
- **Key Watercourse:** Cumberland River.

---

## 1. Relevance to the Cook Lineage

Cumberland County provided the crucial migratory transitional link connecting the Virginia family to Kentucky:
- On **2 November 1836**, brothers **George Cook** and **Henry Cook** executed a formal Power of Attorney in Cumberland County (recorded in Pittsylvania County Deed Book 39, page 242).
- The instrument empowered their elder brother **James Cook**, who had already settled in adjoining Adair County, to convey their inherited shares in their father's 277-acre Virginia estate.

## 2. Active Research Targets

- **Cumberland County Annual Tax Books (1833–1845):** Determining the exact years George and Henry Cook were enumerated as white males above age 21 to fix their dates of majority (tracked in Issue #3).
- **Cumberland County Deed Books (1830–1850):** Checking for any land holdings prior to George's relocation to Russell's Creek in Adair County.
